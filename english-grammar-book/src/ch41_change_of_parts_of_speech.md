## 41.1 What Is Meant by "Change of Parts of Speech"?

Most English words belong to **word families**: one root appears as a **noun**, a **verb**, an **adjective** and an **adverb** with different endings.

::: ex
- *beauty* (n) — *beautify* (v) — *beautiful* (adj) — *beautifully* (adv)
- *success* (n) — *succeed* (v) — *successful* (adj) — *successfully* (adv)
- *decision* (n) — *decide* (v) — *decisive* (adj) — *decisively* (adv)
:::

Questions on this topic are very common in BCS, Bank and admission tests: "*What is the noun form of …?*", "*Change the part of speech of the underlined word*", or "*Use the correct form of the word in brackets*" (*He was praised for his (honest)* → *honesty*). In GMAT and SAT, choosing the wrong word form (*economic* vs *economical*, *a dependence* vs *dependent*) is a common error.

::: tip
**How to choose the right form in a sentence:** (1) after *a/an/the/his/this* → **noun** (or adjective + noun); (2) after *to* or a modal → **verb**; (3) after *very / too / so / be / seem / become* → **adjective**; (4) describing a verb → **adverb**. Then use the tables below to find the correct spelling.
:::

## 41.3 Some Special Cases

### 41.3.1 One Word, Two Noun Forms

| Root | Noun 1 | Noun 2 |
|---|---|---|
| *succeed* | **success** (achievement) | **succession** (following in order) |
| *respect* | **respect** (honour) | **respectability** (being respectable) |
| *sense* | **sensibility** (sensitivity, feeling) | **sensitivity / sensation** |
| *judge* | **judgement** (opinion, verdict) | **judiciary** (system of courts) |
| *industry* | **industry** (manufacturing; diligence) | **industriousness** (diligence) |
| *economy* | **economy** (financial system; thrift) | **economics** (the subject) |
| *history* | **history** | **historian** (person) |
| *person* | **personality** (character) | **personnel** (staff) |

### 41.3.2 Adjective Pairs with Different Meanings

Both are formed from the same root, but they mean different things (see Chapter 09.10 and Chapter 52):

*economic / economical; historic / historical; industrial / industrious; respectable / respectful / respective; sensible / sensitive; childish / childlike; imaginary / imaginative; continual / continuous; credible / credulous / creditable; luxurious / luxuriant; momentary / momentous; official / officious; ingenious / ingenuous; human / humane; honorary / honourable; comprehensible / comprehensive; contemptible / contemptuous; judicial / judicious; politic / political.*

## 41.4 Using the Correct Form in a Sentence: Examples

::: errs
1. He was praised for his **(honest)**. → **honesty** (after *his* → noun)
2. She sang **(beautiful)**. → **beautifully** (modifies *sang* → adverb)
3. We should **(strong)** our economy. → **strengthen** (after *should* → verb)
4. The **(poor)** of the villagers is shocking. → **poverty**
5. He is a **(success)** businessman. → **successful** (before noun → adjective)
6. His **(arrive)** was delayed. → **arrival**
7. The book is very **(use)**. → **useful**
8. He gave a **(describe)** of the accident. → **description**
9. She has the **(able)** to solve it. → **ability**
10. I **(real)** my mistake at once. → **realised**
11. Please **(simple)** the explanation. → **simplify**
12. He spoke **(angry)**. → **angrily**
13. Dhaka is a **(crowd)** city. → **crowded**
14. The film was **(interest)**. → **interesting**
15. We must show **(respect)** to our elders. → **respect**
16. He is **(respect)** to his teachers. → **respectful**
17. He comes from a **(respect)** family. → **respectable**
18. They went to their **(respect)** homes. → **respective**
19. Smoking is **(injury)** to health. → **injurious**
20. Her **(proud)** was hurt. → **pride**
21. The **(long)** of the river is 200 km. → **length**
22. The road was **(wide)** last year. → **widened**
23. He acted **(wise)**. → **wisely**

25. Bangladesh became **(depend)** in 1971. → **independent**

## 41.5 Change of Parts of Speech in the SAT, GRE and GMAT

::: gre
- **Word-form errors (SAT/GMAT):** ✘ *The company's economic plan saved money.* — if the meaning is "thrifty", ✔ *economical*. ✘ *Her ability of solving* → ✔ *ability **to solve***. ✘ *The research showed a significant improve* → ✔ *improvement*.
- **Noun vs verb concision (GMAT):** GMAT favours **verbs** over long noun phrases: ✘ *made an improvement in* → ✔ *improved*; ✘ *the reduction of* → ✔ *reducing* (when parallel with verbs).
- **GRE vocabulary families:** learning a word with its family multiplies your vocabulary: *obdurate / obduracy, laconic / laconically, ephemeral / ephemerality, pragmatic / pragmatism, enervate / enervation, venerate / veneration / venerable, mitigate / mitigation / unmitigated*.
:::
