## 47.1 What Is a Phrasal (Group) Verb?

A **phrasal verb** (or **group verb**) is a **verb + one or two particles** (an adverb and/or a preposition) that together have a **single meaning**, often quite different from the meaning of the verb alone.

::: ex
- *give* = hand over; ***give up*** = stop doing / abandon (*He **gave up** smoking.*)
- *look* = see; ***look after*** = take care of (*She **looks after** her parents.*)
- *put* = place; ***put up with*** = tolerate (*I can't **put up with** his rudeness.*)
:::

Phrasal verbs are extremely common in everyday English. In Bangladeshi exams they are tested as "*Make sentences with the following group verbs*", "*Fill in the gap with the right particle*" (*The meeting was put ___ till Monday* → *off*) and as MCQs asking for the **meaning**. In the SAT and GRE they appear in reading passages, and GMAT sometimes prefers a **single precise verb** to a phrasal one in formal writing (*investigate* rather than *look into*).

### 47.1.1 Types of Phrasal Verbs

| Type | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Intransitive** | no object | *The car **broke down**. Prices **went up**.* |
| **Transitive, separable** | object can come between verb and particle; a **pronoun object must** come between them | *She **put on** her coat. / She **put** her coat **on**. / She **put it on**.* (✘ put on it) |
| **Transitive, inseparable** | object always after the particle | *She **looks after** her mother / **looks after** her.* (✘ looks her after) |
| **Three-word (phrasal-prepositional)** | verb + adverb + preposition; inseparable | *I can't **put up with** this noise. We **look forward to** meeting you.* |

### 47.1.2 Common Meanings of Particles

| Particle | Typical meanings | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **up** | increase; completion; approach; appear | *go up, speed up, eat up, use up, finish up, come up to, turn up, show up* |
| **down** | decrease; stop; write; defeat; suppress | *slow down, come down, break down, write down, put down, turn down, shut down* |
| **out** | completely; disappear; outward; discover | *sell out, die out, run out, find out, work out, carry out, give out, wipe out* |
| **off** | departure; removal; cancel; start | *set off, take off, call off, put off, switch off, see off, cut off* |
| **on** | continue; attach; start | *go on, carry on, keep on, put on, switch on, take on* |
| **over** | again; examine; from one to another | *think over, look over, hand over, take over, get over* |
| **away** | leave; continuously; disappear | *go away, run away, give away, pass away, fade away, work away* |
| **back** | return; reply; restrain | *come back, call back, hold back, pay back, turn back* |
| **in / into** | enter; submit; collapse | *break in, hand in, give in, fill in, look into, run into* |
| **through** | complete; experience | *go through, get through, see through, fall through* |

::: vocab

**Bear with** [ধৈর্য ধরা]{.bn} — to be patient with. *Ex:* Please **bear with** me for a moment.

**Bear down on / upon** [তীব্রভাবে এগিয়ে আসা; চাপ দেওয়া]{.bn} — to move towards threateningly; to press hard. *Ex:* The storm was **bearing down on** the coast.
