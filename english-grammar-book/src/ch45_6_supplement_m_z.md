## 45.5 Learning Vocabulary Efficiently

::: tip
**A five-step routine that works:** (1) **Learn 20–30 words a day** from these lists with their example sentences. (2) **Group them** by meaning or root (e.g., all words for *praise*: *laud, extol, eulogise, acclaim, commend, panegyric, encomium*; all words for *criticise*: *castigate, censure, berate, lambaste, upbraid, excoriate, vilify*). (3) **Use each word** in your own sentence. (4) **Revise** on days 1, 3, 7 and 30 (spaced repetition). (5) **Meet the words again** in reading — editorials and long-form articles — so that they move from your "recognition" vocabulary into your "active" vocabulary.
:::
