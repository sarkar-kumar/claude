## 45.4 Supplementary Word List: Advanced and High-Frequency Words

The master list above covers the words most often asked in Bangladeshi examinations. This supplementary list adds **further high-value words** — many of them frequent in the **GRE, SAT and GMAT**, in English newspaper editorials, and increasingly in **Bank (AD/Senior Officer)** and **IBA/DU admission** papers. The format is the same: **word**, *(part of speech)*, [Bangla meaning]{.bn}, English meaning, [SYN]{.lab}, [ANT]{.lab .ant} and an example.
