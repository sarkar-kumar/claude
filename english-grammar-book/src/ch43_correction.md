## 43.1 About "Correction" Questions

"**Correct the following sentences**" is one of the oldest and most common questions in Bangladeshi examinations — SSC, HSC, university admission tests, BCS Written and Preliminary, Bank written tests and other job tests. Unlike *Pin Point Error* (Chapter 35), where you only **identify** the faulty part, here you must **rewrite** the sentence correctly.

::: rule
**How to answer a correction question**

1. **Read the whole sentence** and find the intended **meaning**.
2. **Locate the error(s)** using the ten-point checklist (Chapter 35.2): agreement, tense, pronoun, verb form, preposition, article, adjective/adverb, noun number, conjunction/order, redundancy/modifier.
3. **Change only what is wrong.** Do not rewrite the whole sentence or change its meaning.
4. **Write the full corrected sentence** neatly; in written exams, **underline** the corrected part.
5. A sentence may contain **more than one error** — check it again after correcting.
:::

The rest of this chapter presents **typical exam sentences**, grouped by error type, with the correction and the reason. Many are taken from patterns that appear repeatedly in BCS, Bank and admission papers.

## 43.2 Nouns and Number

::: errs
1. ✘ The sceneries of Cox's Bazar are very charming. ✔ The **scenery** of Cox's Bazar **is** very charming. — *scenery* is uncountable.
2. ✘ He gave me many good advices. ✔ He gave me **a lot of good advice**. — *advice* is uncountable.
3. ✘ I have bought three dozens pencils. ✔ I have bought three **dozen** pencils. — no *-s* after a number.
4. ✘ The informations he gave were false. ✔ The **information** he gave **was** false.
5. ✘ He has two sister-in-laws. ✔ He has two **sisters-in-law**.
6. ✘ There are many furnitures in the room. ✔ There **is a lot of furniture** in the room.
7. ✘ The cattles are grazing in the field. ✔ The **cattle** are grazing in the field.
8. ✘ All his family members came. ✔ **All the members of his family** came.
9. ✘ She has long black hairs. ✔ She has long black **hair**.
10. ✘ My luggages have been stolen. ✔ My **luggage has** been stolen.
11. ✘ He is one of the greatest poet of Bangladesh. ✔ … one of the greatest **poets** …
12. ✘ He drew a ten-miles line. ✔ He drew a **ten-mile** line.
13. ✘ These datas are not reliable. ✔ These **data** are not reliable.
14. ✘ The police has caught the thieves. ✔ The police **have** caught the thieves.
15. ✘ He has many machineries. ✔ He has **a lot of machinery / many machines**.
16. ✘ The poors are honest. ✔ **The poor** are honest.
17. ✘ I have no more works to do. ✔ I have no more **work** to do.
18. ✘ Her jewelleries are costly. ✔ Her **jewellery is** costly.
:::

## 43.3 Pronouns

::: errs
1. ✘ It is me who am to blame. ✔ It is **I** who am to blame.
2. ✘ He and myself are friends. ✔ He and **I** are friends.
3. ✘ Every student must bring their own book. ✔ Every student must bring **his / his or her** own book.
4. ✘ One should do his duty. ✔ One should do **one's** duty.
5. ✘ Between you and I, he is not honest. ✔ Between you and **me**, …
6. ✘ Whom do you think is the best player? ✔ **Who** do you think is the best player?
7. ✘ This is the boy whom I think stole the pen. ✔ This is the boy **who** I think stole the pen.
8. ✘ He enjoyed during the holiday. ✔ He **enjoyed himself** during the holiday.
9. ✘ He availed of the opportunity. ✔ He **availed himself of** the opportunity.
10. ✘ I and Rahim went to the cinema. ✔ **Rahim and I** went to the cinema.
11. ✘ He, you and I are to blame. ✔ **I, you and he** are to blame.
12. ✘ All what you said is false. ✔ **All that** you said is false.
13. ✘ He is the only one who can help us, isn't it? ✔ … **isn't he?**
14. ✘ Each of the girls have their own room. ✔ Each of the girls **has her** own room.
15. ✘ Is this pen your's? ✔ Is this pen **yours**?
16. ✘ Who's book is this? ✔ **Whose** book is this?
17. ✘ None of them were present. (traditional) ✔ None of them **was** present.
18. ✘ My teacher, that teaches English, is kind. ✔ My teacher, **who** teaches English, is kind.
:::

## 43.5 Adjectives, Adverbs and Comparison

::: errs
1. ✘ He is more wiser than his brother. ✔ He is **wiser** than his brother.
2. ✘ Rahim is the tallest of the two brothers. ✔ Rahim is the **taller** of the two brothers.
3. ✘ He is senior than me. ✔ He is senior **to** me.
4. ✘ She is elder than her husband. ✔ She is **older** than her husband.
5. ✘ He is better than any boy in the class. ✔ … than any **other** boy in the class.
6. ✘ The weather of Sylhet is cooler than Dhaka. ✔ … cooler than **that of** Dhaka.
7. ✘ This is the most unique book. ✔ This is a **unique** book.
8. ✘ He is very stronger than me. ✔ He is **much** stronger than I.
9. ✘ The rose smells sweetly. ✔ The rose smells **sweet**.
10. ✘ She looks beautifully. ✔ She looks **beautiful**.
11. ✘ He speaks English very good. ✔ He speaks English very **well**.
12. ✘ He is enough clever to understand. ✔ He is **clever enough** to understand.
13. ✘ I am too glad to see you. ✔ I am **very** glad to see you.
14. ✘ He reads hardly to pass. ✔ He reads **hard** to pass.
15. ✘ I am very interesting in music. ✔ I am very **interested** in music.
16. ✘ The film was very bored. ✔ The film was very **boring**.
17. ✘ He has less friends than I. ✔ He has **fewer** friends than I.
18. ✘ This is a ten years old building. ✔ This is a **ten-year-old** building.
19. ✘ He is so weak to walk. ✔ He is **too** weak to walk.
20. ✘ He always is late. ✔ He is **always** late.
:::

::: note
Item 8: *very much* + comparative is acceptable (*very much stronger*), but *very* + comparative alone is not. Correction questions sometimes include such "trick" sentences; check the rule before changing anything.
:::

## 43.10 Usage, Vocabulary and "Banglish"

These sentences reflect literal translation from Bangla or local usage.

::: errs
1. ✘ He is my cousin brother. ✔ He is my **cousin**.
2. ✘ I am having a car. ✔ I **have** a car.
3. ✘ Please do the needful. ✔ Please **do what is necessary**.
4. ✘ He told me to come at evening. ✔ He told me to come **in the evening**.
5. ✘ My all friends are good. ✔ **All my** friends are good.
6. ✘ I am going to office. ✔ I am going to **the** office / **to work**.

14. ✘ He said me a story. ✔ He **told** me a story.
15. ✘ Open the light. ✔ **Switch / Turn on** the light.
16. ✘ Close the fan. ✔ **Switch / Turn off** the fan.
17. ✘ He is a good cricketer and he plays good. ✔ … and he plays **well**.
18. ✘ What is your good name? ✔ What is your **name**? / May I know your name?
19. ✘ I shall come within Monday. ✔ I shall come **by** Monday.
20. ✘ He is staying in the hostel since 2023. ✔ He **has been staying** in the hostel since 2023.
21. ✘ I am in tension. ✔ I am **worried / tense**.
22. ✘ The light is cut off due to load shedding. ✔ **There is a power cut** due to load-shedding.
23. ✘ He is a very much honest man. ✔ He is a **very** honest man.
24. ✘ Let us discuss over the matter. ✔ Let us **discuss** the matter.

:::

## 43.11 Frequently Repeated Exam Sentences

The following sentences (or close variants) have appeared again and again in BCS, Bank and university admission papers.

::: errs
1. ✘ He has no less than fifty books. ✔ He has **no fewer than** fifty books. (*fewer* with countable nouns in formal English)
2. ✘ He is a man of less words. ✔ He is a man of **few** words. (idiom)
3. ✘ The man is too old that he cannot walk. ✔ The man is **so** old that he cannot walk.
4. ✘ Each of the students are present. ✔ Each of the students **is** present.
5. ✘ I am waiting for you since morning. ✔ I **have been waiting** for you since morning.
6. ✘ He is more cleverer than his brother. ✔ He is **cleverer** than his brother.
7. ✘ I shall be glad if you will help me. ✔ I shall be glad if you **help** me / **would** help me.
8. ✘ He is one of the best boy in the class. ✔ … best **boys** …
9. ✘ My father is a teacher of a high school. ✔ My father **teaches at** a high school / is a **high-school teacher**.
10. ✘ Give me a few milk. ✔ Give me **a little** milk.
11. ✘ He gave me a five-takas note. ✔ … a **five-taka** note.
12. ✘ Ten miles are a long distance. ✔ … **is** a long distance.
13. ✘ I have read the book which you gave me it. ✔ I have read the book which you gave me.
14. ✘ The boy is suffering from fever for a week. ✔ … **has been suffering** from fever for a week.
15. ✘ The beggar was laughed by the boys. ✔ … was laughed **at** by the boys.
16. ✘ It is I who is wrong. ✔ It is I who **am** wrong.
17. ✘ Many a students have failed. ✔ Many a **student has** failed.
18. ✘ The Prime Minister and the President is coming. ✔ … **are** coming.
19. ✘ If I were you, I will not go. ✔ If I were you, I **would** not go.
20. ✘ The Padma bridge was opened at 25 June 2022. ✔ The **Padma Bridge** was opened **on** 25 June 2022.
:::

::: note
Exam papers sometimes include sentences that are already correct to test your confidence. **Do not change a correct sentence**; change only what breaks a rule, and keep the original meaning.
:::

## 43.12 Correction in the SAT, GRE and GMAT

::: gre
- **GMAT Sentence Correction** is "correction" in multiple-choice form: one underlined portion and five versions. Use the same checklist, but remember GMAT's priorities: **(1) grammar and idiom, (2) logic and meaning, (3) concision and clarity**.
- **SAT Writing & Language** asks you to choose the best revision — very similar to correction questions, but including **punctuation**, **transitions** and **concision**.
- A common strategy for both: **identify the error first**, then eliminate every choice that keeps it; compare the remaining choices for **new errors**.
:::
