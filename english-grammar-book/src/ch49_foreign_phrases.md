## 49.1 Foreign Words in English

English has borrowed words from almost every language — **Latin** and **Greek** (law, science, learning), **French** (government, cooking, fashion, diplomacy), **Italian** (music, art), **German**, **Spanish**, **Arabic**, **Persian** and **Indian languages**. Many foreign expressions are still used **in their original form**: *ad hoc, bona fide, coup d'état, status quo, vis-à-vis*. These are tested regularly in BCS, Bank and admission tests ("*The meaning of 'sine die' is …*", "*'Fait accompli' is a … phrase*") and appear in newspaper editorials, legal documents and GRE/SAT reading passages.

::: tip
**How to write foreign phrases:** In formal writing, foreign phrases not fully absorbed into English are written in *italics* (or underlined in handwriting): *The meeting was adjourned **sine die**.* Fully naturalised words (*alibi, agenda, cafe, ballet*) are not italicised. Keep the accents in French words where possible: *café, fiancé, déjà vu, coup d'état*.
:::

## 49.2 Latin Phrases

::: vocab
**A fortiori** [আরও জোরালো কারণে]{.bn} — with even stronger reason. *Ex:* If a child can do it, a fortiori an adult can.

**A posteriori** [অভিজ্ঞতালব্ধ; ফল থেকে কারণে]{.bn} — reasoning from experience or effects to causes. *Ex:* Scientific laws are established a posteriori.

**A priori** [★]{.star} [পূর্বসিদ্ধ; কারণ থেকে ফলে]{.bn} — reasoning from principles, before experience. *Ex:* He assumed a priori that the plan would fail.

**Ab initio** [শুরু থেকে]{.bn} — from the beginning. *Ex:* The contract was void ab initio.

**Ad hoc** [★]{.star} [বিশেষ উদ্দেশ্যে, অস্থায়ী]{.bn} — for a particular purpose only. *Ex:* An ad hoc committee was formed to investigate the fire.

**Ad infinitum** [★]{.star} [অনন্তকাল ধরে, অসীমভাবে]{.bn} — endlessly, for ever. *Ex:* The debate could go on ad infinitum.

**Ad interim** [অন্তর্বর্তীকালীন]{.bn} — for the meantime, temporarily. *Ex:* He was appointed chairman ad interim.

**Ad libitum (ad lib)** [ইচ্ছামতো; প্রস্তুতি ছাড়া]{.bn} — at one's pleasure; spontaneously. *Ex:* The actor spoke ad lib when he forgot his lines.

**Ad nauseam** [বিরক্তিকর মাত্রায়]{.bn} — to a disgusting or boring extent. *Ex:* He repeated the same story ad nauseam.

**Ad valorem** [মূল্য অনুযায়ী]{.bn} — in proportion to the value (tax). *Ex:* An ad valorem tax is charged on imported cars.

**Addendum (pl. addenda)** [সংযোজনী]{.bn} — something added, a supplement. *Ex:* An addendum was attached to the report.

**Affidavit** [হলফনামা]{.bn} — a written statement confirmed by oath. *Ex:* He submitted an affidavit to the court.

**Agenda** [আলোচ্যসূচি]{.bn} — a list of items to be discussed. *Ex:* The first item on the agenda is the budget.

**Alibi** [★]{.star} [অন্যত্র উপস্থিতির দাবি]{.bn} — a plea of having been elsewhere when a crime happened. *Ex:* The accused had a strong alibi.

**Alma mater** [★]{.star} [নিজের পুরোনো শিক্ষাপ্রতিষ্ঠান]{.bn} — the school or university one attended. *Ex:* He donated money to his alma mater, Dhaka University.

**Alter ego** [★]{.star} [অভিন্নহৃদয় বন্ধু; দ্বিতীয় সত্তা]{.bn} — a second self; a very close friend. *Ex:* Rahim is my alter ego.

**Alumnus (pl. alumni)** [প্রাক্তন ছাত্র]{.bn} — a former student. *Ex:* The alumni of the college met last week.

**Anno Domini (AD)** [খ্রিষ্টাব্দ]{.bn} — in the year of the Lord. *Ex:* The Taj Mahal was completed in 1653 AD.

**Ante meridiem (a.m.)** [দুপুরের আগে]{.bn} — before noon. *Ex:* The office opens at 9 a.m.

**Bona fide** [★]{.star} [সরল বিশ্বাসে; প্রকৃত, খাঁটি]{.bn} — genuine; in good faith. *Ex:* Only bona fide students may use the library.

**Carpe diem** [বর্তমানকে উপভোগ করো]{.bn} — seize the day. *Ex:* Carpe diem — don't waste your youth.

**Caveat** [সতর্কবাণী]{.bn} — a warning. *Ex:* There is a caveat: the offer ends tomorrow.

**Caveat emptor** [ক্রেতা সাবধান]{.bn} — let the buyer beware. *Ex:* In second-hand markets, caveat emptor applies.

**Circa (c.)** [প্রায়, আনুমানিক]{.bn} — approximately (with dates). *Ex:* The temple was built circa 800 AD.

**Compos mentis** [সুস্থ মস্তিষ্ক]{.bn} — of sound mind. *Ex:* The court found him compos mentis.

**Corrigendum (pl. corrigenda)** [সংশোধনী]{.bn} — a correction of an error in print. *Ex:* A corrigendum was published the next day.

**Curriculum vitae (CV)** [জীবনবৃত্তান্ত]{.bn} — a summary of one's education and career. *Ex:* Send your curriculum vitae with the application.

**De facto** [★]{.star} [কার্যত, বাস্তবে]{.bn} — in fact, in reality (whether legal or not). *Ex:* He is the de facto leader of the party.

**De jure** [★]{.star} [আইনত]{.bn} — by right, according to law. *Ex:* The king was the de jure ruler, but the general held de facto power.

**De novo** [নতুন করে]{.bn} — anew, from the beginning. *Ex:* The case will be heard de novo.

**Deus ex machina** [অলৌকিক সমাধান]{.bn} — an unexpected, contrived solution to a difficulty. *Ex:* The sudden inheritance was a deus ex machina in the novel.

**Erratum (pl. errata)** [মুদ্রণপ্রমাদ]{.bn} — an error in printing. *Ex:* A list of errata is given at the end of the book.

**Et cetera (etc.)** [ইত্যাদি]{.bn} — and so on. *Ex:* We bought rice, lentils, oil, etc.

**Ex gratia** [অনুগ্রহপূর্বক (বাধ্যবাধকতা ছাড়া)]{.bn} — as a favour, not legally required. *Ex:* The company made an ex gratia payment to the victims.

**Ex officio** [★]{.star} [পদাধিকারবলে]{.bn} — by virtue of one's office. *Ex:* The Vice-Chancellor is the ex officio chairman of the Syndicate.

**Ex parte** [একতরফা]{.bn} — on behalf of one side only (law). *Ex:* The court passed an ex parte order.

**Exempli gratia (e.g.)** [যেমন, উদাহরণস্বরূপ]{.bn} — for example. *Ex:* Citrus fruits, e.g. lemons and oranges, are rich in vitamin C.

**Habeas corpus** [বন্দি প্রদর্শনের রিট]{.bn} — a writ requiring a detained person to be brought before a court. *Ex:* His lawyer filed a writ of habeas corpus.

**Id est (i.e.)** [অর্থাৎ]{.bn} — that is. *Ex:* He is a polyglot, i.e. he speaks many languages.

**In absentia** [অনুপস্থিতিতে]{.bn} — in one's absence. *Ex:* He was sentenced in absentia.

**In camera** [★]{.star} [রুদ্ধদ্বার কক্ষে, গোপনে]{.bn} — in private (of court proceedings). *Ex:* The trial was held in camera.

**In memoriam** [স্মরণে]{.bn} — in memory of. *Ex:* A meeting was held in memoriam of the martyrs.

**In situ** [যথাস্থানে]{.bn} — in its original place. *Ex:* The statue was preserved in situ.

**In toto** [সম্পূর্ণভাবে]{.bn} — completely. *Ex:* The proposal was rejected in toto.

**Inter alia** [অন্যান্যের মধ্যে]{.bn} — among other things. *Ex:* The report discussed, inter alia, poverty and education.

**Ipso facto** [★]{.star} [সেই কারণেই, স্বতঃসিদ্ধভাবে]{.bn} — by that very fact. *Ex:* A convicted criminal is ipso facto disqualified from the election.

**Lingua franca** [সংযোগ ভাষা]{.bn} — a common language between speakers of different languages. *Ex:* English is the lingua franca of international business.

**Magna Carta** [মহাসনদ]{.bn} — the Great Charter (England, 1215); any fundamental charter of rights. *Ex:* The 1972 Constitution is the Magna Carta of Bangladesh.

**Magnum opus** [★]{.star} [শ্রেষ্ঠ রচনা]{.bn} — a great work; the masterpiece of an artist. *Ex:* *Gitanjali* is considered Tagore's magnum opus.

**Mala fide** [অসৎ উদ্দেশ্যে]{.bn} — in bad faith (opposite of *bona fide*). *Ex:* The order was passed mala fide.

**Memorandum (pl. memoranda)** [স্মারকলিপি]{.bn} — a written note or proposal. *Ex:* The union submitted a memorandum to the minister.

**Modus operandi** [★]{.star} [কার্যপ্রণালী]{.bn} — method of working. *Ex:* The police studied the gang's modus operandi.

**Modus vivendi** [সহাবস্থানের উপায়]{.bn} — a way of living together in spite of differences. *Ex:* The two parties reached a modus vivendi.

**Mutatis mutandis** [প্রয়োজনীয় পরিবর্তনসহ]{.bn} — with the necessary changes. *Ex:* The same rules apply, mutatis mutandis, to part-time staff.

**Nota bene (N.B.)** [বিশেষ দ্রষ্টব্য]{.bn} — note well. *Ex:* N.B. Late applications will not be accepted.

**Pari passu** [সমান তালে]{.bn} — at an equal rate, side by side. *Ex:* Wages should rise pari passu with prices.

**Per annum** [★]{.star} [বার্ষিক, প্রতি বছর]{.bn} — per year. *Ex:* His salary is ten lakh taka per annum.

**Per capita** [★]{.star} [মাথাপিছু]{.bn} — per head. *Ex:* The per capita income of Bangladesh has increased.

**Per diem** [দৈনিক]{.bn} — per day; a daily allowance. *Ex:* He gets a per diem of 2,000 taka.

**Per se** [★]{.star} [স্বয়ং, নিজ গুণে]{.bn} — by itself, intrinsically. *Ex:* Money per se is not evil.

**Persona non grata** [★]{.star} [অবাঞ্ছিত ব্যক্তি]{.bn} — an unwelcome person (esp. a diplomat). *Ex:* The diplomat was declared persona non grata.

**Post meridiem (p.m.)** [দুপুরের পরে]{.bn} — after noon. *Ex:* The shop closes at 8 p.m.

**Post mortem** [★]{.star} [ময়নাতদন্ত]{.bn} — examination of a dead body; analysis after an event. *Ex:* The body was sent for post mortem. / The team held a post-mortem of the defeat.

**Postscript (P.S.)** [পুনশ্চ]{.bn} — something added at the end of a letter. *Ex:* P.S. Don't forget to call me.

**Prima facie** [★]{.star} [আপাতদৃষ্টিতে]{.bn} — at first sight; based on first impression. *Ex:* There is a prima facie case against him.

**Pro bono (publico)** [জনস্বার্থে, বিনা পারিশ্রমিকে]{.bn} — for the public good; without charge. *Ex:* The lawyer took the case pro bono.

**Pro forma** [নিয়মরক্ষার্থে]{.bn} — as a matter of form. *Ex:* A pro forma invoice was sent.

**Pro rata** [আনুপাতিক হারে]{.bn} — in proportion. *Ex:* Profits will be shared pro rata.

**Pro tem (pro tempore)** [সাময়িকভাবে]{.bn} — for the time being. *Ex:* He is acting as secretary pro tem.

**Quid pro quo** [★]{.star} [কিছুর বিনিময়ে কিছু]{.bn} — something given in return for something. *Ex:* The deal was a clear quid pro quo.

**Quorum** [কোরাম, ন্যূনতম উপস্থিতি]{.bn} — the minimum number of members needed for a valid meeting. *Ex:* The meeting was postponed for lack of quorum.

**Sic** [যেমন আছে তেমন]{.bn} — thus (used to show an error is in the original). *Ex:* "He recieved [sic] the letter."

**Sine die** [★]{.star} [অনির্দিষ্টকালের জন্য]{.bn} — without fixing a date. *Ex:* The session was adjourned sine die.

**Sine qua non** [★]{.star} [অপরিহার্য শর্ত]{.bn} — an essential condition. *Ex:* Hard work is a sine qua non of success.

**Status quo** [★]{.star} [পূর্বাবস্থা, যথাপূর্ব অবস্থা]{.bn} — the existing state of affairs. *Ex:* The court ordered both parties to maintain the status quo.

**Sub judice** [★]{.star} [বিচারাধীন]{.bn} — under judicial consideration. *Ex:* We cannot comment; the matter is sub judice.

**Suo motu** [স্বতঃপ্রণোদিত হয়ে]{.bn} — on its own motion (of a court). *Ex:* The High Court issued a rule suo motu.

**Terra firma** [শুকনো মাটি]{.bn} — dry land. *Ex:* After the voyage, we were glad to be on terra firma.

**Terra incognita** [অজানা দেশ/বিষয়]{.bn} — unknown territory. *Ex:* Quantum physics is terra incognita to me.

**Ultra vires** [ক্ষমতা-বহির্ভূত]{.bn} — beyond one's legal power. *Ex:* The order was ultra vires and therefore void.

**Verbatim** [★]{.star} [হুবহু, শব্দে শব্দে]{.bn} — word for word. *Ex:* He reported the speech verbatim.

**Versus (v., vs.)** [বনাম]{.bn} — against. *Ex:* Bangladesh versus India.

**Via** [মাধ্যমে, হয়ে]{.bn} — by way of. *Ex:* We flew to London via Dubai.

**Vice versa** [★]{.star} [বিপরীতক্রমে]{.bn} — the other way round. *Ex:* He helps me and vice versa.

**Viva voce** [★]{.star} [মৌখিক (পরীক্ষা)]{.bn} — by word of mouth; an oral examination. *Ex:* The viva voce will be held next week.

**Vox populi** [জনগণের কণ্ঠ]{.bn} — the voice of the people. *Ex:* Vox populi, vox Dei (the voice of the people is the voice of God).
:::

**Chargé d'affaires** [ভারপ্রাপ্ত রাষ্ট্রদূত]{.bn} — a diplomat in charge in the absence of an ambassador.

**Cliché** [গতানুগতিক/জীর্ণ কথা]{.bn} — an overused expression. *Ex:* "As old as the hills" is a cliché.

**Crème de la crème** [সেরাদের সেরা]{.bn} — the very best. *Ex:* Only the crème de la crème are admitted to that school.

**Cul-de-sac** [কানাগলি]{.bn} — a dead-end street. *Ex:* They live in a quiet cul-de-sac.

**Debut** [আত্মপ্রকাশ]{.bn} — first appearance. *Ex:* He made his Test debut in 2015.

**Déjà vu** [আগে দেখা মনে হওয়ার অনুভূতি]{.bn} — the feeling of having experienced something before. *Ex:* I had a strange sense of déjà vu.

**Détente** [উত্তেজনা প্রশমন]{.bn} — easing of hostility between countries. *Ex:* The détente between the superpowers.

**Elite** [অভিজাত শ্রেণি]{.bn} — the best or most powerful group. *Ex:* the ruling elite.

**En bloc** [একযোগে]{.bn} — all together. *Ex:* The members resigned en bloc.

**En masse** [★]{.star} [দলবদ্ধভাবে]{.bn} — in a body, all together. *Ex:* The workers resigned en masse.

**En route** [★]{.star} [পথিমধ্যে]{.bn} — on the way. *Ex:* We stopped at Cumilla en route to Chattogram.

**Entente** [মৈত্রী, সমঝোতা]{.bn} — a friendly understanding between countries.

**Entrepreneur** [উদ্যোক্তা]{.bn} — a person who starts a business. *Ex:* Young entrepreneurs are creating jobs.

**Esprit de corps** [দলীয় চেতনা, সংহতি]{.bn} — team spirit. *Ex:* The army has a strong esprit de corps.

**Fait accompli** [★]{.star} [সম্পন্ন ঘটনা যা আর বদলানো যায় না]{.bn} — something already done and irreversible. *Ex:* By the time we objected, the merger was a fait accompli.

**Faux pas** [★]{.star} [সামাজিক ভুল/অশোভন আচরণ]{.bn} — a social blunder. *Ex:* Asking her age was a faux pas.

**Genre** [ধরন, শ্রেণি]{.bn} — a category of art or literature. *Ex:* Detective fiction is a popular genre.

**Laissez-faire** [★]{.star} [অবাধ নীতি; হস্তক্ষেপহীন]{.bn} — a policy of non-interference (esp. by government in the economy). *Ex:* He believes in laissez-faire economics.

::: note
***e.g.* vs *i.e.*:** *e.g.* introduces **examples** (not a complete list): *fruits, e.g. mangoes and bananas*. *i.e.* introduces an **explanation or equivalent** (*that is*): *He is a polyglot, i.e. he speaks many languages.* Do not confuse them.
:::

## 49.6 Foreign Phrases in the SAT, GRE and GMAT

::: gre
GRE and SAT reading passages (especially on law, politics, philosophy and the arts) freely use *a priori, ad hoc, de facto, per se, status quo, quid pro quo, sine qua non, laissez-faire, raison d'être, fait accompli, bête noire, avant-garde, zeitgeist, hubris, nemesis, panacea*. Several of these are also GRE vocabulary items in their own right: **hubris**, **panacea**, **nemesis**, **dogma(tic)**, **cliché**, **naïve**, **blasé**, **connoisseur**, **parvenu**, **aficionado**, **virtuoso**, **juggernaut**. GMAT style favours plain English over foreign phrases, but *per capita* and *pro rata* are common in data-based questions.
:::
