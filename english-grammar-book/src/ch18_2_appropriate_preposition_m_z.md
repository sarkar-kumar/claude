## 18.5 Common Prepositional Phrases

These phrases begin with a preposition and function as adverbs or adjectives. They appear often in fill-in-the-blank questions where the preposition is removed.

### 18.5.1 With *At*

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| at a loss | puzzled | *I was **at a loss** what to do.* |
| at all costs | whatever happens | *We must win **at all costs**.* |
| at any rate | in any case | ***At any rate**, we tried.* |
| at ease | relaxed | *She felt **at ease** in the new school.* |
| at fault | to blame | *The driver was **at fault**.* |
| at first sight | at the first look | *love **at first sight*** |
| at hand | near; soon | *The exam is **at hand**.* |
| at large | free (criminal); in general | *The murderer is still **at large**. the public **at large*** |
| at last | finally | ***At last** he came.* |
| at least | not less than | *It will cost **at least** 500 taka.* |
| at length | in detail; finally | *He spoke **at length**.* |
| at one's wits' end | completely puzzled | *I am **at my wits' end**.* |
| at random | without plan | *He picked a card **at random**.* |
| at stake | at risk | *Our honour is **at stake**.* |
| at the cost of | sacrificing | *He worked **at the cost of** his health.* |
| at the eleventh hour | at the last moment | *He came **at the eleventh hour**.* |
| at the mercy of | in the power of | *The boat was **at the mercy of** the waves.* |
| at times | sometimes | ***At times** I feel lonely.* |
| at variance | in disagreement | *His words are **at variance** with his deeds.* |

### 18.5.2 With *By*

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| by accident / by chance | accidentally | *I met him **by chance**.* |
| by all means | certainly | *"May I come?" — "**By all means**."* |
| by and by | gradually, soon | ***By and by** he recovered.* |
| by and large | on the whole | ***By and large**, the plan worked.* |
| by dint of | by means of | *He succeeded **by dint of** hard work.* |
| by fair means or foul | by any method | *He wants to win **by fair means or foul**.* |
| by heart | from memory | *Learn the poem **by heart**.* |
| by hook or by crook | by any means | *He wants money **by hook or by crook**.* |
| by leaps and bounds | very rapidly | *Our economy is growing **by leaps and bounds**.* |
| by mistake | in error | *I took his umbrella **by mistake**.* |
| by no means | not at all | *He is **by no means** a fool.* |
| by the way | incidentally | ***By the way**, have you seen Rahim?* |
| by virtue of | because of | ***By virtue of** his position, he has power.* |
| by way of | as a form of; via | *He said it **by way of** a joke.* |

### 18.5.3 With *In*

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| in a nutshell | briefly | ***In a nutshell**, we failed.* |
| in accordance with | in agreement with | ***In accordance with** the law …* |
| in brief / in short | briefly | ***In short**, he is a liar.* |
| in charge of | responsible for | *He is **in charge of** the office.* |
| in common | shared | *We have nothing **in common**.* |
| in consequence of | as a result of | ***In consequence of** the flood, crops were destroyed.* |
| in debt | owing money | *He is deeply **in debt**.* |
| in detail | fully | *Explain it **in detail**.* |
| in fact | actually | ***In fact**, he is very poor.* |
| in favour of | supporting | *I am **in favour of** the plan.* |
| in full swing | at its peak | *The party was **in full swing**.* |
| in lieu of | instead of | *He got money **in lieu of** leave.* |
| in no time | very quickly | *He finished it **in no time**.* |
| in order | arranged; allowed | *Everything is **in order**.* |
| in person | personally | *Please come **in person**.* |
| in spite of | despite | ***In spite of** the rain, we went out.* |
| in the long run | eventually | *Honesty pays **in the long run**.* |
| in the nick of time | just in time | *He arrived **in the nick of time**.* |
| in vain | uselessly | *All our efforts were **in vain**.* |
| in view of | considering | ***In view of** the situation, the exam was postponed.* |

### 18.5.4 With *On*, *Out of*, *Under*, *Of*, *Without*, *With*

| Phrase | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| on account of | because of | *He was absent **on account of** illness.* |
| on behalf of | representing | *I thank you **on behalf of** the school.* |
| on duty / off duty | working / not working | *The guard is **on duty**.* |
| on no account | never | ***On no account** should you go.* |
| on purpose | intentionally | *He broke it **on purpose**.* |
| on the contrary | the opposite is true | *He is not lazy; **on the contrary**, he is very active.* |
| on the eve of | just before | ***on the eve of** the election* |
| on the whole | generally | ***On the whole**, it was a success.* |
| out of date | old-fashioned | *This dictionary is **out of date**.* |
| out of order | not working | *The lift is **out of order**.* |
| out of question / out of the question | impossible | *A holiday is **out of the question**.* |
| out of sorts | unwell, upset | *He is **out of sorts** today.* |
| out of the blue | unexpectedly | *He arrived **out of the blue**.* |
| under consideration | being considered | *The matter is **under consideration**.* |
| under no circumstances | never | ***Under no circumstances** will I agree.* |
| under pressure | stressed, forced | *He works well **under pressure**.* |
| of late | recently | *I haven't seen him **of late**.* |
| of one's own accord | voluntarily | *He came **of his own accord**.* |
| without fail | certainly | *Come tomorrow **without fail**.* |
| with a view to | with the intention of | *He went abroad **with a view to** studying medicine.* |
| with regard to / with reference to | concerning | ***With reference to** your letter, …* |
| for good | permanently | *He left Bangladesh **for good**.* |
| for the sake of | for the benefit of | *He did it **for the sake of** his children.* |

## 18.6 Common Errors with Appropriate Prepositions

::: errs
1. ✘ He is fond for music. ✔ He is fond **of** music.
2. ✘ She is married with an engineer. ✔ She is married **to** an engineer.
3. ✘ I congratulate you for your success. ✔ I congratulate you **on** your success.
4. ✘ He was accused for theft. ✔ He was accused **of** theft.
5. ✘ He is independent from his parents. ✔ He is independent **of** his parents.
6. ✘ He died for cholera. ✔ He died **of** cholera.
7. ✘ He is good in English. ✔ He is good **at** English.
8. ✘ I am angry on you. ✔ I am angry **with** you.
9. ✘ He insisted to go. ✔ He insisted **on going**.
10. ✘ He prevented me to go. ✔ He prevented me **from going**.
11. ✘ She is interested on music. ✔ She is interested **in** music.
12. ✘ He is capable to do it. ✔ He is capable **of doing** it.
13. ✘ I have no objection in your going. ✔ I have no objection **to** your going.
14. ✘ The book is divided in five chapters. ✔ … divided **into** five chapters.
15. ✘ He was sentenced for death. ✔ He was sentenced **to** death.
16. ✘ Smoking is injurious for health. ✔ Smoking is injurious **to** health.
17. ✘ I am confident about success. (acceptable) ✔ I am confident **of** success. (exam answer)
18. ✘ He was deprived from his rights. ✔ He was deprived **of** his rights.
19. ✘ She is jealous on her sister. ✔ She is jealous **of** her sister.
20. ✘ He complained against the noise. ✔ He complained **about / of** the noise.
21. ✘ He is suffering with fever. ✔ He is suffering **from** fever.
22. ✘ He is senior than me. ✔ He is senior **to** me.
23. ✘ Translate this in English. ✔ Translate this **into** English.
24. ✘ We should abide with the law. ✔ We should abide **by** the law.
25. ✘ This custom is peculiar with our country. ✔ … peculiar **to** our country.
26. ✘ I am indebted for him. ✔ I am indebted **to** him.
27. ✘ He is addicted with smoking. ✔ He is addicted **to** smoking.
28. ✘ He was absent in the class. ✔ He was absent **from** the class.
29. ✘ He takes pride on his work. ✔ He takes pride **in** his work. / He prides himself **on** his work.
30. ✘ He is proficient at English. ✔ He is proficient **in** English.
:::

## 18.7 Idiomatic Prepositions in the GMAT

::: gre
GMAT Sentence Correction lists dozens of "preposition idioms". The most frequently tested:

| ✔ Correct idiom | Typical wrong version |
|---|---|
| *attribute X **to** Y* | attribute X *as* / *for* Y |
| *based **on*** | based *upon* (acceptable) / based *in* (✘) |
| *believe X **to be** Y* | believe X *as* Y |
| *compared **with** / **to*** | compared *against* |
| *consistent **with*** | consistent *to* |
| *contrast X **with** Y* | contrast X *to* Y (less preferred) |
| *depend **on** / **upon*** | depend *of* |
| *differ **from*** | differ *than* |
| *distinguish X **from** Y / **between** X **and** Y* | distinguish *between* X *from* Y |
| *estimated **to be*** | estimated *at being* |
| *the same **as*** | the same *to* |
| *native **to*** (plant, animal) | native *of* (for people: *a native of Sylhet* ✔) |
| *prefer X **to** Y* | prefer X *than* Y |
| *a responsibility **to*** + verb / **for** + noun | |
| *resistance **to*** | resistance *against* |
| *a tendency **to*** | a tendency *of* |
| *think of X **as** Y* | think of X *to be* Y |
:::
