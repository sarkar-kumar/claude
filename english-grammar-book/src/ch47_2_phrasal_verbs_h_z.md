## 47.3 The Master List of Phrasal Verbs: H – Z

::: letter
Hand · Hang · Hold
:::

::: vocab
**Hand down** [বংশ পরম্পরায় দিয়ে যাওয়া; রায় দেওয়া]{.bn} — to pass on to the next generation; to deliver (a judgement). *Ex:* These customs have been **handed down** for centuries.

**Hand in** [★]{.star} [জমা দেওয়া]{.bn} — to submit. *Ex:* **Hand in** your assignments by Friday.

**Hand out** [বিতরণ করা]{.bn} — to distribute. *Ex:* Volunteers **handed out** relief goods.

**Hand over** [★]{.star} [হস্তান্তর করা]{.bn} — to give control or possession to another. *Ex:* The thief was **handed over** to the police.

**Hang about / around** [উদ্দেশ্যহীনভাবে ঘোরাফেরা করা]{.bn} — to wait or stay idly. *Ex:* Some boys were **hanging around** the gate.

**Hang on** [অপেক্ষা করা; শক্ত করে ধরে রাখা]{.bn} — to wait; to hold tightly; to depend on. *Ex:* **Hang on** a minute. / Everything **hangs on** his decision.

**Hang up** [ফোন রেখে দেওয়া]{.bn} — to end a telephone call. *Ex:* She **hung up** on me.

**Hold back** [সংযত রাখা; গোপন রাখা]{.bn} — to restrain; to hide. *Ex:* She **held back** her tears. / Don't **hold back** any information.

**Hold good** [প্রযোজ্য থাকা]{.bn} — to remain valid. *Ex:* The offer still **holds good**.

**Hold on** [অপেক্ষা করা; ধরে থাকা]{.bn} — to wait; to keep holding. *Ex:* **Hold on**, I'll call him.

**Hold out** [★]{.star} [টিকে থাকা; প্রদান করা]{.bn} — to resist, last; to offer. *Ex:* The soldiers **held out** for a week. / The plan **holds out** hope for the poor.

**Hold over** [স্থগিত রাখা]{.bn} — to postpone. *Ex:* The matter was **held over** till the next meeting.

**Hold up** [★]{.star} [বিলম্বিত করা; ডাকাতি করা; তুলে ধরা]{.bn} — to delay; to rob with a weapon; to present as an example. *Ex:* We were **held up** in traffic. / Robbers **held up** the bank. / He is **held up** as a model of honesty.
:::

::: letter
Keep · Knock
:::

::: vocab
**Keep away (from)** [দূরে থাকা]{.bn} — to stay at a distance. *Ex:* **Keep away from** bad company.

**Keep back** [গোপন রাখা; আটকে রাখা]{.bn} — to withhold; to hide. *Ex:* He **kept back** part of the money.

**Keep down** [দমন করা; কম রাখা]{.bn} — to control; to suppress. *Ex:* We must **keep down** costs.

**Keep off** [দূরে থাকা]{.bn} — to stay away from. *Ex:* **Keep off** the grass.

**Keep on** [★]{.star} [চালিয়ে যাওয়া]{.bn} — to continue. *Ex:* He **kept on** talking.

**Keep out** [প্রবেশ করতে না দেওয়া]{.bn} — to prevent from entering. *Ex:* Danger! **Keep out**.

**Keep up** [★]{.star} [বজায় রাখা]{.bn} — to maintain; to continue. *Ex:* **Keep up** the good work. / We must **keep up** our traditions.

**Keep up with** [★]{.star} [তাল মিলিয়ে চলা]{.bn} — to stay at the same level as. *Ex:* It is hard to **keep up with** the latest technology.

**Knock down** [ধাক্কা দিয়ে ফেলে দেওয়া; ভেঙে ফেলা]{.bn} — to hit and make fall; to demolish. *Ex:* A bus **knocked down** a pedestrian.

**Knock off** [কাজ বন্ধ করা; কমানো]{.bn} — to stop work; to reduce a price. *Ex:* We **knock off** at 5 p.m.

**Knock out** [অজ্ঞান করে দেওয়া; প্রতিযোগিতা থেকে বাদ দেওয়া]{.bn} — to make unconscious; to eliminate. *Ex:* Bangladesh **knocked out** the defending champions.
:::

::: letter
Lay · Let · Live · Look
:::

::: vocab
**Lay aside / by** [সঞ্চয় করা; সরিয়ে রাখা]{.bn} — to save; to put aside. *Ex:* He has **laid aside** money for his old age.

**Lay down** [★]{.star} [ত্যাগ করা; নির্ধারণ করা]{.bn} — to give up (arms, life); to establish (rules). *Ex:* Our martyrs **laid down** their lives for the country. / The rules **laid down** by the authority must be followed.

**Lay off** [ছাঁটাই করা]{.bn} — to dismiss workers. *Ex:* The factory **laid off** 200 workers.

**Lay out** [নকশা করা; সাজানো; ব্যয় করা]{.bn} — to arrange; to plan; to spend. *Ex:* The garden is beautifully **laid out**.

**Let down** [★]{.star} [হতাশ করা]{.bn} — to disappoint. *Ex:* Don't **let** me **down**.

**Let in** [প্রবেশ করতে দেওয়া]{.bn} — to allow to enter. *Ex:* **Let** him **in**.

**Let off** [রেহাই দেওয়া; ছোড়া]{.bn} — to excuse from punishment; to fire (a gun, firework). *Ex:* The teacher **let** him **off** with a warning.

**Let out** [ফাঁস করা; ছেড়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — to reveal; to release. *Ex:* Who **let out** the secret?

**Live on** [খেয়ে বেঁচে থাকা]{.bn} — to survive on. *Ex:* Cows **live on** grass.

**Live up to** [প্রত্যাশা পূরণ করা]{.bn} — to meet expectations. *Ex:* He **lived up to** his parents' expectations.

**Look after** [★]{.star} [দেখাশোনা করা]{.bn} — to take care of. *Ex:* She **looks after** her younger brothers.

**Look back** [অতীতের কথা ভাবা]{.bn} — to remember the past. *Ex:* **Looking back**, I regret nothing.

**Look down on / upon** [★]{.star} [ঘৃণা করা, হেয় চোখে দেখা]{.bn} — to despise. *Ex:* Don't **look down upon** the poor.

**Look for** [★]{.star} [খোঁজা]{.bn} — to search for. *Ex:* I am **looking for** my keys.

**Look forward to** [★]{.star} [সাগ্রহে অপেক্ষা করা]{.bn} — to anticipate with pleasure. *Ex:* I **look forward to** hearing from you.

**Look in (on)** [সংক্ষেপে দেখা করে যাওয়া]{.bn} — to pay a short visit. *Ex:* I'll **look in on** you tomorrow.

**Look into** [★]{.star} [তদন্ত করা]{.bn} — to investigate. *Ex:* The police are **looking into** the matter.

**Look on** [দর্শক হিসেবে দেখা]{.bn} — to watch without taking part. *Ex:* People **looked on** as the house burned.

**Look out** [★]{.star} [সতর্ক থাকা]{.bn} — to be careful. *Ex:* **Look out**! A car is coming.

**Look over** [পরীক্ষা করা]{.bn} — to examine quickly. *Ex:* Please **look over** my essay.

**Look through** [চোখ বুলিয়ে নেওয়া]{.bn} — to read quickly. *Ex:* I **looked through** the newspaper.

**Look up** [★]{.star} [অভিধানে খোঁজা; উন্নতি হওয়া]{.bn} — to search for information; to improve. *Ex:* **Look up** the word in a dictionary. / Things are **looking up**.

**Look up to** [★]{.star} [শ্রদ্ধা করা]{.bn} — to respect. *Ex:* Students **look up to** their teachers.
:::

::: letter
Make · Make up
:::

::: vocab
**Make for** [দিকে যাওয়া; সহায়ক হওয়া]{.bn} — to move towards; to contribute to. *Ex:* The ship **made for** the harbour. / Good manners **make for** a happy life.

**Make off (with)** [চুরি করে পালানো]{.bn} — to run away (with something stolen). *Ex:* The thief **made off with** my bag.

**Make out** [★]{.star} [বুঝতে পারা; দেখতে পাওয়া; লেখা]{.bn} — to understand; to see with difficulty; to write (a cheque). *Ex:* I can't **make out** what he means. / **Make out** a cheque for 5,000 taka.

**Make over** [হস্তান্তর করা]{.bn} — to transfer ownership. *Ex:* He **made over** his property to his son.

**Make up** [★]{.star} [গঠন করা; বানানো; মিটমাট করা; প্রসাধন করা]{.bn} — to form; to invent (a story); to become friends again; to apply cosmetics. *Ex:* Women **make up** half the population. / He **made up** an excuse. / They quarrelled but soon **made up**.

**Make up for** [★]{.star} [ক্ষতিপূরণ করা]{.bn} — to compensate for. *Ex:* He worked hard to **make up for** lost time.

**Make up one's mind** [মনস্থির করা]{.bn} — to decide. *Ex:* I have **made up my mind** to study abroad.
:::

::: letter
Pass · Pay · Pick · Pull
:::

::: vocab
**Pass away** [★]{.star} [মারা যাওয়া]{.bn} — to die (polite). *Ex:* His grandfather **passed away** last year.

**Pass by** [পাশ দিয়ে যাওয়া; উপেক্ষা করা]{.bn} — to go past; to ignore. *Ex:* Opportunities **pass by** the lazy.

**Pass for / as** [বলে চালিয়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — to be accepted as (often falsely). *Ex:* He could **pass for** a foreigner.

**Pass off** [সুষ্ঠুভাবে সম্পন্ন হওয়া; মিথ্যা পরিচয়ে চালানো]{.bn} — to take place (well); to present falsely. *Ex:* The election **passed off** peacefully. / He **passed** himself **off** as a doctor.

**Pass on** [হস্তান্তর করা]{.bn} — to transmit; to give to another. *Ex:* Please **pass on** the message.

**Pass out** [অজ্ঞান হওয়া; (BrE) প্রশিক্ষণ শেষ করা]{.bn} — to faint; to complete military training. *Ex:* He **passed out** in the heat.

**Pay back** [ঋণ শোধ করা; প্রতিশোধ নেওয়া]{.bn} — to repay; to take revenge. *Ex:* I'll **pay** you **back** next week.

**Pay off** [সম্পূর্ণ পরিশোধ করা; সুফল দেওয়া]{.bn} — to repay fully; to succeed. *Ex:* His hard work **paid off**.

**Pick out** [বেছে নেওয়া; চিনতে পারা]{.bn} — to choose; to recognise. *Ex:* She **picked out** the best mangoes.

**Pick up** [★]{.star} [তুলে নেওয়া; শিখে ফেলা; গাড়িতে তোলা; উন্নতি হওয়া]{.bn} — to lift; to learn casually; to collect someone; to improve. *Ex:* He **picked up** English in London. / I'll **pick** you **up** at 7. / Business is **picking up**.

**Pull down** [★]{.star} [ভেঙে ফেলা]{.bn} — to demolish. *Ex:* The old building was **pulled down**.

**Pull off** [সফলভাবে সম্পন্ন করা]{.bn} — to succeed in something difficult. *Ex:* The team **pulled off** a surprise win.

**Pull through** [সেরে ওঠা]{.bn} — to recover from illness. *Ex:* The doctors hope she will **pull through**.

**Pull up** [থামানো; তিরস্কার করা]{.bn} — to stop (a vehicle); to scold. *Ex:* The taxi **pulled up** at the gate.
:::

**Put down** [★]{.star} [দমন করা; লিখে রাখা]{.bn} — to suppress; to write. *Ex:* The army **put down** the rebellion. / **Put down** your name.

**Put forward** [প্রস্তাব করা]{.bn} — to propose. *Ex:* He **put forward** a new plan.

**Put in** [ব্যয় করা; দাখিল করা]{.bn} — to spend (time/effort); to submit. *Ex:* She **put in** a lot of effort.

**Put off** [★]{.star} [স্থগিত করা; নিরুৎসাহিত করা; খোলা]{.bn} — to postpone; to discourage; to take off. *Ex:* Never **put off** till tomorrow what you can do today. / The meeting was **put off**.

**Put on** [★]{.star} [পরিধান করা; ভান করা; বাড়ানো]{.bn} — to wear; to pretend; to gain (weight). *Ex:* **Put on** your shoes. / He **put on** an air of innocence. / She has **put on** weight.

**Put out** [★]{.star} [নেভানো; বিরক্ত করা]{.bn} — to extinguish; to annoy. *Ex:* The firemen **put out** the fire.

**Put up** [★]{.star} [থাকা; থাকতে দেওয়া; নির্মাণ করা; বাড়ানো]{.bn} — to stay; to accommodate; to build; to raise. *Ex:* We **put up** at a hotel. / They **put up** a monument.

**Put up with** [★]{.star} [সহ্য করা]{.bn} — to tolerate. *Ex:* I can't **put up with** his rudeness any longer.

**Run into** [★]{.star} [হঠাৎ দেখা হওয়া; ধাক্কা খাওয়া; পৌঁছানো]{.bn} — to meet by chance; to collide with; to reach (an amount). *Ex:* I **ran into** an old friend. / The car **ran into** a tree. / The cost **ran into** millions.

**Run out (of)** [★]{.star} [ফুরিয়ে যাওয়া]{.bn} — to have no more of. *Ex:* We have **run out of** sugar. / Time is **running out**.

**Run over** [গাড়ি চাপা দেওয়া; চোখ বুলানো]{.bn} — to knock down with a vehicle; to read quickly. *Ex:* A dog was **run over** by a truck.

**Run through** [দ্রুত দেখা; উড়িয়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — to read/practise quickly; to spend quickly. *Ex:* He **ran through** his fortune in a year.

**Run up against** [সম্মুখীন হওয়া]{.bn} — to face (a difficulty). *Ex:* We **ran up against** many problems.

## 47.5 Common Errors with Phrasal Verbs

::: errs
1. ✘ Please switch on it. ✔ Please switch **it on**. (pronoun object between verb and particle)
2. ✘ Look him after. ✔ **Look after** him. (inseparable)
3. ✘ I can't put up his rudeness. ✔ I can't put up **with** his rudeness.
4. ✘ The meeting was put out till Monday. ✔ The meeting was put **off** till Monday.
5. ✘ He gave out smoking. ✔ He gave **up** smoking.
6. ✘ The fire brigade put off the fire. ✔ … put **out** the fire.
7. ✘ She takes over her mother. (= resembles) ✔ She takes **after** her mother.
8. ✘ I look forward to see you. ✔ I look forward to **seeing** you.
9. ✘ Cholera broke up in the village. ✔ Cholera broke **out** in the village.
10. ✘ He turned out the offer. (= rejected) ✔ He turned **down** the offer.
:::

## 47.6 Phrasal Verbs in the SAT, GRE and GMAT

::: gre
- **SAT Reading — Words in Context:** phrasal verbs in passages are often tested for their **meaning in context** (*"take up" most nearly means — "begin" / "occupy"*).
- **GMAT formal style:** a **single Latin-based verb** is usually preferred over an informal phrasal verb: *investigate* (look into), *tolerate* (put up with), *postpone* (put off), *abolish* (do away with), *discover* (find out), *establish* (set up), *cancel* (call off), *continue* (carry on), *resemble* (take after), *invent/devise* (come up with).
- **GRE vocabulary pairs:** many GRE words are formal equivalents of phrasal verbs: *abjure* (give up), *abrogate* (do away with), *exacerbate* (make worse), *mitigate* (tone down), *placate* (calm down), *disparage* (run down), *emulate* (live up to / imitate), *capitulate* (give in), *abscond* (run off), *ferret out* (find out).
:::
