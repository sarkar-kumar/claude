## 48.3 The Master List of Idioms and Phrases: F – M

::: letter
F
:::

::: vocab
**Face the music** [★]{.star} [পরিণতির মুখোমুখি হওয়া]{.bn} — to accept the unpleasant consequences of one's actions. *Ex:* He cheated, and now he must face the music.

**Fair and square** [সৎভাবে]{.bn} — honestly, fairly. *Ex:* We won the match fair and square.

**Fair sex** [নারী জাতি]{.bn} — women. *Ex:* The fair sex is taking up leadership roles.

**Fall flat** [ব্যর্থ হওয়া]{.bn} — to fail to have the intended effect. *Ex:* His joke fell flat.

**Fall to one's lot** [ভাগ্যে পড়া]{.bn} — to become one's responsibility. *Ex:* The task fell to my lot.

**Far and away** [নিঃসন্দেহে, সবচেয়ে]{.bn} — by a great amount. *Ex:* She is far and away the best singer.

**Far and near / Far and wide** [★]{.star} [সর্বত্র]{.bn} — everywhere. *Ex:* His fame spread far and wide.

**Feather in one's cap** [★]{.star} [গৌরবের বিষয়]{.bn} — an achievement to be proud of. *Ex:* Winning the award was a feather in his cap.

**Feather one's nest** [নিজের আখের গোছানো]{.bn} — to make money dishonestly for oneself. *Ex:* The official feathered his nest while in office.

**Few and far between** [★]{.star} [কদাচিৎ, বিরল]{.bn} — rare. *Ex:* Honest officials are few and far between.

**Fight shy of** [এড়িয়ে চলা]{.bn} — to avoid. *Ex:* He fights shy of hard work.

**Fish in troubled waters** [★]{.star} [ঘোলা জলে মাছ শিকার করা]{.bn} — to take advantage of a confused situation. *Ex:* Some politicians fish in troubled waters during a crisis.

**Flesh and blood** [★]{.star} [রক্তমাংসের মানুষ; আত্মীয়]{.bn} — human nature; one's own relatives. *Ex:* It is more than flesh and blood can bear. / He is my own flesh and blood.

**Fly in the face of** [সরাসরি বিরোধিতা করা]{.bn} — to oppose openly. *Ex:* His theory flies in the face of the evidence.

**Fly off the handle** [★]{.star} [রেগে আগুন হওয়া]{.bn} — to lose one's temper suddenly. *Ex:* He flies off the handle at the slightest provocation.

**Follow suit** [অনুকরণ করা]{.bn} — to do the same as someone else. *Ex:* When one shop lowered prices, others followed suit.

**Foot the bill** [খরচ বহন করা]{.bn} — to pay. *Ex:* Who will foot the bill for the party?

**For good** [★]{.star} [চিরকালের জন্য]{.bn} — permanently. *Ex:* He has left Bangladesh for good.

**French leave** [★]{.star} [বিনা অনুমতিতে ছুটি]{.bn} — absence without permission. *Ex:* He took French leave and was dismissed.

**From hand to mouth** [★]{.star} [দিন আনে দিন খায়]{.bn} — with just enough for immediate needs. *Ex:* The day-labourers live from hand to mouth.

**From the bottom of one's heart** [অন্তরের অন্তস্তল থেকে]{.bn} — sincerely. *Ex:* I thank you from the bottom of my heart.

**From time to time** [মাঝে মাঝে]{.bn} — occasionally. *Ex:* He visits us from time to time.
:::

::: letter
G
:::

::: vocab
**Get into hot water** [★]{.star} [বিপদে পড়া]{.bn} — to get into trouble. *Ex:* He got into hot water for coming late.

**Get the better of** [পরাস্ত করা]{.bn} — to defeat; to overcome. *Ex:* His anger got the better of him.

**Get wind of** [আঁচ পাওয়া]{.bn} — to hear about secretly. *Ex:* The police got wind of the plan.

**Gift of the gab** [★]{.star} [বাকপটুতা]{.bn} — the ability to speak fluently and persuasively. *Ex:* Salesmen need the gift of the gab.

**Give a piece of one's mind** [কড়া কথা শোনানো]{.bn} — to scold frankly. *Ex:* I gave him a piece of my mind.

**Give the devil his due** [শত্রুর প্রাপ্য প্রশংসা দেওয়া]{.bn} — to be fair even to someone one dislikes. *Ex:* Give the devil his due — he is a hard worker.

**Give the slip** [ফাঁকি দিয়ে পালানো]{.bn} — to escape from. *Ex:* The thief gave the police the slip.

**Give up the ghost** [★]{.star} [মারা যাওয়া]{.bn} — to die. *Ex:* The old man gave up the ghost last night.

**Go to the dogs** [★]{.star} [উচ্ছন্নে যাওয়া]{.bn} — to be ruined. *Ex:* Without proper care, the business went to the dogs.

**Go the whole hog** [পুরোপুরি করা]{.bn} — to do something thoroughly. *Ex:* If you're going to renovate, go the whole hog.

**Gnash one's teeth** [দাঁত কিড়মিড় করা]{.bn} — to show anger. *Ex:* He gnashed his teeth in rage.

**Greek to me** [★]{.star} [দুর্বোধ্য]{.bn} — impossible to understand. *Ex:* The lecture on quantum physics was Greek to me.

**Green-eyed** [ঈর্ষাপরায়ণ]{.bn} — jealous. *Ex:* He is green-eyed at his brother's success.

**Green horn** [অনভিজ্ঞ ব্যক্তি]{.bn} — an inexperienced person. *Ex:* He is a greenhorn in politics.
:::

::: letter
H
:::

::: vocab
**Hale and hearty** [সুস্থ-সবল]{.bn} — strong and healthy. *Ex:* My grandfather is still hale and hearty.

**Hand in glove** [★]{.star} [ঘনিষ্ঠ যোগসাজশে; গলায় গলায় ভাব]{.bn} — in close association (often in wrongdoing). *Ex:* The smuggler was hand in glove with some officials.

**Hand to mouth** — see *From hand to mouth*.

**Hard and fast** [★]{.star} [কঠোর ও অনমনীয়]{.bn} — strict, fixed. *Ex:* There is no hard and fast rule about this.

**Hard up** [অর্থকষ্টে]{.bn} — short of money. *Ex:* He is hard up these days.

**Haul over the coals** [কঠোর তিরস্কার করা]{.bn} — to scold severely. *Ex:* The boss hauled him over the coals.

**Have a finger in every pie** [সব ব্যাপারে নাক গলানো]{.bn} — to be involved in many things. *Ex:* He has a finger in every pie in the village.

**Head and shoulders above** [অনেক শ্রেষ্ঠ]{.bn} — far superior to. *Ex:* She is head and shoulders above her classmates.

**Head over heels** [আপাদমস্তক; সম্পূর্ণ]{.bn} — completely (esp. in love). *Ex:* He fell head over heels in love with her.

**Heart and soul** [★]{.star} [সর্বান্তঃকরণে]{.bn} — with full devotion. *Ex:* She worked heart and soul for the poor.

**Hen-pecked husband** [স্ত্রৈণ স্বামী]{.bn} — a husband dominated by his wife. *Ex:* He is a hen-pecked husband.

**High time** [উপযুক্ত সময়]{.bn} — the proper (or already late) time. *Ex:* It is high time we acted.

**Hit below the belt** [অন্যায় আঘাত করা]{.bn} — to act unfairly. *Ex:* Criticising his family was hitting below the belt.

**Hit the nail on the head** [★]{.star} [সঠিক কথা বলা]{.bn} — to say exactly the right thing. *Ex:* You hit the nail on the head when you blamed corruption.

**Hold water** [★]{.star} [যুক্তিসঙ্গত/টেকসই হওয়া]{.bn} — to be logical or valid. *Ex:* His argument does not hold water.

**Hold one's tongue** [চুপ থাকা]{.bn} — to remain silent. *Ex:* Hold your tongue!

**Hot water** — see *Get into hot water*.

**Hue and cry** [★]{.star} [হৈচৈ, শোরগোল]{.bn} — a loud public protest. *Ex:* There was a hue and cry over the price hike.

**Hush money** [ঘুষ (মুখ বন্ধ রাখার জন্য)]{.bn} — a bribe to keep something secret. *Ex:* He was paid hush money.
:::

::: letter
I
:::

::: vocab
**Ill-gotten gains** [অসৎ উপায়ে অর্জিত সম্পদ]{.bn} — money obtained dishonestly. *Ex:* Ill-gotten gains never prosper.

**In a nutshell** [★]{.star} [সংক্ষেপে]{.bn} — briefly. *Ex:* In a nutshell, we failed.

**In black and white** [★]{.star} [লিখিতভাবে]{.bn} — in writing. *Ex:* Please give me the agreement in black and white.

**In cold blood** [★]{.star} [ঠান্ডা মাথায়, নির্মমভাবে]{.bn} — deliberately and cruelly. *Ex:* The man was killed in cold blood.

**In full swing** [পুরোদমে]{.bn} — at the peak of activity. *Ex:* Preparations for the festival are in full swing.

**In hot water** [বিপদে]{.bn} — in trouble. *Ex:* He is in hot water with his boss.

**In the air** [গুজব হিসেবে ছড়িয়ে থাকা]{.bn} — being talked about; uncertain. *Ex:* Rumours of a strike are in the air.

**In the dark** [অজ্ঞ]{.bn} — uninformed. *Ex:* We were kept in the dark about the plan.

**In the long run** [★]{.star} [পরিণামে]{.bn} — eventually. *Ex:* Honesty pays in the long run.

**In the nick of time** [★]{.star} [ঠিক সময়ে]{.bn} — just in time. *Ex:* The doctor arrived in the nick of time.

**In the teeth of** [প্রবল বিরোধিতা সত্ত্বেও]{.bn} — in spite of strong opposition. *Ex:* The law was passed in the teeth of opposition.

**In vain** [বৃথা]{.bn} — uselessly. *Ex:* All our efforts were in vain.

**Ins and outs** [★]{.star} [খুঁটিনাটি]{.bn} — all the details. *Ex:* He knows the ins and outs of banking.

**Iron will** [দৃঢ় সংকল্প]{.bn} — very strong determination. *Ex:* Bangabandhu was a man of iron will.

**Itching palm** [ঘুষের লোভ]{.bn} — greed for money, bribes. *Ex:* The officer has an itching palm.
:::

::: letter
J · K
:::

::: vocab
**Jack of all trades** — see *A Jack of all trades*.

**Jump to conclusions** [তাড়াহুড়ো করে সিদ্ধান্তে পৌঁছানো]{.bn} — to decide too quickly. *Ex:* Don't jump to conclusions without proof.

**Keep an eye on** [নজর রাখা]{.bn} — to watch carefully. *Ex:* Keep an eye on my bag, please.

**Keep at bay** [দূরে রাখা]{.bn} — to keep away. *Ex:* Exercise keeps diseases at bay.

**Keep body and soul together** [কোনোমতে বেঁচে থাকা]{.bn} — to survive with difficulty. *Ex:* He earns barely enough to keep body and soul together.

**Keep one's fingers crossed** [সাফল্যের আশা করা]{.bn} — to hope for good luck. *Ex:* Keep your fingers crossed for my result.

**Keep the ball rolling** [কাজ চালিয়ে যাওয়া]{.bn} — to keep an activity going. *Ex:* Let's keep the ball rolling with fresh ideas.

**Keep the wolf from the door** [অভাব দূরে রাখা]{.bn} — to avoid hunger and poverty. *Ex:* He works two jobs to keep the wolf from the door.

**Kick the bucket** [★]{.star} [মারা যাওয়া]{.bn} — to die (informal). *Ex:* The old miser kicked the bucket last year.

**Kill two birds with one stone** [★]{.star} [এক ঢিলে দুই পাখি মারা]{.bn} — to achieve two aims with one action. *Ex:* By cycling to work, he kills two birds with one stone — exercise and saving money.

**Kith and kin** [★]{.star} [আত্মীয়-স্বজন]{.bn} — friends and relatives. *Ex:* He invited all his kith and kin to the wedding.
:::

::: letter
L
:::

::: vocab
**Last but not least** [শেষ কিন্তু কম গুরুত্বপূর্ণ নয়]{.bn} — last in order but equally important. *Ex:* Last but not least, I thank my parents.

**Laughing stock** [★]{.star} [হাসির পাত্র]{.bn} — an object of ridicule. *Ex:* His foolish behaviour made him a laughing stock.

**Lay down one's life** [জীবন উৎসর্গ করা]{.bn} — to sacrifice one's life. *Ex:* Many freedom fighters laid down their lives for the country.

**Lead a cat and dog life** [সর্বদা ঝগড়া করে জীবন কাটানো]{.bn} — to quarrel constantly. *Ex:* The couple lead a cat-and-dog life.

**Leave no stone unturned** [★]{.star} [সাধ্যমতো সব চেষ্টা করা]{.bn} — to try every possible means. *Ex:* The police left no stone unturned to catch the killer.

**Leave in the lurch** [★]{.star} [বিপদে ফেলে চলে যাওয়া]{.bn} — to desert someone in difficulty. *Ex:* His friends left him in the lurch.

**Lend an ear** [মনোযোগ দিয়ে শোনা]{.bn} — to listen. *Ex:* Lend me your ears.

**Let bygones be bygones** [অতীত ভুলে যাওয়া]{.bn} — to forget past quarrels. *Ex:* Let bygones be bygones and be friends again.

**Let the cat out of the bag** [★]{.star} [গোপন কথা ফাঁস করা]{.bn} — to reveal a secret. *Ex:* He let the cat out of the bag about the surprise party.

**Lion's share** [★]{.star} [সিংহভাগ]{.bn} — the largest part. *Ex:* The eldest son got the lion's share of the property.

**Live from hand to mouth** — see *From hand to mouth*.

**Look a gift horse in the mouth** [দানের গরুর দাঁত গোনা]{.bn} — to find fault with a gift. *Ex:* Don't look a gift horse in the mouth.

**Lose heart** [হতাশ হওয়া]{.bn} — to be discouraged. *Ex:* Don't lose heart; try again.

**Lose one's head** [মাথা গরম করা; দিশেহারা হওয়া]{.bn} — to panic or lose control. *Ex:* Don't lose your head in an emergency.

**Lose temper** [রেগে যাওয়া]{.bn} — to become angry. *Ex:* He loses his temper easily.
:::

::: letter
M
:::

::: vocab
**Maiden speech** [প্রথম ভাষণ]{.bn} — a first speech (esp. in Parliament). *Ex:* The new MP delivered his maiden speech.

**Make a clean breast of** [★]{.star} [সব খুলে বলা, অপরাধ স্বীকার করা]{.bn} — to confess fully. *Ex:* The accused made a clean breast of his crime.

**Make a mountain of a molehill** [★]{.star} [তিলকে তাল করা]{.bn} — to exaggerate a small problem. *Ex:* Don't make a mountain of a molehill.

**Make amends** [ক্ষতিপূরণ করা]{.bn} — to compensate. *Ex:* He tried to make amends for his mistake.

**Make both ends meet** [★]{.star} [আয়-ব্যয়ের সংগতি রাখা]{.bn} — to live within one's income. *Ex:* With rising prices, poor families can hardly make both ends meet.

**Make hay while the sun shines** [★]{.star} [সুযোগের সদ্ব্যবহার করা]{.bn} — to use an opportunity while it lasts. *Ex:* You're young; make hay while the sun shines.

**Make one's blood boil** [ভীষণ রাগিয়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — to make very angry. *Ex:* Cruelty to children makes my blood boil.

**Man in the street** [★]{.star} [সাধারণ মানুষ]{.bn} — an ordinary person. *Ex:* The man in the street wants peace and stability.

**Man of letters** — see *A man of letters*.

**Many a time** [অনেকবার]{.bn} — often. *Ex:* Many a time I have warned him.

**Mare's nest** [অলীক আবিষ্কার]{.bn} — a false discovery; a mess. *Ex:* His great discovery proved a mare's nest.

**Midas touch** [★]{.star} [যাতে হাত দেয় তাতেই সাফল্য]{.bn} — the ability to make money from everything. *Ex:* The businessman seems to have the Midas touch.

**Mind one's p's and q's** [সাবধানে আচরণ করা]{.bn} — to be careful about one's behaviour. *Ex:* Mind your p's and q's in front of the boss.

**Move heaven and earth** [সর্বাত্মক চেষ্টা করা]{.bn} — to make every possible effort. *Ex:* She moved heaven and earth to get her son admitted.
:::
