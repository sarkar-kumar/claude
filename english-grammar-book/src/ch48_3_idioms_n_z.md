::: vocab
**Neck and neck** [সমানে সমান]{.bn} — exactly level in a race or competition. *Ex:* The two horses ran neck and neck.

**Nip in the bud** [★]{.star} [অঙ্কুরেই বিনাশ করা]{.bn} — to stop something at an early stage. *Ex:* The conspiracy was nipped in the bud.

**No love lost** [পরস্পরের প্রতি বিরূপ]{.bn} — mutual dislike. *Ex:* There is no love lost between the two leaders.

**Null and void** [★]{.star} [বাতিল, অকার্যকর]{.bn} — having no legal force. *Ex:* The agreement was declared null and void.

**Of no avail** [★]{.star} [নিষ্ফল]{.bn} — useless. *Ex:* All his efforts were of no avail.

**Off and on / On and off** [মাঝে মাঝে]{.bn} — occasionally. *Ex:* It rained on and off all day.

**Off the cuff** [প্রস্তুতি ছাড়া]{.bn} — without preparation. *Ex:* He gave an off-the-cuff speech.

**Old guard** [পুরোনো পন্থী ব্যক্তিবর্গ]{.bn} — long-standing members resisting change. *Ex:* The old guard opposed the reforms.

**On the horns of a dilemma** [★]{.star} [উভয় সংকটে]{.bn} — facing two equally unpleasant choices. *Ex:* I am on the horns of a dilemma — whether to study abroad or stay with my parents.

**On the spur of the moment** [ঝোঁকের মাথায়]{.bn} — suddenly, without planning. *Ex:* We decided on the spur of the moment to visit Sylhet.

**On the verge of** [দ্বারপ্রান্তে]{.bn} — very near to. *Ex:* Many species are on the verge of extinction.

**Once and for all** [চিরতরে]{.bn} — finally, permanently. *Ex:* Let us settle this matter once and for all.

**Once in a blue moon** [★]{.star} [কালেভদ্রে]{.bn} — very rarely. *Ex:* He visits us once in a blue moon.

**Out and out** [পুরোদস্তুর]{.bn} — thorough, complete. *Ex:* He is an out-and-out rogue.

**Out of sorts** [অসুস্থ বা মনমরা]{.bn} — unwell or upset. *Ex:* She is out of sorts today.

**Out of the question** [★]{.star} [অসম্ভব]{.bn} — impossible. *Ex:* A holiday this year is out of the question.

**Out of the wood(s)** [বিপদমুক্ত]{.bn} — out of danger. *Ex:* The patient is not yet out of the woods.

**Over head and ears** [আকণ্ঠ নিমজ্জিত]{.bn} — deeply (in debt, in love). *Ex:* He is over head and ears in debt.

**Play second fiddle** [অধীনস্থ ভূমিকা পালন করা]{.bn} — to take a secondary role. *Ex:* He refused to play second fiddle to his junior.

**Play truant** [স্কুল পালানো]{.bn} — to stay away from school without permission. *Ex:* The boy often plays truant.

**Point-blank** [স্পষ্টভাবে, সরাসরি]{.bn} — directly, bluntly. *Ex:* He refused point-blank.

**Pros and cons** [★]{.star} [ভালো-মন্দ দিক]{.bn} — advantages and disadvantages. *Ex:* Consider the pros and cons before deciding.

**Pull one's leg** [★]{.star} [ঠাট্টা করা]{.bn} — to tease, joke. *Ex:* Don't take it seriously; I was only pulling your leg.

**Put on airs** [ভাব দেখানো]{.bn} — to behave arrogantly. *Ex:* She puts on airs because she is rich.

**Put the cart before the horse** [★]{.star} [ঘোড়ার আগে গাড়ি জোড়া; উল্টো কাজ করা]{.bn} — to do things in the wrong order. *Ex:* Buying furniture before the house is putting the cart before the horse.

**Put one's foot down** [দৃঢ় অবস্থান নেওয়া]{.bn} — to take a firm stand. *Ex:* The headmaster put his foot down about late arrivals.

**Put one's foot in it** [বেফাঁস কথা বলা]{.bn} — to say something embarrassing. *Ex:* I really put my foot in it when I asked about her divorce.

## 48.6 Common Errors with Idioms

::: errs
1. ✘ It is raining cats and rats. ✔ It is raining **cats and dogs**.
2. ✘ He is a black goat of the family. ✔ He is the **black sheep** of the family.
3. ✘ At sixes and eights ✔ **At sixes and sevens**
4. ✘ A bolt from the sky ✔ **A bolt from the blue**
5. ✘ He has a lion share. ✔ He has **the lion's share**.
6. ✘ By hook and crook ✔ **By hook or by crook**
7. ✘ Hand in hand with the thieves (= in collusion) ✔ **Hand in glove** with the thieves
8. ✘ Once in a red moon ✔ **Once in a blue moon**
9. ✘ He burnt the night oil. ✔ He burnt **the midnight oil**.
10. ✘ To make a mountain from a molehill ✔ To make a mountain **of / out of** a molehill
:::

## 48.7 Idioms in the SAT, GRE and GMAT

::: gre
- **SAT Reading:** idioms in passages are tested in **"words in context"** questions — choose the meaning that fits the sentence (*"the plan **fell through**" — failed*).
- **GRE:** many advanced words are compressed idioms: *pyrrhic, quixotic, draconian, herculean, procrustean, Sisyphean* (endless, futile labour), *Pandora's box, Achilles' heel, Trojan horse, sword of Damocles, Gordian knot* — knowing the story behind them is valuable in Text Completion and Reading.
- **GMAT:** formal writing avoids colloquial idioms; a choice that uses slang or a cliché is rarely correct.
:::
