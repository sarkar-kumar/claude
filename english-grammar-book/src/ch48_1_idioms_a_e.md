## 48.1 What Is an Idiom?

An **idiom** is a group of words whose meaning **cannot be understood from the meanings of the individual words**. *To kick the bucket* has nothing to do with kicking or buckets — it means *to die*. A **phrase**, in this context, is a fixed expression that is used as a unit (*by and by, null and void, at a loss*).

::: ex
- *It's raining **cats and dogs**.* — raining very heavily
- *He **let the cat out of the bag**.* — revealed a secret
- *She is **the apple of her father's eye**.* — very dear to her father
:::

Idioms are tested in almost every BCS Preliminary, Bank and admission test ("*The idiom 'a white elephant' means …*"), and in BCS Written ("*Make sentences with these idioms*"). They also appear in SAT and GRE reading passages, where you must understand them from context.

::: tip
**How to learn idioms:** (1) Learn the **meaning with an example sentence** — never a list of meanings alone. (2) Know the **origin story** where possible (*Achilles' heel, crocodile tears, Herculean task*) — it fixes the meaning in memory. (3) **Don't change the words** of an idiom: *a bolt from the blue* (✘ a bolt from the sky); *at sixes and sevens* (✘ at sixes and eights). (4) In written exams, write a sentence that **clearly shows the meaning**.
:::

**Dead letter** [★]{.star} [অকার্যকর আইন]{.bn} — a law no longer in force. *Ex:* Many old laws have become dead letters.

**Dead of night** [গভীর রাত]{.bn} — the middle of the night. *Ex:* The thieves came in the dead of night.

**Dog in the manger** [★]{.star} [যে নিজেও ভোগ করে না, অন্যকেও করতে দেয় না]{.bn} — a person who prevents others from using what he cannot use himself. *Ex:* Don't be a dog in the manger — lend him the book you don't need.

**Dog's life** [দুর্দশাগ্রস্ত জীবন]{.bn} — a miserable life. *Ex:* The poor labourers lead a dog's life.

**Donkey's years** [দীর্ঘকাল]{.bn} — a very long time. *Ex:* I haven't seen him for donkey's years.

**Double-dealing** [প্রতারণা, দ্বিমুখী আচরণ]{.bn} — deceit. *Ex:* I hate his double-dealing.

**Down to earth** [বাস্তববাদী]{.bn} — practical, realistic. *Ex:* She is a down-to-earth person.

**Draw the line** [সীমারেখা টানা]{.bn} — to set a limit. *Ex:* I don't mind helping, but I draw the line at lying.

**Eat humble pie** [★]{.star} [অপমান মেনে নেওয়া; ভুল স্বীকার করা]{.bn} — to admit one's mistake humbly. *Ex:* After losing the argument, he had to eat humble pie.

**Eat one's words** [★]{.star} [কথা ফিরিয়ে নেওয়া]{.bn} — to take back what one has said. *Ex:* He was forced to eat his words.

**Egg on** [প্ররোচিত করা]{.bn} — to urge someone to do something (usually bad). *Ex:* His friends egged him on to fight.

**Elbow room** [চলাফেরার জায়গা]{.bn} — enough space or freedom. *Ex:* There is no elbow room in this bus.
