## 17.1 What Is a Preposition?

A **preposition** is a word placed **before** a noun or pronoun (or a noun-equivalent) to show its **relation** to some other word in the sentence — a relation of **place, time, direction, cause, manner, means, possession** and so on. *Pre-position* means "placed before".

::: ex
- The book is **on** the table. (place)
- We met **at** six o'clock. (time)
- He walked **towards** the river. (direction)
- He died **of** cholera. (cause)
- She cut the cake **with** a knife. (instrument)
- He went **by** bus. (means)
:::

The noun or pronoun after a preposition is its **object**, and a pronoun object is always in the **objective case**: *with **him**, for **us**, between you and **me**, except **her***.

The object of a preposition may be:

- a **noun / pronoun**: *in **the room**, for **them***
- a **gerund**: *fond **of reading**, without **saying** a word*
- an **adverb**: *from **here**, till **now**, since **then***
- a **phrase**: *from **behind the curtain**, until **after the exam***
- a **clause**: *Listen to **what he says**.*

## 17.2 Kinds of Prepositions

| Kind | Description | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | single, short words | at, by, for, from, in, of, off, on, out, over, through, till, to, up, with |
| **Compound** | formed by joining a preposition to another word (often *a-* or *be-*) | about, above, across, against, along, amid(st), among(st), around, before, behind, below, beneath, beside, between, beyond, inside, into, onto, outside, throughout, towards, underneath, upon, within, without |
| **Phrase (complex)** | a group of words used as one preposition | according to, along with, apart from, as for, because of, by means of, by dint of, by virtue of, by way of, due to, for the sake of, in accordance with, in addition to, in case of, in front of, in lieu of, in order to (infinitive marker), in place of, in spite of, instead of, in view of, on account of, on behalf of, owing to, with a view to, with reference to, with regard to, in the event of, in comparison with |
| **Participle** | present/past participles used as prepositions | barring, concerning, considering, during, excepting, notwithstanding, pending, regarding, respecting, touching, given, provided |
| **Disguised** | shortened forms | *a-* (= on): *He went **a**-fishing.* / *o'* (= of): *five **o'**clock* |
| **Double** | two prepositions together | from behind, from under, from among, till after, over against |

::: ex
- **According to** the report, prices will rise.
- The match was cancelled **because of** rain.
- He succeeded **by dint of** hard work.
- **In spite of** his illness, he attended the meeting.
- **Considering** his age, he is very active.
- **Pending** the inquiry, the officer was suspended.
- The cat jumped out **from under** the bed.
:::

## 17.3 Position of the Preposition

A preposition normally comes **before** its object. But it is placed at the **end** in these cases:

1. **Questions with *who, what, which, where*:** *What are you looking **at**? Who are you talking **to**? Where do you come **from**?*
2. **Relative clauses** (especially with *that* or an omitted relative): *This is the house (that) I live **in**. The man (whom) you spoke **to** is my uncle.* (Formal: *the house **in which** I live; the man **to whom** you spoke*.)
3. **Passive sentences:** *He was laughed **at**. The matter will be looked **into**. The child was taken care **of**.*
4. **Infinitive phrases:** *I need a chair to sit **on**. She has no one to talk **to**. Give me a pen to write **with**.*
5. **Exclamations:** *What a mess you are **in**!*

::: note
The old rule "Never end a sentence with a preposition" is **not** a real rule of English; the end position is natural and often the only correct choice (*What are you afraid of?*). But **don't** add an unnecessary preposition at the end: ✘ *Where are you going **to**?* ✘ *Where is he **at**?* → ✔ *Where are you going?* ✔ *Where is he?*
:::

## 17.4 Prepositions of Time

### 17.4.1 *At*, *On*, *In* (Time)

| Preposition | Used with | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | a **point** of time; clock time; festivals; short holiday periods; *night, noon, midnight, dawn, sunrise, sunset* | *at 6 o'clock, at noon, at night, at midnight, at dawn, at sunset, at lunchtime, at Eid, at Christmas, at the weekend (BrE), at present, at the moment, at the age of ten* |
| **on** | **days** and **dates**; a particular part of a particular day | *on Friday, on 26 March, on Independence Day, on my birthday, on Monday morning, on a cold night, on the morning of 16 December* |
| **in** | **longer periods**: parts of the day, months, seasons, years, decades, centuries | *in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, in May, in summer, in 1971, in the 1990s, in the 21st century, in a week* |

::: ex
- We wake up **at** six **in** the morning.
- He was born **on** 5 June 2000. / He was born **in** June / **in** 2000.
- We met **on** a rainy evening. (*evening* made particular → *on*)
- **At** night (✘ in night) / **in** the night (= during one particular night: *I woke up in the night*).
:::

::: rule
**No preposition** before *this, that, next, last, every, each, some, any, one, all, today, tomorrow, yesterday*: *I'll see you **next** Friday.* (✘ on next Friday) *He came **last** year.* (✘ in last year) *We meet **every** day. I'll do it **tomorrow** morning.*
:::

### 17.4.2 *Since*, *For*, *From*

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **since** | from a **point** of time in the past until now (perfect tenses) | *I have lived here **since** 2010 / **since** Monday / **since** childhood.* |
| **for** | a **period** (length) of time | *I have lived here **for** ten years / **for** three days.* |
| **from** | the **starting point**, usually with *to/till/until* (any tense) | *The shop is open **from** 9 **to** 5. He worked **from** morning **till** night.* |

::: ex
- ✘ I have been waiting since two hours. ✔ … **for** two hours.
- ✘ He has been ill from Monday. ✔ He has been ill **since** Monday.
- ✘ I am living here since 2015. ✔ I **have been living** here since 2015. (Chapter 21)
:::

### 17.4.3 *In*, *Within*, *After* (Time)

- ***in*** = at the end of a period (future): *I'll be back **in** an hour.* (= after one hour from now)
- ***within*** = before the end of a period: *Finish it **within** an hour.* (= not later than one hour)
- ***after*** = later than (mainly past, or with a definite time): *He returned **after** two days. Come **after** 5 p.m.* (✘ *I'll come after an hour* — prefer *in an hour*)

### 17.4.4 *By*, *Till / Until*, *Before*

- ***by*** = **not later than** (a deadline; the action happens once, at or before that time): *Submit the form **by** Friday. I'll be home **by** 10.*
- ***till / until*** = **up to** that time (the action continues): *Wait here **till** I return. The shop stays open **until** 9 p.m.* (✘ Wait here by 5)
- ***before*** = earlier than: *Come **before** sunset.*

### 17.4.5 *During*, *For*, *While*

- ***during*** + noun = **when** something happens (within a period): ***During** the war, many people died. He slept **during** the lecture.*
- ***for*** + period = **how long**: *He slept **for** two hours.*
- ***while*** is a **conjunction** + clause: ***While** I was reading, the light went out.* (✘ during I was reading)

### 17.4.6 *On time* / *In time*; *At the end* / *In the end*

- ***on time*** = punctually, at the planned time: *The train arrived **on time**.*
- ***in time*** = early enough (before the last moment): *We reached the station **in time** to catch the train.*
- ***at the end (of)*** = at the final point: ***at the end of** the month / **at the end of** the film.*
- ***in the end*** = finally, after a long time or many difficulties: ***In the end**, he agreed.*
- ***at last*** = finally (after impatience); ***at least*** = not less than.

## 17.5 Prepositions of Place and Position

### 17.5.1 *At*, *On*, *In* (Place)

| Preposition | Used for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | a **point**, a specific location; addresses with numbers; events; small towns and villages (traditional rule) | *at the door, at the bus stop, at the station, at the corner, at home, at school, at work, at the meeting, at a party, at 25 Green Road, at Savar* |
| **on** | a **surface**; a line (street, river, coast); public transport; floors; pages; electronic media | *on the table, on the wall, on the floor, on the road, on the bank of the Padma, on the coast, on the bus/train/plane/ship, on a bicycle, on the second floor, on page 10, on TV, on the radio, on the Internet, on the left/right* |
| **in** | an **enclosed space** or an **area**; big cities, countries, continents; cars and taxis; water, sky; books and newspapers | *in the room, in the box, in the garden, in Dhaka, in Bangladesh, in Asia, in a car, in a taxi, in the sea, in the sky, in the newspaper, in bed, in hospital, in prison, in the picture, in a queue, in a row* |

::: ex
- He lives **at** Mohammadpur **in** Dhaka. (small locality → *at*; large city → *in*)
- She is **at** school. (as a student) / She is **in** the school building.
- The picture is **on** the wall. / The nail is **in** the wall.
- He is **in** the car. / He is **on** the bus. (✘ in the bus — common in Bangladesh, but standard English says *on the bus*)
- Sit **at** the table (for a meal/work) / Put it **on** the table.
- I read it **in** the newspaper (✘ on the newspaper) / I saw it **on** the Internet.
:::

### 17.5.2 Other Prepositions of Place

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **above** | higher than (not directly, no contact) | *The plane flew **above** the clouds. His rank is **above** mine.* |
| **over** | directly above / covering / across | *He held an umbrella **over** her head. Spread a sheet **over** the bed. a bridge **over** the river* |
| **below** | lower than (not directly) | *The temperature is **below** zero. Write your name **below** the line.* |
| **under** | directly beneath / covered by / less than | *The cat is **under** the table. children **under** five* |
| **beneath** | under (formal, literary) | ***beneath** the surface; **beneath** his dignity* |
| **underneath** | directly under, hidden | *a note **underneath** the door* |
| **beside** | by the side of, next to | *Sit **beside** me.* |
| **besides** | in addition to | ***Besides** English, he speaks French.* |
| **between** | two persons/things (or more, considered individually) | *Divide it **between** you and me. a treaty **between** three nations* |
| **among** | more than two, as a group | *Divide the sweets **among** the children.* |
| **behind** | at the back of | *The garden is **behind** the house.* |
| **in front of** | facing, ahead of | *There is a tree **in front of** our house.* |
| **opposite** | facing, on the other side | *The bank is **opposite** the post office.* |
| **near / near to / close to / next to / by** | at a short distance | *The school is **near** our house. Sit **by** me.* |
| **against** | touching and pressing; in opposition | *He leaned **against** the wall. We fought **against** injustice.* |
| **amid / amidst** | in the middle of (formal) | ***amid** cheers; **amid** the confusion* |
| **around / round** | surrounding, in a circle | *They sat **around** the fire. The earth moves **round** the sun.* |
| **beyond** | farther than, outside the limits of | *a village **beyond** the hills; **beyond** my control* |
| **within** | inside the limits of | ***within** the city; **within** reach* |
| **throughout** | in every part of | ***throughout** the country / the year* |

## 17.6 Prepositions of Direction and Movement

| Pair / Preposition | Difference | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **in / into** | *in* = position (no movement); *into* = movement from outside to inside | *He is **in** the room.* / *He went **into** the room.* (*jump into the river, fall into the trap*) |
| **on / onto** | *on* = position; *onto* = movement to a surface | *The cat is **on** the roof.* / *The cat jumped **onto** the roof.* |
| **to / towards** | *to* = destination reached; *towards* = in the direction of | *He went **to** Chattogram.* / *He walked **towards** the river.* |
| **at / to** | *at* = target, often aggressive; *to* = recipient | *He threw a stone **at** the dog* (to hit it) / *He threw the ball **to** me* (for me to catch). *Shout **at** someone* (anger) / *shout **to** someone* (to be heard). |
| **across** | from one side to the other (surface) | *He swam **across** the river. a bridge **across** the Jamuna* |
| **through** | from one end to the other (inside something) | *The train passed **through** a tunnel. walk **through** the forest* |
| **along** | following the length of | *We walked **along** the river bank.* |
| **up / down** | to a higher / lower place | *He climbed **up** the tree. She ran **down** the stairs.* |
| **out of** | from inside to outside | *He came **out of** the room.* |
| **off** | away from a surface/position | *He fell **off** the horse. Keep **off** the grass.* |
| **from** | starting point | *He came **from** Rajshahi.* |
| **past** | going by | *He walked **past** my house.* |

## 17.7 Prepositions of Means, Cause, Agent and Manner

### 17.7.1 *By* and *With*

- ***by*** = the **agent** (doer) in the passive, and the **means** of transport/communication: *The book was written **by** Tagore. He came **by** train. I sent it **by** post / **by** email.*
- ***with*** = the **instrument** (tool) used by an agent: *He killed the snake **with** a stick. She writes **with** a pen.*

::: ex
- The letter was written **by** Rahim **with** a pen.
- He was killed **with** a knife **by** a robber.
- The glass was broken **by** the ball. (the ball as a cause/agent — no human holding it)
:::

### 17.7.2 Transport: *By*, *On*, *In*

- ***by*** + means (no article): *by bus, by train, by car, by plane, by air, by sea, by rickshaw, by bicycle*.
- ***on*** + specific public transport / riding: *on the bus, on the 8 o'clock train, on a ship, on a bicycle, on horseback*; ***on foot*** (✘ by foot).
- ***in*** + private vehicle: *in my car, in a taxi, in a CNG*.

### 17.7.3 Cause: *Of*, *From*, *For*, *With*, *Through*

::: ex
- He died **of** cholera / **of** cancer / **of** hunger. (illness, direct cause)
- He died **from** a wound / **from** overwork / **from** an accident. (external or indirect cause)
- He was punished **for** stealing. (reason)
- She was trembling **with** fear / shivering **with** cold / mad **with** anger. (emotion or physical state)
- He failed **through** negligence.
:::

### 17.7.4 Material: *Made of / Made from / Made out of / Made with / Made into*

- ***made of*** — the material is still recognisable: *This table is **made of** wood.*
- ***made from*** — the material is changed in the process: *Paper is **made from** wood. Wine is **made from** grapes.*
- ***made out of*** — something unusual or re-used: *a lamp **made out of** a bottle*.
- ***made with*** — ingredients: *a cake **made with** eggs and flour*.
- ***made into*** — the product: *Wood is **made into** paper.*

## 17.8 Some Important Distinctions

| Pair | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **between / among** | two (or more seen individually) / more than two as a group | *between the two brothers; among the five brothers* |
| **beside / besides** | next to / in addition to | *He sat **beside** me. **Besides** money, he wants fame.* |
| **except / besides** | excluding / including also | *All came **except** Rahim (he did not come). **Besides** Rahim, three others came (he also came).* |
| **but** (= except) | after negatives and *all, every-, any-, no-* | *Nobody **but** him knew the answer. All **but** one passed.* |
| **like / as** | *like* + noun (similarity); *as* + noun (role, function) | *He worked **like** a slave* (but he was not a slave). *He worked **as** a clerk* (he was a clerk). |
| **due to / owing to / because of** | *due to* = adjective (after *be*); *owing to / because of* = adverbial (beginning or after a verb) | *His absence was **due to** illness.* / ***Owing to** illness, he was absent.* (✘ Due to illness, he was absent — traditional rule) |
| **of / off** | possession, part / away from | *a cup **of** tea* / *Get **off** the bus.* |
| **in / at** (arrival) | *arrive in* (city, country) / *arrive at* (building, small place) | *We arrived **in** Dhaka **at** the airport.* (✘ arrived to) |
| **above / over** | higher (no contact) / directly over, covering | *above sea level; a blanket over the bed* |
| **under / below** | directly beneath / lower level | *under the bed; below the knee; below average* |
| **across / through** | surface / inside a space | *across the road; through the jungle* |
| **since / for / from** | point / period / starting point | See 17.4.2 |
| **by / until** | deadline / continuing | See 17.4.4 |
| **in / within** | end of period / before the end | See 17.4.3 |

## 17.9 Verbs That Do Not Take a Preposition

Many Bangladeshi learners add a preposition after transitive verbs that take a **direct object**. In the following, **no preposition** is used:

| ✘ Wrong | ✔ Right |
|---|---|
| discuss about the matter | **discuss** the matter |
| enter into the room | **enter** the room (*enter into* an agreement ✔) |
| reach at the station | **reach** the station (*arrive at* ✔) |
| resemble with his father | **resemble** his father |
| attack on the enemy | **attack** the enemy (*make an attack on* ✔) |
| marry with her | **marry** her (*get married to* her ✔) |
| approach to the manager | **approach** the manager |
| accompany with me | **accompany** me |
| comprise of five parts | **comprise** five parts (*consist of / be composed of* ✔) |
| emphasise on the point | **emphasise** the point (*lay emphasis on* ✔) |
| stress on | **stress** the point |
| request for help | **request** help (*make a request for* ✔) |
| order for books | **order** books (*place an order for* ✔) |
| answer to the question | **answer** the question (*the answer to* ✔) |
| obey to the rules | **obey** the rules |
| join with the party | **join** the party |
| lack in courage | **lack** courage (*be lacking in* courage ✔) |
| investigate into the case | **investigate** the case (*inquire into* ✔) |
| return back | **return** |
| describe about | **describe** |
| demand for | **demand** (*a demand for* ✔) |
| mention about | **mention** |
| order him to | ✔ correct |
| tell to him | **tell** him |
| affect on | **affect** (*have an effect on* ✔) |

::: tip
**Exam Tip — Noun takes the preposition, verb does not:** *discuss* (v) but *a discussion **about/on*** (n); *attack* (v) but *an attack **on***; *request* (v) but *a request **for***; *emphasise* (v) but *emphasis **on***; *answer* (v) but *the answer **to***; *demand* (v) but *a demand **for***; *reply* (v) **to** and *a reply **to*** (both take *to*).
:::

## 17.10 Words That Can Be Prepositions, Adverbs or Conjunctions

A preposition **must have an object**. If the same word has no object, it is an **adverb**; if it introduces a clause, it is a **conjunction** (Chapter 01.4.3).

::: ex
- He stood **before** the gate. (preposition)
- I have seen him **before**. (adverb)
- Come **before** it gets dark. (conjunction)
- The ball rolled **down** the hill. (preposition) / Sit **down**. (adverb)
- He has been ill **since** Monday. (preposition) / **Since** you insist, I'll go. (conjunction)
:::

## 17.11 Common Errors with Prepositions

::: errs
1. ✘ He was born in 16 December. ✔ He was born **on** 16 December.
2. ✘ I will meet you on next Monday. ✔ I will meet you **next Monday**.
3. ✘ He has been absent from Sunday. ✔ He has been absent **since** Sunday.
4. ✘ I have been waiting since an hour. ✔ … **for** an hour.
5. ✘ We discussed about the problem. ✔ We **discussed** the problem.
6. ✘ He entered into the room. ✔ He **entered** the room.
7. ✘ She resembles with her mother. ✔ She **resembles** her mother.
8. ✘ He reached at Dhaka. ✔ He **reached** Dhaka / **arrived in** Dhaka.
9. ✘ Divide the mangoes between the four boys. ✔ … **among** the four boys.
10. ✘ He died from cholera. ✔ He died **of** cholera.
11. ✘ He is angry upon me. ✔ He is angry **with** me. (*angry at* a thing)
12. ✘ He killed the bird by a stone. ✔ He killed the bird **with** a stone.
13. ✘ He came by foot. ✔ He came **on** foot.
14. ✘ He jumped in the river. (movement) ✔ He jumped **into** the river.
15. ✘ The table is made from wood. ✔ The table is made **of** wood.
16. ✘ Besides Rahim, no one came. ✔ **Except** Rahim / **But** Rahim, no one came.
17. ✘ He sat besides me. ✔ He sat **beside** me.
18. ✘ Due to rain, the match was cancelled. (traditional) ✔ **Owing to** rain, … / The cancellation was **due to** rain.
19. ✘ Wait here by I return. ✔ Wait here **till/until** I return.
20. ✘ Finish it till Friday. ✔ Finish it **by** Friday.
21. ✘ He works like a manager in this company. (if he is the manager) ✔ He works **as** a manager.
22. ✘ I prefer tea than coffee. ✔ I prefer tea **to** coffee.
23. ✘ He is married with a doctor. ✔ He is married **to** a doctor.
24. ✘ Where are you going to? ✔ Where are you going?
25. ✘ She is good in English. ✔ She is good **at** English.
26. ✘ I am senior than him. ✔ I am senior **to** him.
27. ✘ He comprises of good qualities. ✔ He **has** many good qualities. / The book **comprises** ten chapters.
28. ✘ I congratulate you for your success. ✔ I congratulate you **on** your success.
29. ✘ He insisted to go. ✔ He insisted **on going**.
30. ✘ We arrived to Dhaka at night. ✔ We arrived **in** Dhaka at night.
:::

## 17.12 Prepositions in the SAT, GRE and GMAT

::: gre
Prepositions are the backbone of **idioms**, a major category in GMAT Sentence Correction and SAT Writing. Some high-frequency idioms (more in Chapter 18):

- *differ **from*** (be unlike) / *differ **with*** (disagree with a person)
- *distinguish **between** X **and** Y* / *distinguish X **from** Y*
- *a debate / dispute **over*** (a topic); *a debate **between** X **and** Y*
- *responsible **for***; *contribute **to***; *consist **of***; *derive **from***; *depend **on***
- *the effect **of** X **on** Y*; *an impact **on***; *an increase **in*** (amount) vs *an increase **of*** (size: *an increase of 10%*)
- *in contrast **to/with***; *as opposed **to***; *compared **with/to***
- *regard X **as** Y*; *view X **as** Y*; *think of X **as** Y*
- *between X **and** Y* (✘ between X **to** Y); *from X **to** Y* (✘ from X **and** Y)
- ***Between* + two objects joined by *and***: ✘ *between each house* ✔ *between **the houses** / between **each house and the next***.
:::
