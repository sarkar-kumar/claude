## 52.3 The Master List: M – Z

::: vocab
**Marshal / Martial** — *marshal* (n) [সেনাপতি]{.bn}; (v) [সংগঠিত করা]{.bn}; *martial* (adj) [যুদ্ধ-সংক্রান্ত, সামরিক]{.bn}. *Ex:* Field **Marshal** / **martial** law / **martial** arts.

**Masterful / Masterly** — *masterful* (adj) [কর্তৃত্বপরায়ণ]{.bn} = domineering; *masterly* (adj) [দক্ষতাপূর্ণ]{.bn} = very skilful. *Ex:* a **masterly** performance.

**Meter / Metre** — *meter* (n) [পরিমাপক যন্ত্র]{.bn}; *metre* (n, BrE) [মিটার (দৈর্ঘ্যের একক); ছন্দ]{.bn}. *Ex:* an electricity **meter** / a 100-**metre** race.

**Momentary / Momentous** — *momentary* (adj) [ক্ষণস্থায়ী]{.bn}; *momentous* (adj) [অতি গুরুত্বপূর্ণ]{.bn}. *Ex:* a **momentary** pause / a **momentous** decision.

**Moral / Morale** — *moral* (adj/n) [নৈতিক; নীতিকথা]{.bn}; *morale* (n) [মনোবল]{.bn}. *Ex:* the **moral** of the story / The victory boosted the team's **morale**.

**Naughty / Knotty** — *naughty* (adj) [দুষ্টু]{.bn}; *knotty* (adj) [জটিল]{.bn}. *Ex:* a **naughty** boy / a **knotty** problem.

**Notable / Notorious** — *notable* (adj, +) [উল্লেখযোগ্য]{.bn}; *notorious* (adj, −) [কুখ্যাত]{.bn}. *Ex:* a **notable** achievement / a **notorious** criminal.

**Observance / Observation** — *observance* (n) [পালন (আচার/নিয়ম)]{.bn}; *observation* (n) [পর্যবেক্ষণ]{.bn}. *Ex:* the **observance** of Ramadan / scientific **observation**.

**Official / Officious** — *official* (adj) [সরকারি, দাপ্তরিক]{.bn}; *officious* (adj) [অযাচিত খবরদারি করে এমন]{.bn}. *Ex:* an **official** letter / an **officious** clerk.

**Ordinance / Ordnance** — *ordinance* (n) [অধ্যাদেশ]{.bn}; *ordnance* (n) [গোলাবারুদ, সামরিক রসদ]{.bn}. *Ex:* The President issued an **ordinance**. / an **ordnance** factory.

**Passed / Past** — *passed* (V2/V3 of *pass*); *past* (n/adj/prep) [অতীত; পেরিয়ে]{.bn}. *Ex:* He **passed** the exam. / Forget the **past**. / He walked **past** me.

**Peace / Piece** — *peace* (n) [শান্তি]{.bn}; *piece* (n) [টুকরো]{.bn}.

**Persecute / Prosecute** — *persecute* (v) [নিপীড়ন করা]{.bn}; *prosecute* (v) [মামলা করা]{.bn}. *Ex:* Minorities were **persecuted**. / Trespassers will be **prosecuted**.

**Personal / Personnel** — *personal* (adj) [ব্যক্তিগত]{.bn}; *personnel* (n) [কর্মীবৃন্দ]{.bn}. *Ex:* a **personal** matter / military **personnel**.

**Perspective / Prospective** — *perspective* (n) [দৃষ্টিকোণ]{.bn}; *prospective* (adj) [ভাবী, সম্ভাব্য]{.bn}. *Ex:* from a new **perspective** / **prospective** students.

**Physic / Physique / Physics** — *physic* (n, old) [ওষুধ]{.bn}; *physique* (n) [দেহগঠন]{.bn}; *physics* (n) [পদার্থবিজ্ঞান]{.bn}. *Ex:* a strong **physique**.

**Plain / Plane** — *plain* (adj/n) [সরল; সমতলভূমি]{.bn}; *plane* (n) [বিমান; সমতল]{.bn}.

**Popular / Populous** — *popular* (adj) [জনপ্রিয়]{.bn}; *populous* (adj) [জনবহুল]{.bn}. *Ex:* a **popular** singer / Dhaka is a **populous** city.

**Practicable / Practical** — *practicable* (adj) [বাস্তবায়নযোগ্য]{.bn} = can be done; *practical* (adj) [ব্যবহারিক; বাস্তবমুখী]{.bn} = useful, sensible. *Ex:* Is the plan **practicable**? / She is a **practical** person.

**Practice / Practise** — *practice* (n) [অনুশীলন]{.bn}; *practise* (v, BrE) [অনুশীলন করা]{.bn}. *Ex:* **Practice** makes perfect. / **Practise** daily.

**Pray / Prey** — *pray* (v) [প্রার্থনা করা]{.bn}; *prey* (n) [শিকার]{.bn}. *Ex:* We **pray** five times a day. / The deer fell **prey** to the tiger.

**Precede / Proceed** — *precede* (v) [পূর্বে আসা]{.bn}; *proceed* (v) [অগ্রসর হওয়া]{.bn}. *Ex:* A storm **preceded** the flood. / **Proceed** with your work.

**Prescribe / Proscribe** — *prescribe* (v) [ব্যবস্থাপত্র দেওয়া; নির্ধারণ করা]{.bn}; *proscribe* (v) [নিষিদ্ধ করা]{.bn}. *Ex:* The doctor **prescribed** medicine. / The party was **proscribed**.

**Principal / Principle** — *principal* (n/adj) [অধ্যক্ষ; প্রধান]{.bn}; *principle* (n) [নীতি]{.bn}. *Ex:* the **principal** of the college / a man of **principle**.

**Prophecy / Prophesy** — *prophecy* (n) [ভবিষ্যদ্বাণী]{.bn}; *prophesy* (v) [ভবিষ্যদ্বাণী করা]{.bn}.

**Quiet / Quite / Quit** — *quiet* (adj) [শান্ত]{.bn}; *quite* (adv) [বেশ; সম্পূর্ণ]{.bn}; *quit* (v) [ছেড়ে দেওয়া]{.bn}. *Ex:* Keep **quiet**. / It is **quite** good. / He **quit** his job.

**Raise / Rise** — *raise* (transitive) [তোলা]{.bn}; *rise* (intransitive) [ওঠা]{.bn} — Chapter 11.

**Respectable / Respectful / Respective** — *respectable* [সম্মানিত]{.bn}; *respectful* [শ্রদ্ধাশীল]{.bn}; *respective* [নিজ নিজ]{.bn}. *Ex:* a **respectable** family / **respectful** to elders / They went to their **respective** homes.

**Rob / Steal** — *rob* (a person/place) [ডাকাতি করা]{.bn}; *steal* (a thing) [চুরি করা]{.bn}. *Ex:* They **robbed** the bank. / They **stole** money.

**Role / Roll** — *role* (n) [ভূমিকা]{.bn}; *roll* (n/v) [তালিকা; গড়ানো]{.bn}. *Ex:* He played a key **role**. / the **roll** call.

**Sensible / Sensitive / Sensual** — *sensible* [বিবেচক]{.bn}; *sensitive* [সংবেদনশীল]{.bn}; *sensual* [ইন্দ্রিয়সুখ-সম্পর্কিত]{.bn}. *Ex:* a **sensible** answer / **sensitive** skin.

**Sociable / Social** — *sociable* (adj) [মিশুক]{.bn}; *social* (adj) [সামাজিক]{.bn}. *Ex:* a **sociable** person / **social** problems.

**Stationary / Stationery** — *stationary* (adj) [স্থির]{.bn}; *stationery* (n) [লেখার সামগ্রী]{.bn}. *Ex:* a **stationary** car / a **stationery** shop.

**Statue / Stature / Statute** — *statue* (n) [মূর্তি]{.bn}; *stature* (n) [উচ্চতা; মর্যাদা]{.bn}; *statute* (n) [সংবিধি, আইন]{.bn}. *Ex:* a bronze **statue** / a man of great **stature** / a **statute** passed by Parliament.

**Storey / Story** — *storey* (n, BrE) [তলা]{.bn}; *story* (n) [গল্প]{.bn}. *Ex:* a ten-**storey** building / a short **story**.

**Suit / Suite** — *suit* (n) [পোশাক; মামলা]{.bn}; *suite* (n) [কক্ষসমষ্টি; একসেট আসবাব]{.bn}. *Ex:* a new **suit** / a hotel **suite**.

**Than / Then** — *than* (conj) [চেয়ে]{.bn}; *then* (adv) [তখন; তারপর]{.bn}. *Ex:* taller **than** me / We ate, **then** left.

## 52.4 Groups of Near-Synonyms with Different Uses

These words have similar meanings but are used in different contexts. They are tested in BCS and GRE sentence completion.

| Words | Distinction | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **say / tell / speak / talk** | *say* something (to someone); *tell* someone something; *speak* a language / formally; *talk* = converse | *He **said** that … / He **told** me … / He **speaks** English. / We **talked** for hours.* |
| **see / look / watch** | *see* = perceive; *look* = direct the eyes; *watch* = observe something moving | *I **saw** a bird. **Look** at me. **Watch** the match.* |
| **hear / listen** | *hear* = perceive sound; *listen* = pay attention | *I **heard** a noise. **Listen** to the teacher.* |
| **bring / take / fetch** | *bring* = towards the speaker; *take* = away; *fetch* = go and bring back | ***Bring** it here. **Take** it there. **Fetch** some water.* |
| **beat / win / defeat** | *win* a match/prize; *beat / defeat* an opponent | *We **won** the match. We **beat** India.* (✘ won India) |
| **job / work / occupation / profession / vocation** | *work* (uncountable activity); *job* (a paid position); *profession* (needs special training); *vocation* (a calling) | *Medicine is a noble **profession**.* |
| **customer / client / guest / patient** | shop / professional service / hotel / doctor | *a lawyer's **client**, a doctor's **patient*** |
| **journey / trip / voyage / tour / travel** | *journey* (going from one place to another); *trip* (short, return); *voyage* (by sea/space); *tour* (visiting many places); *travel* (general activity, uncountable) | *a sea **voyage**; a world **tour**; **Travel** broadens the mind.* |
| **remember / remind** | *remember* = keep in memory; *remind* = make someone remember | *I **remember** him. **Remind** me to call him.* |
| **rob / steal / burgle** | *rob* a person/place (often with force); *steal* a thing; *burgle* a house | |
| **fault / mistake / error / blunder** | *fault* (responsibility; defect); *mistake* (wrong act); *error* (formal, in calculation/judgement); *blunder* (serious, careless mistake) | |
| **economic / financial / fiscal / monetary** | *economic* (of the economy); *financial* (of money/finance); *fiscal* (of government revenue, taxes); *monetary* (of money supply, currency) | ***fiscal** policy (budget, tax) vs **monetary** policy (central bank)* |
| **affect / afflict / infect / inflict** | *affect* (influence); *afflict* (cause suffering); *infect* (spread disease); *inflict* (impose something painful) | *The disease **afflicted** thousands. The judge **inflicted** a heavy penalty.* |
| **wound / injury / hurt / harm** | *wound* (by a weapon); *injury* (by an accident); *hurt* (pain, physical or emotional); *harm* (damage in general) | |

## 52.5 Words Often Misused by Bangladeshi Learners

::: errs
1. ✘ He is my cousin brother. ✔ He is my **cousin**.
2. ✘ Can I lend your pen? ✔ Can I **borrow** your pen? / Can you **lend** me your pen?
3. ✘ I am going to give the exam. ✔ I am going to **take / sit** the exam.
4. ✘ He told that he was ill. ✔ He **said** that he was ill.
5. ✘ We won Pakistan in the match. ✔ We **beat / defeated** Pakistan.
6. ✘ The price of rice has raised. ✔ … has **risen**.
7. ✘ Please discuss about it. ✔ Please **discuss** it.
8. ✘ He stole the bank. ✔ He **robbed** the bank.
9. ✘ I have learnt him English. ✔ I have **taught** him English.
10. ✘ Open the light / close the fan. ✔ **Switch on** the light / **switch off** the fan.
11. ✘ He is a very much good man. ✔ He is a **very** good man.
12. ✘ She gave a good advice. ✔ She gave **good advice**.
13. ✘ The principle of the college ✔ The **principal** of the college
14. ✘ He is an eminent danger. ✔ He is an **imminent** danger.
15. ✘ The heavy rain effected our plan. ✔ The heavy rain **affected** our plan.
:::

## 52.6 Confusable Words in the SAT, GRE and GMAT

::: gre
- **SAT Writing** includes a dedicated question type on **frequently confused words**: *affect/effect, accept/except, than/then, its/it's, their/there/they're, principal/principle, complement/compliment, allude/elude, elicit/illicit, cite/site/sight, council/counsel, eminent/imminent*.
- **GRE Text Completion and Sentence Equivalence** test fine shades of meaning among near-synonyms: *venal/venial, ingenious/ingenuous, tortuous/torturous, credulous/credible, imply/infer, disinterested/uninterested, flaunt/flout, prescribe/proscribe, deprecate/depreciate, complacent/complaisant*.
- **GMAT idioms:** *economic* vs *economical*, *fewer* vs *less*, *number* vs *amount*, *between* vs *among*, *imply* vs *infer*, *affect* vs *effect*.
:::
