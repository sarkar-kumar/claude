## 47.1 What Is a Phrasal (Group) Verb?

A **phrasal verb** (or **group verb**) is a **verb + one or two particles** (adverb and/or preposition) that together have a meaning **different from** the meanings of the separate words.

::: ex
- *give* + *up* = **give up** (stop, abandon): *He gave up smoking.*
- *look* + *after* = **look after** (take care of): *She looks after her parents.*
- *put* + *up* + *with* = **put up with** (tolerate): *I can't put up with this noise.*
:::

Phrasal verbs are a **core item** in BCS Preliminary and Written, Bank (officer, AD, cash), university admission tests and IELTS/TOEFL, usually as "*The phrase 'call off' means…*" or as fill-in-the-blank items (*The meeting was put ___ till Monday*). The GRE and SAT also use phrasal verbs inside reading passages, where knowing the idiomatic meaning is essential.

### 47.1.1 Types of Phrasal Verbs

| Type | Explanation | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Intransitive** (no object) | verb + particle only | *The plane* **took off**. *The car* **broke down**. |
| **Transitive separable** | object can come between verb and particle; a **pronoun object must** come between | *Put off the meeting / Put the meeting off / Put* **it** *off* (✘ put off it) |
| **Transitive inseparable** | object always after the particle | **Look after** *the child / Look after* **him** (✘ look him after) |
| **Three-word (phrasal-prepositional)** | verb + adverb + preposition; inseparable | **put up with**, **look forward to**, **run out of**, **get along with**, **come up with**, **do away with** |

::: tip
**How to study phrasal verbs:** Learn them **by base verb** (all *break*, all *bring*, all *call*…), always with an example sentence, and note whether they are separable. Many have **more than one meaning** — the example makes the meaning stick. The Bangla meaning is given to help quick recall.
:::

**Give out** [বিতরণ করা; ফুরিয়ে যাওয়া]{.bn} — distribute; be exhausted. *His strength gave out.*

**Give up** [ত্যাগ করা]{.bn} — abandon, stop. *He gave up smoking. / Never give up hope.*

**Give way** [ভেঙে পড়া; জায়গা ছেড়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — collapse; yield. *The bridge gave way. / Give way to the ambulance.*

**Go after** [পিছু নেওয়া; চেষ্টা করা]{.bn} — chase; try to get. *He went after the thief.*

**Go against** [বিরুদ্ধে যাওয়া]{.bn} — oppose; be unfavourable. *The verdict went against him.*

**Go ahead** [এগিয়ে যাওয়া]{.bn} — proceed. *Go ahead with your plan.*

**Go by** [অতিবাহিত হওয়া; অনুসরণ করা]{.bn} — pass (time); follow (rules). *Years went by. / Go by the rules.*

**Go down** [ডুবে যাওয়া; কমা]{.bn} — sink; decrease; be remembered. *The ship went down. / He will go down in history.*

**Go for** [আক্রমণ করা; বেছে নেওয়া]{.bn} — attack; choose. *I'll go for the blue one.*

**Go in for** [অংশ নেওয়া; শখ হিসেবে নেওয়া]{.bn} — take part in; adopt as a hobby or career. *He went in for law.*

**Go off** [বিস্ফোরিত হওয়া; নষ্ট হওয়া; বাজা]{.bn} — explode; go bad; ring (alarm). *The bomb went off. / The milk has gone off. / The alarm went off.*

**Go on** [চালিয়ে যাওয়া; ঘটা]{.bn} — continue; happen. *Go on with your work. / What is going on here?*

**Go out** [নিভে যাওয়া]{.bn} — stop burning. *The light went out.*

**Go over** [খুঁটিয়ে দেখা]{.bn} — examine; revise. *Go over your answers before submitting.*

**Go through** [ভোগ করা; পরীক্ষা করা]{.bn} — suffer; examine carefully. *She went through a lot of hardship.*

**Go up** [বৃদ্ধি পাওয়া]{.bn} — rise. *Prices have gone up.*

**Go without** [ছাড়া থাকা]{.bn} — manage without. *Many people go without food.*

**Grow up** [বড় হওয়া]{.bn} — become an adult. *He grew up in a village.*
