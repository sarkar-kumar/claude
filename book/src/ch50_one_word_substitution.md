## 50.1 What Is One Word Substitution?

**One word substitution** means replacing a phrase or a clause with **a single word** that carries the same meaning: *a person who cannot read or write* → **illiterate**; *the killing of one's father* → **patricide**. It makes writing concise and is tested in every BCS Preliminary, Bank and admission paper ("*One who does not believe in God* is called —"). In the GRE and SAT, these precise words appear as answer choices in Text Completion and Sentence Equivalence.

The lists below are grouped by **theme** so that related words support one another in memory. Bangla meanings are given for quick recall.

## 50.9 Speech, Writing and Language

::: list
**Soliloquy** [স্বগতোক্তি]{.bn} — speaking one's thoughts aloud alone (especially in a play). **Monologue** [একক বক্তৃতা]{.bn} — a long speech by one person. **Dialogue** [সংলাপ]{.bn} — conversation between two or more.

**Eulogy / Panegyric / Encomium** [প্রশংসাসূচক বক্তৃতা]{.bn} — a speech or writing in praise of someone.

**Elegy** [শোকগাথা]{.bn} — a poem of mourning for the dead. **Epitaph** [সমাধিলিপি]{.bn} — inscription on a tomb. **Obituary** [মৃত্যু-সংবাদ]{.bn} — notice of death with a short biography.

**Autobiography** [আত্মজীবনী]{.bn} — life story written by oneself. **Biography** [জীবনী]{.bn} — written by another.

**Anonymous** [বেনামি]{.bn} — of unknown name/authorship. **Pseudonym / Pen-name** [ছদ্মনাম]{.bn} — a false name used by a writer.

**Manuscript** [পাণ্ডুলিপি]{.bn} — a handwritten or unpublished work.

**Illegible** [অস্পষ্ট, পড়া যায় না এমন]{.bn} — that cannot be read. **Legible** [পাঠযোগ্য]{.bn}. **Inaudible** [অশ্রাব্য]{.bn} — that cannot be heard. **Invisible** [অদৃশ্য]{.bn}. **Ineffable / Inexpressible** [অনির্বচনীয়]{.bn} — too great to be expressed in words. **Unutterable**.

**Verbose / Prolix** [শব্দবহুল]{.bn} — using too many words. **Laconic / Terse** [সংক্ষিপ্ত]{.bn} — using very few words.

**Plagiarism** [কুম্ভিলকবৃত্তি]{.bn} — literary theft.

**Anthology** [সংকলন]{.bn} — a collection of poems or pieces. **Bibliography** [গ্রন্থপঞ্জি]{.bn} — a list of books. **Glossary** [শব্দকোষ]{.bn} — a list of difficult words with meanings. **Thesaurus** [সমার্থকোষ]{.bn} — a dictionary of synonyms. **Lexicon** [অভিধান]{.bn}. **Encyclopaedia** [বিশ্বকোষ]{.bn}. **Almanac** [পঞ্জিকা]{.bn}. **Itinerary** [ভ্রমণসূচি]{.bn} — a plan of a journey.

**Preface / Foreword / Prologue** [ভূমিকা / মুখবন্ধ]{.bn}; **Epilogue** [উপসংহার]{.bn} — concluding part of a play or book.

**Vernacular** [দেশীয় ভাষা]{.bn} — the native language of a region. **Dialect** [উপভাষা]{.bn}. **Lingua franca** [সংযোগ ভাষা]{.bn} — a common language used between speakers of different languages. **Jargon** [পরিভাষা]{.bn} — technical language of a profession. **Slang** [অপভাষা]{.bn}.

**Tautology** [পুনরুক্তি]{.bn} — needless repetition. **Euphemism** [সুভাষণ]{.bn} — a mild word for a harsh one (*pass away* for *die*). **Paradox** [আপাতবিরোধী উক্তি]{.bn}. **Oxymoron** [বিরোধাভাস]{.bn}. **Palindrome** [উল্টো পড়লেও একই শব্দ]{.bn} — a word/sentence that reads the same backwards (*madam, level*).

**Onomatopoeia** [ধ্বন্যাত্মক শব্দ]{.bn} — a word that imitates a sound (*buzz, hiss*).

**SAT / GRE / GMAT Corner — Precision Words:** Many "one-word substitutes" are core GRE vocabulary: *laconic, loquacious, garrulous, taciturn, reticent* (speech); *sycophant, misanthrope, philanthropist, ascetic, hedonist, stoic, cynic, sceptic* (character); *ineffable, indelible, inexorable, inevitable, irrevocable, impeccable, infallible* (the *in-/im-/ir-* family); *pandemic, endemic, epidemic* (scope). In Sentence Equivalence, look for **two** such words that fit the blank and mean the same: *reticent / taciturn*, *loquacious / garrulous*, *ephemeral / transient*.
