## 48.3 The Master List of Idioms and Phrases (L–Z)

::: letter
L
:::

::: list
**Lame duck** [অকেজো ব্যক্তি/প্রতিষ্ঠান]{.bn} — a weak or ineffective person or organisation; an official in the final period of office. *The state-owned company has become a lame duck.*

**Lead a dog's life** [দুর্দশাগ্রস্ত জীবন কাটানো]{.bn} — live a miserable life. *The refugees lead a dog's life.*

**Leave no stone unturned** [সর্বাত্মক চেষ্টা করা]{.bn} — try every possible means. *The police left no stone unturned to find the killer.*

**Let bygones be bygones** [অতীত ভুলে যাওয়া]{.bn} — forget past quarrels. *Let bygones be bygones and be friends again.*

**Let the cat out of the bag** [গোপন কথা ফাঁস করা]{.bn} — reveal a secret. *He let the cat out of the bag about the surprise party.*

**Lion's share** [সিংহভাগ]{.bn} — the largest part. *The eldest son got the lion's share of the property.*

**Live in a fool's paradise** [বোকার স্বর্গে বাস করা]{.bn} — be happy because of false beliefs. *If you think you will pass without studying, you are living in a fool's paradise.*

**Lock, stock and barrel** [সম্পূর্ণভাবে]{.bn} — completely. *He sold his business lock, stock and barrel.*

**Look before you leap** [ভেবেচিন্তে কাজ করা]{.bn} — think carefully before acting. *Look before you leap into any business.*

**Lose heart** [হতাশ হওয়া]{.bn} — become discouraged. *Don't lose heart; try again.*

**Lose one's head** [মাথা খারাপ হওয়া]{.bn} — panic. *He lost his head when he saw the fire.*

**Lose the thread** [খেই হারানো]{.bn} — forget what one was saying. *The speaker lost the thread of his speech.*
:::

::: letter
M
:::

::: list
**Maiden speech** [প্রথম বক্তৃতা]{.bn} — first speech (especially in Parliament). *The new MP gave his maiden speech.*

**Make a clean breast of** [সব স্বীকার করা]{.bn} — confess fully. *He made a clean breast of his crime.*

**Make both ends meet** [আয়ে-ব্যয়ে কুলানো]{.bn} — live within one's income. *With rising prices, it is hard to make both ends meet.*

**Make hay while the sun shines** [সুযোগের সদ্ব্যবহার করা]{.bn} — take advantage of a good opportunity. *You are young; make hay while the sun shines.*

**Make light of** [গুরুত্ব না দেওয়া]{.bn} — treat as unimportant. *He made light of his illness.*

**Make a mountain of a molehill** [তিলকে তাল করা]{.bn} — exaggerate a minor problem. *Don't make a mountain of a molehill.*

**Man in the street** [সাধারণ মানুষ]{.bn} — an ordinary person. *The man in the street suffers most from inflation.*

**Mare's nest** [অলীক আবিষ্কার]{.bn} — a false discovery; a confused situation. *His great discovery proved a mare's nest.*

**Midsummer madness** [চরম পাগলামি]{.bn} — extreme folly. *Quitting a secure job now is midsummer madness.*

**Milk of human kindness** [মানবিক দয়া]{.bn} — compassion (Shakespeare, *Macbeth*). *He is full of the milk of human kindness.*

**Move heaven and earth** [সর্বাত্মক চেষ্টা করা]{.bn} — do everything possible. *She moved heaven and earth to save her son.*
:::

::: letter
N · O
:::

::: list
**Neck and neck** [সমানে সমান]{.bn} — level in a race or competition. *The two horses ran neck and neck.*

**Nip in the bud** [অঙ্কুরে বিনাশ করা]{.bn} — stop something at an early stage. *The revolt was nipped in the bud.*

**Null and void** [বাতিল, অকার্যকর]{.bn} — having no legal force. *The contract was declared null and void.*

**Odds and ends** [টুকিটাকি জিনিস]{.bn} — small miscellaneous items. *The drawer was full of odds and ends.*

**Of no avail** [নিষ্ফল]{.bn} — useless. *His efforts were of no avail.*

**Off and on / On and off** [মাঝে মাঝে]{.bn} — occasionally. *He visits us off and on.*

**Off the record** [অনানুষ্ঠানিকভাবে]{.bn} — unofficially. *The minister spoke off the record.*

**Once in a blue moon** [কালেভদ্রে]{.bn} — very rarely. *He visits his village once in a blue moon.*

**On the horns of a dilemma** [উভয় সংকটে]{.bn} — faced with two unpleasant choices. *I am on the horns of a dilemma.*

**On the wane** [ক্ষয়িষ্ণু]{.bn} — declining. *His popularity is on the wane.*

**Out and out** [পুরোপুরি]{.bn} — thorough, complete. *He is an out-and-out liar.*

**Out of sorts** [অসুস্থ বা মনমরা]{.bn} — unwell or unhappy. *I am feeling out of sorts today.*

**Out of the wood(s)** [বিপদমুক্ত]{.bn} — out of danger. *The patient is not yet out of the woods.*

**Over head and ears** [আপাদমস্তক]{.bn} — completely (usually in debt or love). *He is over head and ears in debt.*
:::

P · Q
:::

::: list
**Pay through the nose** [অতিরিক্ত দাম দেওয়া]{.bn} — pay too much. *We paid through the nose for the tickets.*

**Pell-mell** [বিশৃঙ্খলভাবে]{.bn} — in a confused rush. *The crowd ran pell-mell.*

**Pick holes in** [খুঁত ধরা]{.bn} — find faults. *He always picks holes in my work.*

**Pin money** [হাতখরচ]{.bn} — a small allowance for personal spending. *She earns pin money by tutoring.*

**Play second fiddle** [দ্বিতীয় স্থান মেনে নেওয়া]{.bn} — take a subordinate role. *He refused to play second fiddle to his junior.*

**Play truant** [স্কুল পালানো]{.bn} — stay away from school without permission. *The boy often plays truant.*

**Pros and cons** [ভালো-মন্দ দিক]{.bn} — advantages and disadvantages. *Consider the pros and cons before deciding.*

**Pull one's leg** [ঠাট্টা করা]{.bn} — tease. *Don't worry, I'm only pulling your leg.*

**Put the cart before the horse** [উল্টো কাজ করা]{.bn} — do things in the wrong order. *Buying the furniture before the house is putting the cart before the horse.*

**Pyrrhic victory** [অত্যধিক মূল্যে অর্জিত জয়]{.bn} — a victory won at too great a cost. *Winning the case after spending everything was a Pyrrhic victory.*

**Queer fish** [অদ্ভুত লোক]{.bn} — a strange person. *Our new neighbour is a queer fish.*
:::

::: list
**Rain cats and dogs** [মুষলধারে বৃষ্টি হওয়া]{.bn} — rain very heavily. *It was raining cats and dogs.*

**Rank and file** [সাধারণ সদস্যরা]{.bn} — ordinary members. *The rank and file of the party opposed the decision.*

**Read between the lines** [গূঢ় অর্থ বোঝা]{.bn} — understand the hidden meaning. *Read between the lines of his letter.*

**Red tape** [আমলাতান্ত্রিক জটিলতা, লালফিতা]{.bn} — excessive official formality. *Red tape delays development work.*

**Rest on one's laurels** [অতীত সাফল্যে সন্তুষ্ট থাকা]{.bn} — stop trying after success. *Don't rest on your laurels.*

**Rise to the occasion** [পরিস্থিতির মোকাবিলায় সক্ষম হওয়া]{.bn} — handle a difficult situation well. *The young bowler rose to the occasion.*

**Rob Peter to pay Paul** [এক জনের ধন কেড়ে আরেক জনকে দেওয়া]{.bn} — take from one to pay another. *Borrowing to repay a loan is robbing Peter to pay Paul.*

**Rule the roost** [কর্তৃত্ব করা]{.bn} — be in charge. *In this house, Grandmother rules the roost.*

**Run riot** [উচ্ছৃঙ্খল হওয়া]{.bn} — behave wildly. *The fans ran riot after the match.*
:::

**Sail in the same boat** [একই অবস্থায় থাকা]{.bn} — be in the same difficult situation. *We are all sailing in the same boat.*

**See eye to eye** [একমত হওয়া]{.bn} — agree. *We don't see eye to eye on politics.*

**Set the Thames on fire** [অসাধারণ কিছু করা]{.bn} — do something remarkable (usually negative). *He will never set the Thames on fire.*

**Show a clean pair of heels** [পালিয়ে যাওয়া]{.bn} — run away. *The thief showed a clean pair of heels.*

**Show the white feather** [ভীরুতা দেখানো]{.bn} — show cowardice. *A soldier must never show the white feather.*

**Sitting on the fence** [নিরপেক্ষ থাকা; সিদ্ধান্তহীন]{.bn} — not taking sides. *He is sitting on the fence on this issue.*

**Smell a rat** [ষড়যন্ত্রের আভাস পাওয়া]{.bn} — suspect something wrong. *I smelt a rat when he offered such a cheap price.*

**Spick and span** [ঝকঝকে তকতকে]{.bn} — neat and clean. *Her kitchen is always spick and span.*

**Spill the beans** [গোপন কথা ফাঁস করা]{.bn} — reveal a secret. *Who spilled the beans?*

**Split hairs** [চুলচেরা বিশ্লেষণ করা]{.bn} — argue about trivial details. *Stop splitting hairs.*

**Steal a march on** [আগেভাগে সুবিধা নেওয়া]{.bn} — gain an advantage secretly. *Our rival company stole a march on us.*

**Stick to one's guns** [নিজের মতে অটল থাকা]{.bn} — refuse to change one's opinion. *Despite criticism, she stuck to her guns.*

**Swan song** [শেষ কাজ বা পরিবেশনা]{.bn} — final performance or work. *This film was the director's swan song.*

**Sword of Damocles** [মাথার ওপর ঝুলন্ত বিপদ]{.bn} — an ever-present danger. *The threat of dismissal hung over him like the sword of Damocles.*

## 48.4 Idioms Grouped by Key Word

| Key word | Idioms (meaning) |
|---|---|
| **Blood** | *bad blood* (enmity), *blue blood* (noble birth), *in cold blood* (cruelly), *new blood* (fresh members), *one's blood boils* (to be very angry), *flesh and blood* (relatives / human nature) |
| **Heart** | *by heart* (from memory), *heart and soul* (completely), *lose heart* (be discouraged), *take heart* (be encouraged), *to one's heart's content* (as much as one wants), *a heart of gold* (very kind) |
| **Hand** | *hand in glove* (closely associated), *at hand* (near), *hand to mouth* (bare subsistence), *an old hand* (an experienced person), *have a hand in* (be involved), *wash one's hands of* (refuse responsibility), *with a high hand* (arrogantly) |
| **Eye** | *apple of one's eye*, *see eye to eye* (agree), *an eye-opener*, *turn a blind eye* (ignore deliberately), *in the twinkling of an eye* (instantly), *eye-wash* (pretence) |
| **Head** | *head over heels* (completely, esp. in love), *keep one's head* (stay calm), *lose one's head* (panic), *off one's head* (mad), *head and shoulders above* (far superior) |
| **Cat / Dog** | *bell the cat*, *let the cat out of the bag*, *rain cats and dogs*, *a cat-and-dog life*, *dog in the manger*, *go to the dogs*, *let sleeping dogs lie* (avoid stirring up trouble) |
| **Fire / Water** | *add fuel to the fire*, *play with fire* (take risks), *hold water* (be valid), *in hot water* (in trouble), *throw cold water on*, *fish in troubled waters* |
| **Colours** | *red-letter day*, *red tape*, *white elephant*, *white lie*, *show the white feather*, *black sheep* (disgraced member), *in black and white*, *green with envy*, *once in a blue moon*, *blue blood*, *with flying colours* |
| **Classical / Historical** | *Achilles' heel*, *Herculean task*, *Pyrrhic victory*, *cross the Rubicon*, *sword of Damocles*, *Hobson's choice*, *Pandora's box* (a source of many troubles), *Trojan horse* (a hidden danger), *Midas touch* (ability to make money), *Procrustean bed* (forced conformity), *Gordian knot* (a very difficult problem), *Sisyphean task* (endless futile work), *Cassandra* (a prophet who is not believed), *Shylock* (a merciless moneylender), *Utopia* (an ideal place) |

::: gre
**SAT / GRE / GMAT Corner — Idioms in Reading:** GRE and SAT passages often contain idioms whose literal reading misleads: "The theory does not **hold water**" (is not valid); "Critics were quick to **pick holes in** the study" (find faults); "The author **damns with faint praise**" (criticises through weak praise); "a **Pyrrhic victory**" (a win not worth its cost). The GRE also uses idiom-derived adjectives: **quixotic** (from Don Quixote — idealistic and impractical), **draconian** (very harsh), **byzantine** (excessively complicated), **Machiavellian** (cunning and unscrupulous), **Sisyphean** (endless and futile), **Herculean**, **stentorian** (very loud), **mercurial** (changeable), **jovial** (cheerful), **spartan** (austere).
:::
