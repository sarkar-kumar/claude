## 18.1 Why Learn "Appropriate Prepositions"?

Many English nouns, verbs and adjectives are followed by a **fixed preposition** — *fond* **of**, *interested* **in**, *depend* **on**, *agree* **with**, *capable* **of**. There is no logical rule that tells you which preposition to use; the combination is fixed by **usage (idiom)**. These combinations are tested in every BCS, Bank, university admission and job exam ("Fill in the blank with an appropriate preposition") and form a large share of the **idiom** questions in the SAT, GRE and GMAT.

The best way to master them is to learn each word together with its preposition and a short example sentence. The list below is arranged **alphabetically**. When one word takes different prepositions with different meanings, all the patterns are shown together.

::: tip
**How to study this list:** Learn 30–40 entries a day. Cover the preposition with your finger and test yourself. Write your own sentence for every entry marked as tricky. Notice "families": most *-ed* adjectives of feeling take *at / with / about* (*annoyed with, surprised at, worried about*); most words of ability take *of / at* (*capable of, good at*); words of dependence take *on/upon*.
:::

## 18.3 Words That Take Different Prepositions with Different Meanings

The following words are especially important because the choice of preposition changes the meaning. Review them carefully.

| Word | Preposition | Meaning / Example |
|---|---|---|
| **agree** | with / to / on | with a person; to a proposal; on a point |
| **angry** | with / at / about | with a person; at an action; about a situation |
| **anxious** | about / for | worried about; eager for |
| **attend** | to / on / — | pay attention to / serve / be present at (no prep.) |
| **call** | at / on / for / off / in | visit a place / visit a person / demand / cancel / summon |
| **compare** | with / to | examine differences / show likeness |
| **consist** | of / in | made of / lies in |
| **deal** | in / with | trade in / handle |
| **die** | of / from / for / by | disease / wound / cause / means |
| **differ** | from / with | be unlike / disagree with a person |
| **familiar** | to / with | known to a person / knowing a thing |
| **free** | from / of | without troubles / without cost |
| **good** | at / for / to | skill / benefit / kindness |
| **impatient** | of / with / for | intolerant of / irritated with / eager for |
| **inquire** | of / into / about / after | ask a person / investigate / ask about / ask about health |
| **interfere** | with / in | hinder / meddle |
| **liable** | for / to | responsible for / likely to, subject to |
