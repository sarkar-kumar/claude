## 47.3 The Master List of Phrasal Verbs (H–Z)

::: letter
H
:::

::: list
**Hand down** [উত্তরাধিকারসূত্রে দেওয়া]{.bn} — pass on to the next generation. *The tradition was handed down from our ancestors.*

**Hand in** [জমা দেওয়া]{.bn} — submit. *Hand in your answer scripts.*

**Hand out** [বিতরণ করা]{.bn} — distribute. *The teacher handed out the question papers.*

**Hand over** [হস্তান্তর করা]{.bn} — give control to. *He handed over charge to his successor.*

**Hang about / around** [উদ্দেশ্যহীন ঘোরাঘুরি করা]{.bn} — wait or stay without purpose. *Boys hang about the street corner.*

**Hang on** [অপেক্ষা করা; শক্ত করে ধরা]{.bn} — wait; hold tightly. *Hang on a minute.*

**Hang up** [ফোন রেখে দেওয়া]{.bn} — end a phone call. *She hung up on me.*

**Hit upon / on** [হঠাৎ মাথায় আসা]{.bn} — discover suddenly. *He hit upon a plan.*

**Hold back** [সংযত করা; গোপন রাখা]{.bn} — restrain; withhold. *She held back her tears.*

**Hold on** [অপেক্ষা করা; ধরে থাকা]{.bn} — wait; grip. *Hold on, please — I'll connect you.*

**Hold out** [টিকে থাকা; বাড়িয়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — last; resist; offer. *The food will hold out for a week. / The soldiers held out against the enemy.*

**Hold over** [মুলতুবি রাখা]{.bn} — postpone. *The matter was held over.*

**Hold up** [থামিয়ে দেওয়া; ডাকাতি করা]{.bn} — delay; rob with violence. *Traffic held us up. / Robbers held up the bank.*

**Hush up** [চাপা দেওয়া]{.bn} — keep secret. *The scandal was hushed up.*

**Jump at** [সাগ্রহে গ্রহণ করা]{.bn} — accept eagerly. *He jumped at the offer.*

**Keep at** [লেগে থাকা]{.bn} — continue working at. *Keep at it until you succeed.*

**Keep away** [দূরে রাখা/থাকা]{.bn} — stay away. *Keep away from bad company.*

**Keep back** [গোপন রাখা; আটকে রাখা]{.bn} — conceal; withhold. *He kept back part of the money.*

**Keep down** [দমন করা]{.bn} — control; suppress. *Keep down the prices.*

**Keep off** [দূরে থাকা]{.bn} — not touch or enter. *Keep off the grass.*

**Keep on** [চালিয়ে যাওয়া]{.bn} — continue. *He kept on talking.*

**Keep out** [ঢুকতে না দেওয়া]{.bn} — prevent from entering. *Keep the dogs out.*

**Keep up** [বজায় রাখা]{.bn} — maintain. *Keep up the good work.*

**Keep up with** [সমান তালে চলা]{.bn} — stay level with. *It's hard to keep up with changing technology.*

**Knock down** [ধাক্কা দিয়ে ফেলা; ভাঙা]{.bn} — hit and cause to fall; demolish. *A car knocked him down.*

**Knock off** [কাজ বন্ধ করা; কমানো]{.bn} — stop work; reduce price. *We knock off at five.*

**Knock out** [পরাজিত করা; অজ্ঞান করা]{.bn} — defeat; make unconscious. *Bangladesh was knocked out of the tournament.*

**Laugh at** [উপহাস করা]{.bn} — mock. *Don't laugh at the poor.*

**Laugh off** [হেসে উড়িয়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — treat as unimportant. *He laughed off the criticism.*

**Lay aside / by** [সঞ্চয় করা; সরিয়ে রাখা]{.bn} — save; put away. *Lay aside some money for the future.*

**Lay down** [ত্যাগ করা; প্রণয়ন করা]{.bn} — give up; establish (rules). *They laid down their arms. / The rules are laid down in the constitution.*

**Lay off** [ছাঁটাই করা]{.bn} — dismiss workers. *The factory laid off 200 workers.*

**Lay out** [নকশা করা; খরচ করা]{.bn} — arrange, plan; spend. *The garden was laid out beautifully.*

**Lead up to** [পূর্বাভাস দেওয়া]{.bn} — prepare the way for. *The events leading up to the war.*

**Leave off** [ছেড়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — stop. *He left off smoking.*

**Leave out** [বাদ দেওয়া]{.bn} — omit. *Don't leave out any question.*

**Let down** [হতাশ করা]{.bn} — disappoint. *Don't let your parents down.*

**Let in** [ঢুকতে দেওয়া]{.bn} — allow to enter. *Let in some fresh air.*

**Let off** [ক্ষমা করা; ছুড়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — excuse from punishment; fire, explode. *The judge let him off with a warning.*

**Let out** [ছেড়ে দেওয়া; প্রকাশ করা]{.bn} — release; reveal. *Who let out the secret?*

**Live on** [খেয়ে বেঁচে থাকা]{.bn} — survive on. *He lives on rice and lentils / on his pension.*

**Live up to** [প্রত্যাশা পূরণ করা]{.bn} — meet (expectations). *He lived up to his reputation.*

**Look after** [দেখাশোনা করা]{.bn} — take care of. *Who looks after your children?*

**Look back** [স্মরণ করা]{.bn} — remember. *Looking back, I regret nothing.*

**Look down on / upon** [অবজ্ঞা করা]{.bn} — despise. *Don't look down upon the poor.*

**Look for** [খোঁজা]{.bn} — search. *I am looking for my keys.*

**Look forward to** [অধীর আগ্রহে অপেক্ষা করা]{.bn} — expect with pleasure (+ V-ing). *I look forward to hearing from you.*

**Look in / on** [সংক্ষিপ্ত সাক্ষাৎ]{.bn} — pay a short visit. *I'll look in on my aunt.*

**Look into** [তদন্ত করা]{.bn} — investigate. *The committee will look into the matter.*

**Look on** [দর্শক হয়ে দেখা]{.bn} — watch without taking part. *People looked on as the house burned.*

**Look out** [সাবধান হওয়া]{.bn} — be careful. *Look out! A car is coming.*

**Look over** [পরীক্ষা করা]{.bn} — examine quickly. *Look over the report.*

**Look through** [চোখ বুলানো]{.bn} — read quickly. *I looked through the newspaper.*

**Look up** [অভিধানে খোঁজা; উন্নতি হওয়া]{.bn} — search for information; improve. *Look up the word in a dictionary. / Things are looking up.*

**Look up to** [শ্রদ্ধা করা]{.bn} — respect. *Students look up to their teachers.*

**Pass out** [অজ্ঞান হওয়া; উত্তীর্ণ হওয়া]{.bn} — faint; graduate (from a military academy). *He passed out in the heat.*

**Pass over** [উপেক্ষা করা]{.bn} — ignore (for promotion). *He was passed over for promotion.*

**Pay back** [ফেরত দেওয়া; প্রতিশোধ নেওয়া]{.bn} — repay; take revenge. *I'll pay you back next week.*

**Pay off** [পরিশোধ করা; সুফল দেওয়া]{.bn} — repay fully; succeed. *His hard work paid off.*

**Pick out** [বেছে নেওয়া]{.bn} — choose; identify. *Pick out the best mango.*

**Pick up** [তুলে নেওয়া; শেখা; উন্নতি হওয়া]{.bn} — lift; collect; learn casually; improve. *I picked up some Hindi in Delhi. / Business is picking up.*

**Point out** [দেখিয়ে দেওয়া]{.bn} — indicate. *He pointed out my mistakes.*

**Pull down** [ভেঙে ফেলা]{.bn} — demolish. *The old building was pulled down.*

**Pull off** [সফলভাবে সম্পন্ন করা]{.bn} — succeed in doing something difficult. *They pulled off a surprise victory.*

**Pull through** [সেরে ওঠা]{.bn} — recover from illness or difficulty. *The patient pulled through.*

**Pull together** [একসঙ্গে কাজ করা]{.bn} — cooperate. *We must pull together in a crisis.*

**Pull up** [থামা; তিরস্কার করা]{.bn} — stop (vehicle); scold. *The bus pulled up at the stop.*

**Put across** [বোঝানো]{.bn} — communicate. *He put across his ideas clearly.*

**Put aside** [সঞ্চয় করা; সরিয়ে রাখা]{.bn} — save; ignore for now. *Put aside your differences.*

**Put away** [গুছিয়ে রাখা]{.bn} — store in its place. *Put away your toys.*

**Put down** [দমন করা; লিখে রাখা]{.bn} — suppress; write. *The army put down the revolt.*

**Put forward** [প্রস্তাব করা]{.bn} — propose. *He put forward a new plan.*

**Put off** [স্থগিত করা; নিরুৎসাহিত করা]{.bn} — postpone; discourage. *Never put off till tomorrow what you can do today.*

**Put on** [পরা; বাড়ানো; ভান করা]{.bn} — wear; gain (weight); pretend. *Put on your coat. / He put on weight.*

**Put out** [নেভানো; বিরক্ত করা]{.bn} — extinguish; annoy. *Put out the fire. / She was put out by his remark.*

**Put through** [সংযোগ দেওয়া]{.bn} — connect by phone. *Please put me through to the manager.*

**Put up** [আশ্রয় দেওয়া; নির্মাণ করা]{.bn} — give lodging; build; raise. *We put up at a hotel. / They put up a tent.*

**Put up with** [সহ্য করা]{.bn} — tolerate. *I can't put up with his rudeness.*

**Wind up** [শেষ করা; গুটিয়ে ফেলা]{.bn} — conclude; close (a business). *He wound up his speech with a poem. / The company was wound up.*

**Wipe out** [নিশ্চিহ্ন করা]{.bn} — destroy completely. *The flood wiped out the village.*

**Work out** [সমাধান করা; ব্যায়াম করা; সফল হওয়া]{.bn} — solve; exercise; turn out well. *Work out the sum. / Things will work out.*

**Write off** [বাতিল/অনাদায়ী গণ্য করা]{.bn} — cancel (a debt); consider a failure. *The bank wrote off the bad loans.*
:::
