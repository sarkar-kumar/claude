## 43.1 About Correction Questions

In a **Correction** (or *Correct the sentences*) question, you are given a sentence that contains one or more errors and must **rewrite it correctly**. It appears in BCS Written, Bank written exams (officer, senior officer), university admission tests (DU, JU, RU, CU, BUP) and many other job tests. Unlike error-spotting, you must **produce** the correct form, so knowledge of every chapter of Part A is needed.

**Method:** Identify the error type (agreement, tense, article, preposition, pronoun, comparison, conjunction, word form, redundancy, idiom, parallelism, modifier, spelling), correct it with the **smallest possible change**, and re-read the sentence to make sure no new error has appeared.

This chapter collects the most frequently set incorrect sentences in Bangladeshi competitive examinations, grouped by error type, each with its correction and a brief reason.

## 43.2 Agreement Errors

::: errs
1. ✘ Each of the students have a book. ✔ Each of the students **has** a book.
2. ✘ One of my friends live in Sylhet. ✔ One of my friends **lives** in Sylhet.
3. ✘ The leader with his followers were arrested. ✔ The leader with his followers **was** arrested.
4. ✘ Neither of them were present. ✔ Neither of them **was** present.
5. ✘ The number of students are increasing. ✔ The number of students **is** increasing.
6. ✘ A number of students was absent. ✔ A number of students **were** absent.
7. ✘ Many a man have died. ✔ Many a man **has** died.
8. ✘ Bread and butter are good for health. ✔ Bread and butter **is** good for health.
9. ✘ The poor is not always dishonest. ✔ The poor **are** not always dishonest.
10. ✘ The news are true. ✔ The news **is** true.
11. ✘ Mathematics are a difficult subject. ✔ Mathematics **is** a difficult subject.
12. ✘ The cattle is grazing. ✔ The cattle **are** grazing.
13. ✘ Every boy and every girl were present. ✔ Every boy and every girl **was** present.
14. ✘ Either you or he are guilty. ✔ Either you or he **is** guilty.
15. ✘ Ten miles are a long distance. ✔ Ten miles **is** a long distance.
16. ✘ The jury has given their verdict. ✔ The jury **has** given **its** verdict.
17. ✘ Slow and steady win the race. ✔ Slow and steady **wins** the race.
18. ✘ Who do not know Rabindranath? ✔ Who **does** not know Rabindranath?
19. ✘ The quality of these mangoes are good. ✔ The quality of these mangoes **is** good.
20. ✘ There is many reasons for this. ✔ There **are** many reasons for this.
:::

## 43.3 Tense and Verb-Form Errors

::: errs
1. ✘ I have seen him yesterday. ✔ I **saw** him yesterday.
2. ✘ He is ill since Monday. ✔ He **has been** ill since Monday.
3. ✘ I am reading for two hours. ✔ I **have been reading** for two hours.
4. ✘ He did not went to school. ✔ He did not **go** to school.
5. ✘ If I was a king, I would help the poor. ✔ If I **were** a king, …
6. ✘ It is high time we start. ✔ It is high time we **started**.
7. ✘ No sooner had he come when she left. ✔ No sooner had he come **than** she left.
8. ✘ He said that he will come. ✔ He said that he **would** come.
9. ✘ I will go if he will come. ✔ I will go if he **comes**.
10. ✘ The patient died before the doctor had come. ✔ The patient **had died** before the doctor **came**.
11. ✘ He has died two years ago. ✔ He **died** two years ago.
12. ✘ It is ten years since he has gone. ✔ It is ten years since he **went**.
13. ✘ I am knowing him for years. ✔ I **have known** him for years.
14. ✘ He has broke the glass. ✔ He has **broken** the glass.
15. ✘ The murderer was hung. ✔ The murderer was **hanged**.
16. ✘ The prices have raised. ✔ The prices have **risen**.
17. ✘ He laid on the bed. ✔ He **lay** on the bed.
18. ✘ The sun rose in the east (general truth). ✔ The sun **rises** in the east.
19. ✘ I wish I am a bird. ✔ I wish I **were** a bird.
20. ✘ He was born at 1971. ✔ He was born **in** 1971.
:::

## 43.6 Pronoun Errors

::: errs
1. ✘ Between you and I, he is a liar. ✔ Between you and **me**, …
2. ✘ Everyone must do their duty. ✔ Everyone must do **his** duty.
3. ✘ One should respect his parents. ✔ One should respect **one's** parents.
4. ✘ I and you will go. ✔ **You and I** will go.
5. ✘ Who are you looking for? ✔ **Whom** are you looking for? (formal)
6. ✘ Let he go. ✔ Let **him** go.
7. ✘ He enjoyed at the party. ✔ He enjoyed **himself** at the party.
8. ✘ The book is your's. ✔ The book is **yours**.
9. ✘ He is taller than me. ✔ He is taller than **I** (am).
10. ✘ Whom do you think is coming? ✔ **Who** do you think is coming?
11. ✘ The man which came here is my uncle. ✔ The man **who** came here is my uncle.
12. ✘ He is one of the best players who has played for Bangladesh. ✔ … who **have** played …
13. ✘ It is me. ✔ It is **I**. (formal)
14. ✘ Myself and Rahim went there. ✔ Rahim and **I** went there.
15. ✘ The two brothers quarrelled with one another. ✔ … with **each other**.
:::

## 43.11 Sentences with Multiple Errors (Frequently Asked)

::: errs
1. ✘ He is one of the best boy who have stood first. ✔ He is one of the best **boys** who **have** stood first.
2. ✘ Each of the boy have done their duty. ✔ Each of the **boys has** done **his** duty.
3. ✘ The poors are not always unhappy and the riches are not always happy. ✔ **The poor** are not always unhappy, and **the rich** are not always happy.
4. ✘ Though he is rich but he is not happy because he has no peace of mind so he is miserable. ✔ Though he is rich, he is not happy because he has no peace of mind; **so** he is miserable.
5. ✘ He told me that he will go to Dhaka tomorrow. ✔ He told me that he **would** go to Dhaka **the next day**.
6. ✘ I have been knowing him since ten years. ✔ I **have known** him **for** ten years.
7. ✘ He is an M.P. and an Minister. ✔ He is an M.P. and **a** Minister.
8. ✘ Rice is the stable food of the most of the Bangladeshi people. ✔ Rice is the **staple** food of **most** Bangladeshis.
9. ✘ The teacher with his students were going to the picnic spot yesterday when it begins raining. ✔ The teacher with his students **was** going to the picnic spot yesterday when it **began** raining.
10. ✘ I and he is going to Chittagong next day. ✔ **He and I are** going to Chattogram **tomorrow**.
11. ✘ Neither of the two sisters have came yet. ✔ Neither of the two sisters **has come** yet.
12. ✘ Please give me a informations about the admission. ✔ Please give me **some information** about the admission.
13. ✘ He is more cleverer than any boy of the class. ✔ He is **cleverer** than any **other** boy **in** the class.
14. ✘ The boy who is standing at the gate he is my brother. ✔ The boy who is standing at the gate is my brother.
15. ✘ He insisted me to go there with him and he disallowed me to come back early. ✔ He **insisted on my going** there with him and **did not allow** me to come back early.
:::

::: gre
**SAT / GRE / GMAT Corner — From Correction to Sentence Correction:** The GMAT's Sentence Correction gives you five versions of the underlined part; your job is the same as in a correction question, except that you must choose the version that is **grammatically correct, logically clear and concise** at once. Fix the obvious error first (agreement, verb form, pronoun), then eliminate any choice that introduces a **new** problem (wordiness, a dangling modifier, a meaning change). The Bangladeshi skill of writing the correct sentence yourself is excellent preparation: if you can produce the correct form, you can recognise it.
:::
