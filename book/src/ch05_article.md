## 5.1 What Are Articles?

**A**, **an** and **the** are called **articles**. They are the most frequently used words in English and are a special kind of determiner (Chapter 04). Traditional grammar calls them "demonstrative adjectives" because they point out or limit nouns.

- **A / An** — the **indefinite article**. It points to *any one* person or thing, not a particular one: *a book* (any book), *an orange*.
- **The** — the **definite article**. It points to a *particular* person or thing that the listener can identify: *the book* (the one we both know about).

::: ex
- I saw **a** dog in the street. (any dog — mentioned for the first time)
- **The** dog was barking loudly. (the particular dog already mentioned)
:::

Because Bangla has no exact equivalent of English articles (we use *-টি, -টা* or nothing), article errors are extremely common among Bangladeshi learners and are tested in every competitive exam — in fill-in-the-blanks, error detection and correction.

## 5.2 *A* or *An*? — The Sound Rule

::: rule
The choice between *a* and *an* depends on the **first sound** (not the first letter) of the next word.

- **A** before a **consonant sound**: *a book, a cat, a university, a European, a one-taka coin, a uniform, a useful thing, a once-famous actor.*
- **An** before a **vowel sound**: *an apple, an egg, an hour, an honest man, an heir, an MA, an MBBS doctor, an SSC candidate, an X-ray, an LLB degree.*
:::

### 5.2.1 Letters That Trick You

| Starts with | Sound | Article | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **u** pronounced "you" (ইউ) | consonant /j/ | **a** | a university, a union, a unique case, a useful book, a usual thing, a European, a ewe, a uniform, a unit, a utensil |
| **u** pronounced "aa" (আ) | vowel | **an** | an umbrella, an uncle, an ugly sight, an umpire, an urgent letter, an undertaking |
| **o** pronounced "wa" (ওয়া) | consonant /w/ | **a** | a one-eyed man, a one-way road, a once-popular singer |
| **silent h** | vowel | **an** | an hour, an honest man, an honour, an heir, an heiress, an honorarium, an honorary post |
| **sounded h** | consonant | **a** | a house, a horse, a hotel, a historic event, a hero, a hospital, a habit |
| **abbreviations** read letter by letter, starting with a vowel sound (A, E, F, H, I, L, M, N, O, R, S, X) | vowel ("eff", "aitch", "em", "es") | **an** | an MP, an M.A., an S.S.C. examinee, an F.R.C.S., an LL.B., an X-ray, an NGO, an HSC candidate, an R.A.B. officer, an IAS officer |
| abbreviations starting with a consonant sound (B, C, D, G, J, K, P, Q, T, U, V, W, Y, Z) | consonant | **a** | a B.A., a B.Sc., a UNO official, a UN agency ("you-en"), a BCS cadre, a PhD student |
| abbreviations read as a word (acronyms) | according to the word | | a NATO member, a UNESCO site ("you-nes-co"), an OPEC country, a SAARC country |

::: tip
**Exam Tip — say it aloud.** Silently pronounce the next word: if the very first *sound* is a, e, i, o, u (as in *apple, egg, ink, ox, umbrella*), use *an*; otherwise use *a*. The most-tested items: **an hour, an honest man, an heir, a university, a European, a one-taka note, a unique, an MA, an M.Sc., an SSC, a useful, an umbrella, an LLB, a UN, a historical** (both *a historical* and the old-fashioned *an historical* exist; *a historical* is the modern standard).
:::

## 5.3 Uses of the Indefinite Article *A / An*

### 5.3.1 Before a singular countable noun mentioned for the first time

::: ex
- I have **a** cat. **The** cat is white.
- Once there lived **a** king. **The** king had three sons.
:::

### 5.3.2 In the sense of *one*

::: ex
- **A** day or two. / Twelve inches make **a** foot. / **A** stitch in time saves nine.
- He did not say **a** word. (= even one word)
- Wait **a** minute.
:::

### 5.3.3 In the sense of *any* / representing a class

::: ex
- **A** cow is a useful animal. (= any cow / cows in general)
- **A** student should be punctual.
- **A** dog has four legs.
:::

### 5.3.4 Before a profession, job or role (singular)

::: ex
- He is **a** doctor. (✘ He is doctor.)
- She wants to be **an** engineer.
- My father is **a** banker.
:::

### 5.3.5 In the sense of *per / each* (rate)

::: ex
- Rice sells at fifty taka **a** kilo.
- He visits us twice **a** week.
- The car runs at eighty kilometres **an** hour.
:::

### 5.3.6 In the sense of *a certain* (before a proper noun)

::: ex
- **A** Mr. Hasan came to see you. (= a certain person named Hasan whom I do not know)
- **A** Rahim from Comilla has applied for the job.
:::

### 5.3.7 Before a proper noun used as a common noun (= someone like)

::: ex
- He is **a** second Newton. (= a great scientist like Newton)
- He thinks he is **a** Shakespeare.
- **A** Daniel come to judgement!
:::

### 5.3.8 In exclamations with *what* and *such* (singular countable nouns)

::: ex
- **What a** beautiful bird! (✘ What beautiful bird!)
- He is **such a** fool!
- **What a** pity! / **What a** shame!
:::

### 5.3.9 With *quite, rather, half, many, so, too, as* (word-order rules)

::: ex
- It is **quite a** long way. / It is **rather a** good idea (or *a rather good idea*).
- **Half an** hour ✔ / a **half-hour** (AmE)
- **Many a** man has died for his country.
- It is **so** difficult **a** problem that I cannot solve it. (formal: *so + adj + a + noun*)
- It is **too** big **a** house for me. (formal: *too + adj + a + noun*)
- He is **as** good **a** player **as** his brother.
:::

### 5.3.10 In certain fixed expressions

*a lot of, a great deal of, a great many, a good many, a few, a little, a couple of, a dozen, a hundred, a thousand, in a hurry, in a fix, at a loss, at a glance, as a rule, as a matter of fact, in a nutshell, at a stretch, a short while, all of a sudden, to put an end to, to make a noise, to make a fool of, to take a fancy to, to have a mind to, to keep an eye on, to have a headache / a fever / a cold, to go for a walk / a swim / a drive, to take a seat, to make an effort, to tell a lie, in a low voice, with a view to, for a long time, a matter of time.*

### 5.3.11 Before uncountable nouns with an adjective (a kind of / an instance of)

::: ex
- He has **a** good knowledge of English.
- She has **a** sweet voice.
- We had **a** wonderful time.
- ✘ He showed **a** great courage. ✔ He showed **great courage**. (*courage, advice, information, weather, progress* do not take *a* even with an adjective.)
- ✔ **A** heavy rain fell last night. (common, but *heavy rain fell* is safer.)
:::

## 5.4 Uses of the Definite Article *The*

### 5.4.1 For something already mentioned or known to both speaker and listener

::: ex
- I bought a pen and a book. **The** pen is red.
- Please shut **the** door. (the door of this room)
- Where is **the** teacher? (our teacher)
:::

### 5.4.2 For things that are **one of a kind** (unique)

*the sun, the moon, the earth, the sky, the world, the universe, the equator, the North Pole, the horizon, the Prime Minister, the Holy Quran, the Bible, the Ramayana, the Internet.*

::: ex
- **The** sun rises in **the** east.
- **The** earth moves round **the** sun.
:::

### 5.4.3 Before a singular noun representing a **whole class** (generic *the*)

::: ex
- **The** cow is a useful animal. (= cows in general)
- **The** rose is the sweetest of all flowers.
- **The** computer has changed our lives.
- **The** dog is a faithful animal.
:::

::: note
**Man** and **woman**, when used for the whole race/sex, take **no article**: **Man** *is mortal.* **Woman** *is the better half of man.* ✘ *The man is mortal* (that would mean a particular man).
:::

### 5.4.4 Before an **adjective** used to mean a **class of people** (plural)

*the rich, the poor, the old, the young, the sick, the blind, the deaf, the dumb, the dead, the wounded, the unemployed, the brave, the wise, the honest, the learned.*

::: ex
- **The rich are** not always happy. (= rich people — plural verb)
- **The poor** should be helped.
- **The wounded were** taken to hospital.
- Only **the brave** deserve the fair. (Dryden)
:::

### 5.4.5 Before **superlatives** and **ordinals**

::: ex
- He is **the best** student in the class.
- Mount Everest is **the highest** peak in the world.
- Friday is **the sixth** day of the week.
- He was **the first** to arrive. / **the last** day / **the next** chapter (in a sequence)
- **The only** son / **the same** book / **the very** man / **the main** reason / **the whole** story
:::

### 5.4.6 Before a **comparative** to show two corresponding increases

::: ex
- **The more** you read, **the more** you learn.
- **The sooner, the better.**
- **The higher** you go, **the colder** it becomes.
- He is **the taller of the two** brothers. (comparative + *of the two*)
:::

### 5.4.7 Before proper nouns of these types (Geography)

| Category | Use **the** | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Rivers | ✔ | the Padma, the Meghna, the Jamuna, the Ganges, the Nile, the Thames |
| Seas, oceans, gulfs, bays, canals, straits | ✔ | the Bay of Bengal, the Pacific (Ocean), the Red Sea, the Persian Gulf, the Suez Canal, the English Channel |
| Mountain ranges | ✔ | the Himalayas, the Alps, the Andes, the Chittagong Hill Tracts |
| Groups of islands | ✔ | the Maldives, the Andamans, the West Indies, the Philippines |
| Deserts | ✔ | the Sahara, the Gobi, the Thar |
| Plural country names / names with *Republic, Kingdom, States, Union* | ✔ | the USA, the UK, the Netherlands, the People's Republic of China, the UAE |
| Directions as regions | ✔ | the Middle East, the Far East, the West, the North |
| Single mountains | ✘ | Mount Everest, Keokradong, Tajingdong (Bijoy) |
| Single lakes | ✘ | Lake Kaptai (but *the Kaptai Lake* is common in BD usage), Lake Victoria |
| Single islands | ✘ | St. Martin's Island, Bhola, Sri Lanka |
| Continents, countries, cities, states | ✘ | Asia, Bangladesh, India, Dhaka, London, Texas |
| Streets, roads, squares, parks | ✘ | Mirpur Road, Elephant Road, Times Square, Hyde Park |

::: ex
- ✘ **The Bangladesh** is a riverine country. ✔ **Bangladesh** is a riverine country.
- ✘ **The Mount Everest** is the highest peak. ✔ **Mount Everest**…
- ✘ **Padma** is a mighty river. ✔ **The Padma** is a mighty river.
- ✔ **The Sundarbans** is the largest mangrove forest in the world.
:::

### 5.4.8 Before names of these (non-geographical) proper nouns

| Category | Examples |
|---|---|
| Holy and famous books | the Quran, the Bible, the Gita, the Ramayana, the Mahabharata, the Vedas (but *Gitanjali*, *Hamlet* — titles of ordinary books take no article unless "the" is part of the title: *The Waste Land*) |
| Newspapers and magazines | the Daily Star, the Prothom Alo, the Times, the Economist (the article is often part of the name) |
| Ships, trains, aircraft | the Titanic, the Subarna Express, the Bangabandhu Satellite |
| Famous buildings, museums, hotels | the Taj Mahal, the National Museum, the Shaheed Minar, the White House, the Sonargaon Hotel |
| Historical events and periods | the French Revolution, the Liberation War, the Renaissance, the Middle Ages |
| Organisations, political parties | the United Nations, the World Bank, the Awami League, the BNP |
| Nations (as a people) | the English, the French, the Japanese, the Bengalis |
| Families (plural surname) | the Khans, the Tagores, the Smiths |

### 5.4.9 Before musical instruments

::: ex
- He plays **the** guitar / **the** flute / **the** harmonium / **the** violin.
- (But games take no article: He plays **cricket / football / chess**.)
:::

### 5.4.10 Before an adjective/noun used in the singular for an abstract quality

::: ex
- **The** pen is mightier than **the** sword.
- He felt **the** patriot in him rise.
- **The** beautiful is not always **the** good.
:::

### 5.4.11 *The* + noun + *of* (when the noun is defined by a phrase)

::: ex
- **The** history **of** Bangladesh / **The** water **of** this well / **The** honesty **of** the man / **The** capital **of** France
- (But: *History is interesting* — general sense, no article.)
:::

### 5.4.12 With body parts after a preposition (instead of a possessive)

::: ex
- He caught me by **the** hand. (✘ by my hand)
- She was shot in **the** leg.
- He patted the boy on **the** back.
:::

### 5.4.13 Fixed expressions with *the*

*in the morning/afternoon/evening, by the way, on the whole, in the end, at the moment, in the meantime, to tell the truth, out of the question, on the contrary, at the same time, by the dozen, on the other hand, all the same, in the long run, the day after tomorrow, the day before yesterday, to go to the cinema/theatre, in the dark, in the sun, at the top, at the bottom.*

## 5.5 Omission of Articles (Zero Article)

No article is used in these cases:

### 5.5.1 Before plural or uncountable nouns used in a general sense

::: ex
- **Books** are our best friends. (✘ The books are…)
- **Honesty** is the best policy. (✘ The honesty…)
- **Milk** is good for health.
- **Gold** is a precious metal.
:::

### 5.5.2 Before most proper nouns (names of persons, countries, cities, continents, days, months, festivals)

::: ex
- **Rahim** is my friend. **Dhaka** is a big city. **Asia** is the largest continent.
- **Sunday** is a holiday. **December** is the month of victory.
- **Eid** is a happy festival. (*the Eid* is also heard, but zero article is standard.)
:::

### 5.5.3 Before names of languages, subjects, games, meals, diseases (most)

::: ex
- He speaks **English**. (But *the English language*, *the English* = the people.)
- I like **mathematics**. She plays **badminton**.
- We have **breakfast** at eight. **Lunch** is ready. (But *a heavy breakfast*, *the lunch I had yesterday*.)
- He is suffering from **fever / cholera / diabetes / cancer**. (But *a cold, a headache, a fever* — informal, *the measles, the flu*.)
:::

### 5.5.4 Before *school, college, university, hospital, church, mosque, temple, prison, jail, court, bed, sea, market, home, work, office* used for their **primary purpose**

| Primary purpose (no article) | Other purpose (with *the*) |
|---|---|
| He goes to **school** (as a student). | His father went to **the school** to meet the headmaster. |
| She is in **hospital** (as a patient). | I went to **the hospital** to see her. |
| The thief was sent to **prison**. | His wife visited **the prison**. |
| We go to **mosque** on Friday (to pray). | The tourists visited **the mosque**. |
| He went to **bed** at ten (to sleep). | He sat on **the bed**. |
| He went to **sea** (became a sailor). | We went to **the sea** for a holiday. |
| He is at **home**. / He came **home**. | He visited **the home** for the elderly. |
| She goes to **work** by bus. | — |

### 5.5.5 Before nouns of time, transport and place in fixed phrases

*by bus, by train, by car, by air, by sea, on foot, at night, at noon, at midnight, at dawn, at sunset, by day, by night, at home, at school, in bed, in hospital, at present, at last, at first, in time, on time, by mistake, by chance, by heart, in fact, in debt, in trouble, at hand, on fire, on duty, on board, at sea, from door to door, from hand to hand, day by day, face to face, side by side, arm in arm.*

::: ex
- He came **by bus**. (✘ by a bus / by the bus — but *in the bus*, *on the 8 o'clock bus* are fine)
- We met **at night**. (but *in the night*, *during the night*)
:::

### 5.5.6 Before titles and positions used as complements or after *as / turned / elected / appointed*

::: ex
- He was elected **chairman** of the committee. (✘ the chairman)
- They made him **captain**.
- He was appointed **Principal** of the college.
- He turned **Muslim / traitor**.
- As **Prime Minister**, she visited China.
:::

### 5.5.7 With *man* (mankind), *woman* (womankind), *nature, society, God, heaven, hell, parliament* in general sense

::: ex
- **Man** is mortal. **Nature** is the best teacher. **God** is great. **Society** must protect children.
:::

### 5.5.8 With *next, last* + time word (relative to now)

::: ex
- I saw him **last** week. He will come **next** Monday. (✘ the last week / the next Monday)
- But: *He left* **the next day**. (= the following day, in a past story) / **the last week of** *March*.
:::

### 5.5.9 In headlines, notices, instructions and after *kind of / sort of / type of*

::: ex
- *Minister opens bridge.* / *Push button to start.*
- What **kind of man** is he? (✘ kind of a man — informal AmE)
:::

### 5.5.10 In paired or balanced expressions

*father and son, husband and wife, day and night, from top to bottom, rich and poor, young and old, from head to foot, hand in hand, body and soul.*

## 5.6 Repetition of Articles

When two nouns/adjectives refer to **different** persons/things, the article is **repeated**; when they refer to the **same** person/thing, the article is used **once**.

::: ex
- **The** Secretary and Treasurer **is** present. (one person holds both posts)
- **The** Secretary and **the** Treasurer **are** present. (two persons)
- I have **a** black and white cow. (one cow, black-and-white in colour)
- I have **a** black and **a** white cow. (two cows)
- **The** poet and philosopher **is** dead. (one person)
- **The** poet and **the** philosopher **are** dead. (two persons)
:::

This rule connects with subject-verb agreement (Chapter 23) and is a favourite BCS item.

## 5.7 Position of the Article

The article normally comes **before** the noun and its adjectives: *a good boy, the tall man.* Exceptions:

- After *such, what, many, half, quite, rather*: *such a man, what a pity, many a time, half an hour, quite a crowd, rather a long story.*
- After an adjective preceded by *so, too, as, how, however*: *so great a man, too heavy a load, as clever a boy as, how tall a man, however great a sinner.*
- After *all, both, double, twice* (pre-determiners): *all the boys, both the girls, double the amount, twice the size.*

## 5.8 Articles with Countable and Uncountable Nouns

| Noun type | General sense | Specific sense |
|---|---|---|
| Singular countable | **a/an** or **the** (generic): *A dog barks. The dog is faithful.* | **the**: *The dog next door barks all night.* |
| Plural countable | **zero**: *Dogs bark.* | **the**: *The dogs next door bark.* |
| Uncountable | **zero**: *Water is essential.* | **the**: *The water in this bottle is dirty.* |

::: rule
A **singular countable noun** can never stand alone. It must have *a/an, the*, or another determiner (*my, this, every*…): ✘ *I bought pen.* ✔ *I bought a pen / the pen / my pen.*
:::

## 5.9 Special Cases Frequently Tested

| Case | Correct form | Explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Few / A few / The few | See Chapter 04 | meaning changes |
| Little / A little / The little | See Chapter 04 | meaning changes |
| *Most* (= majority) | **Most** people… (✘ the most people) | no article |
| *Most* (superlative) | She is **the most** beautiful girl. | *the* |
| *Most* (= very) | It is **a most** interesting book. | *a* (= very) |
| *Other* | **the other** day (= a few days ago) | idiom |
| Musical instruments vs games | play **the** flute / play **cricket** | — |
| Diseases | **the** measles / **the** flu (informal) / **a** cold / **a** headache / **fever** | — |
| Meals | **breakfast** / **a** good breakfast / **the** breakfast we had | — |
| Seasons | in (**the**) winter — both acceptable | — |
| Instruments of the press | **the** press, **the** media | — |
| Unique positions | **the** President, **the** Prime Minister (but *He became President*) | — |
| Cinema / theatre / radio | go to **the** cinema, listen to **the** radio, but watch **television** / **TV** | idiom |

## 5.10 Common Errors with Articles

::: errs
1. ✘ He is **an** university student. ✔ He is **a** university student.
2. ✘ She is **a** honest girl. ✔ She is **an** honest girl.
3. ✘ I waited for **a** hour. ✔ I waited for **an** hour.
4. ✘ He is **a** M.A. ✔ He is **an** M.A.
5. ✘ **The** honesty is the best policy. ✔ **Honesty** is the best policy.
6. ✘ **Sun** rises in **east**. ✔ **The** sun rises in **the** east.
7. ✘ He is **best** boy in the class. ✔ He is **the best** boy in the class.
8. ✘ **The** Dhaka is **a** capital of Bangladesh. ✔ Dhaka is **the** capital of Bangladesh.
9. ✘ **Meghna** is a big river. ✔ **The Meghna** is a big river.
10. ✘ I go to **the** school every day (as a student). ✔ I go to **school** every day.
11. ✘ He plays **the** football. ✔ He plays **football**.
12. ✘ She plays **violin**. ✔ She plays **the violin**.
13. ✘ **The** man is mortal. ✔ **Man** is mortal.
14. ✘ He came by **the** bus. ✔ He came **by bus**.
15. ✘ **Rich** are not always happy. ✔ **The rich** are not always happy.
16. ✘ What **beautiful** day! ✔ What **a** beautiful day!
17. ✘ He is **a** M.P. from Sylhet. ✔ He is **an** M.P. from Sylhet.
18. ✘ **A** English is an international language. ✔ **English** is an international language.
19. ✘ He was elected **the** President. ✔ He was elected **President**.
20. ✘ It is **an** unique opportunity. ✔ It is **a** unique opportunity.
21. ✘ Give me **a** water. ✔ Give me **some** water / **a glass of** water.
22. ✘ **The** Mount Everest ✔ **Mount** Everest
23. ✘ He is **an** European. ✔ He is **a** European. (*European* begins with the "you" sound.)
24. ✘ I have **a** headache and **the** fever. ✔ I have **a** headache and **a** fever (informal) / and **fever**.
25. ✘ The Rajshahi is famous for **the** mangoes. ✔ **Rajshahi** is famous for **mangoes**.
26. ✘ **Hamlet** was written by **the** Shakespeare. ✔ *Hamlet* was written by **Shakespeare**. (Titles of ordinary books take no article unless *The* is part of the title: *Gitanjali, Hamlet, Paradise Lost* but *The Tempest, The Waste Land*.)
27. ✘ He takes **a** tea every morning. ✔ He takes **tea** every morning / **a cup of** tea.
28. ✘ The Prime Minister visited **the** Japan. ✔ … visited **Japan**.
29. ✘ He went to **the** bed early. ✔ He went to **bed** early.
30. ✘ **A** Bangladesh is my motherland. ✔ **Bangladesh** is my motherland.
:::

::: gre
**SAT / GRE / GMAT Corner — Articles and Idiom:** International tests rarely test *a/an* directly but do test idiomatic article usage inside larger structures, e.g. ✔ *"the more…, the more…"*, ✔ *"as + adjective + a + noun + as"* (*as good a choice as*), ✔ *"so + adjective + a + noun + that"* (*so strong a case that*), and the difference between **"the number of"** (singular verb) and **"a number of"** (plural verb). In Reading, the generic *the* (*"The cheetah is endangered"*) signals a statement about a species, not an individual animal.
:::
