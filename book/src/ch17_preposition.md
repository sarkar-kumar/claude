# Preposition {#ch17}

[Part A · Grammar]{.chpart}

## 17.1 Definition

A **preposition** is a word placed **before a noun or pronoun** (or a noun-equivalent) to show its **relation** to some other word in the sentence. The noun or pronoun that follows is called the **object of the preposition**, and the two together form a **prepositional phrase**.

::: ex
- The book is **on** the table. (relation between *book* and *table* — place)
- He came **after** lunch. (time)
- She is fond **of** music. (fixed relation with *fond*)
- I went there **with** him. (accompaniment — object pronoun *him*)
:::

The word *preposition* means "placed before" (Latin *prae* = before + *positio* = placing).

::: rule
**The object of a preposition is always in the objective case:** *between you and* **me** (✘ I), *for* **him** (✘ he), *with* **them** (✘ they), *to* **whom** (✘ who).

**A verb after a preposition takes the gerund (-ing) form:** *fond of* **singing**, *without* **saying**, *before* **leaving**.
:::

## 17.2 Kinds of Prepositions

| Kind | Explanation | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Simple** | single, short words | at, by, for, from, in, of, off, on, out, to, up, with, till, through |
| **Compound** | formed by prefixing *a-* (on) or *be-* (by) to a noun, adjective or adverb | about, above, across, along, amid, among, around, before, behind, below, beneath, beside, between, beyond, inside, outside, within, without, underneath, throughout |
| **Double** | two simple prepositions together | into, onto, upon, from behind, from beneath, from among, out of, from within, till after |
| **Participial** | present/past participles used as prepositions | concerning, considering, regarding, respecting, touching, during, pending, notwithstanding, barring, excepting, following, given, past, owing (to) |
| **Phrasal (complex)** | groups of words acting as one preposition | according to, because of, by means of, by way of, in front of, in spite of, instead of, on account of, in addition to, in place of, with regard to, with reference to, in order to, owing to, due to, in favour of, for the sake of, in the event of, on behalf of, in comparison with, in accordance with, by virtue of, in lieu of, with a view to, as regards, apart from, as for |
| **Disguised** | shortened forms that look like articles | *a* = on/in (*He went* **a**-*hunting*; *ten taka* **a** *kilo* = per), *o'* = of (*five* **o'**clock) |

::: ex
- **Considering** his age, he did well. (participial)
- **During** the war, many people died. (participial)
- **In spite of** his illness, he attended. (phrasal)
- He was absent **on account of** illness. (phrasal)
- **Owing to** heavy rain, the match was cancelled. (phrasal)
:::

## 17.3 Prepositions of Time

### 17.3.1 *At*, *On*, *In*

| Preposition | Used with | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | a **point** of time; clock times; festivals (as periods); fixed expressions | at 6 o'clock, at noon, at midnight, at sunrise, at sunset, at dawn, at night, at present, at the moment, at the weekend (BrE), at Eid, at Christmas, at the end of, at the age of 20 |
| **on** | **days and dates**; specific parts of a particular day | on Friday, on 16 December, on 21st February 1952, on my birthday, on Independence Day, on Friday morning, on the morning of 26 March, on time (= punctually) |
| **in** | **longer periods**: months, years, seasons, centuries, parts of the day (general); future time "after" | in May, in 1971, in winter, in the 19th century, in the morning, in the evening, in the afternoon, in a week (= after a week), in time (= early enough), in the past, in the future |

::: ex
- He was born **in** 1971, **on** 26 March, **at** 4 a.m.
- I will see you **in** the morning. / I will see you **on** Sunday morning.
- ✘ He will come **after** two days (meaning from now). ✔ He will come **in** two days. (*after* is used with past reference: *He came after two days.*)
- ✘ **In** night ✔ **At** night / ✔ **In** the night (= during a particular night)
- ✘ **On** the morning ✔ **In** the morning / ✔ **On** the morning **of** 21st February
- ✘ **At** Sunday ✔ **On** Sunday
- **No preposition** before *this, last, next, every, today, tomorrow, yesterday*: ✘ *on next Friday* ✔ *next Friday*; ✘ *in this week* ✔ *this week*; ✘ *at yesterday* ✔ *yesterday*.
:::

::: note
**On time vs in time.** *On time* = punctually, at the scheduled moment: *The train arrived* **on time**. *In time* = early enough (before it is too late): *We reached the station* **in time** *to catch the train.*
:::

### 17.3.2 *For*, *Since*, *From*

| Preposition | Meaning | Tense | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **for** | a **period / duration** of time | any tense (often perfect) | for two hours, for three days, for a long time, for ten years |
| **since** | a **point** of time in the past up to now | perfect tenses | since Monday, since 2015, since morning, since childhood, since 10 o'clock |
| **from** | starting point (with *to/till/until* or future) | any | from 9 to 5, from Monday to Friday, from now on, from tomorrow |

::: ex
- I have lived here **for** ten years. / I have lived here **since** 2015.
- He has been ill **since** last Friday. (✘ from last Friday)
- It has been raining **since** morning. (✘ from morning)
- The office remains open **from** 9 a.m. **to** 5 p.m.
- The new rule will come into effect **from** next month.
- ✘ He has been absent **from** three days. ✔ … **for** three days.
:::

### 17.3.3 *During*, *For*, *While*

- **During** + noun (when something happens, within a period): **During** *the war, many people died.* / *I fell asleep* **during** *the film.*
- **For** + duration (how long): *I slept* **for** *two hours.*
- **While** is a **conjunction** + clause: **While** *I was watching the film, I fell asleep.* (✘ during I was watching)

### 17.3.4 *By*, *Until/Till*, *Before*, *Within*

- **By** = not later than (deadline): *Submit the form* **by** *Friday.* *He will have finished* **by** *next week.*
- **Until / Till** = up to (continuous action that stops): *Wait here* **until** *5 o'clock.* *He worked* **till** *midnight.*
- **Before** = earlier than: *Come* **before** *10 a.m.*
- **Within** = inside a limit of time: *Finish it* **within** *a week.*

::: ex
- ✘ I'll stay here **by** Friday. ✔ I'll stay here **until** Friday.
- ✘ Return the book **until** Monday. ✔ Return the book **by** Monday.
:::

### 17.3.5 *Ago* and *Before* (Adverbs — Chapter 15)

*Two years* **ago** (from now, past simple) / *two years* **before** (from a past point, past perfect).

## 17.4 Prepositions of Place and Position

### 17.4.1 *At*, *In*, *On*

| Preposition | Used for | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **at** | a **point / specific location**; small places; addresses with numbers; events; public places seen as points | at the door, at the bus stop, at the station, at the corner, at 25 Mirpur Road, at home, at school, at work, at the meeting, at a party, at the top, at the bottom |
| **in** | **inside** an enclosed space; large areas (cities, countries, continents) | in the room, in the box, in the garden, in Dhaka, in Bangladesh, in Asia, in the sky, in the world, in a car, in a taxi, in the newspaper, in a book, in the picture, in bed |
| **on** | a **surface**; lines (roads, rivers, coasts); floors; public transport; devices | on the table, on the wall, on the floor, on the roof, on the first floor, on the Padma (situated on the river), on the coast, on the road, on a bus/train/plane/ship, on a bicycle, on TV, on the radio, on the internet, on page 10, on the left/right |

::: ex
- He lives **in** Dhaka. He lives **at** 12 Green Road. He lives **on** Green Road (AmE) / **in** Green Road (BrE).
- She is **at** home. / She is **in** the house.
- ✘ He is **in** the bus. ✔ He is **on** the bus. (*in* a car / taxi, *on* a bus / train / plane)
- ✘ Write **in** the board. ✔ Write **on** the board.
- I read it **in** a newspaper. / I saw it **on** television.
- Rajshahi is **on** the Padma. (located beside the river)
:::

::: tip
**Exam Tip — *arrive at* vs *arrive in*.** *Arrive* **at** a small place or building (*at the station, at the airport, at school*); *arrive* **in** a city or country (*in Dhaka, in London*). Never *arrive to*. *Reach* takes **no** preposition: ✘ *reach at Dhaka* ✔ *reach Dhaka*.
:::

### 17.4.2 *In* vs *Into*; *On* vs *Onto*

- **In** = position (rest): *He is* **in** *the room.*
- **Into** = movement towards the inside: *He came* **into** *the room.* / *She jumped* **into** *the river.* / *Translate it* **into** *English.*
- **On** = position: *The cat is* **on** *the roof.*
- **Onto** = movement to a surface: *The cat jumped* **onto** *the roof.*

::: ex
- ✘ He fell **in** the well (movement). ✔ He fell **into** the well.
- ✘ He is **into** the room. ✔ He is **in** the room.
- ✔ *Divide the cake* **into** *four pieces.* / *Change water* **into** *ice.*
:::

### 17.4.3 *Between* and *Among*

- **Between** — for **two** persons/things (or more when each is considered individually in a relationship): *Divide the mangoes* **between** *the two boys.* *A treaty* **between** *three countries* (each with each). *Between you and* **me** (✘ I).
- **Among / Amongst** — for **more than two** considered as a group: *Divide the mangoes* **among** *the five boys.* *He is popular* **among** *students.*

::: ex
- ✘ Divide the property **among** the two brothers. ✔ … **between** the two brothers.
- ✘ There was a quarrel **between** the members of the club (as a mass). ✔ … **among** the members.
- ✘ **Between** you and **I** ✔ **Between** you and **me**.
- ✔ **Between** 10 and 11 o'clock / **between** Dhaka and Chattogram
:::

### 17.4.4 *Beside* and *Besides*

- **Beside** = by the side of, next to: *He sat* **beside** *me.* / *a house* **beside** *the river.* Also *beside the point* (irrelevant), *beside oneself* (with anger/joy).
- **Besides** = in addition to, apart from: **Besides** *English, he knows French.* / *Who came* **besides** *Rahim?* (As an adverb = moreover.)

### 17.4.5 *Above*, *Over*, *Below*, *Under*, *Beneath*, *Underneath*

| Preposition | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **above** | higher than (not touching, not directly over) | The plane flew **above** the clouds. / He is **above** 60. / Her marks are **above** average. |
| **over** | directly above / covering / across / more than | Hold an umbrella **over** her. / Spread a sheet **over** the bed. / He jumped **over** the wall. / **over** 100 people / The meeting is **over** (finished). |
| **below** | lower than (not necessarily directly under) | The sun sank **below** the horizon. / **below** zero / **below** average |
| **under** | directly beneath / covered by / less than / subject to | The cat is **under** the table. / **under** 18 / **under** construction / **under** the rule of |
| **beneath** | under (formal, literary); unworthy of | **beneath** the sea / It is **beneath** his dignity. |
| **underneath** | directly under (often hidden) | He found the letter **underneath** the carpet. |

### 17.4.6 *Across*, *Through*, *Along*, *Over*

- **Across** = from one side to the other of a surface (a line, a flat area): *He swam* **across** *the river.* *He walked* **across** *the road.*
- **Through** = from one end to the other **inside** something (3-D: tunnel, forest, crowd, window): *The train passed* **through** *a tunnel.* *We walked* **through** *the forest.* *He looked* **through** *the window.*
- **Along** = following the length of: *We walked* **along** *the river bank.*
- **Over** = across, going above: *He jumped* **over** *the fence.*

### 17.4.7 Other Place Prepositions

| Preposition | Example |
|---|---|
| **in front of** / **behind** | The car is **in front of** the house. / The garden is **behind** the house. |
| **opposite** | The bank is **opposite** the post office. (✘ opposite to — in BrE *opposite* takes no *to* as a preposition) |
| **near / next to / by** | He lives **near** the school. / Sit **next to** me. / a house **by** the lake |
| **against** | He leaned **against** the wall. |
| **around / round** | They sat **around** the fire. / The earth moves **round** the sun. |
| **towards** | He walked **towards** the door. |
| **up / down** | He ran **up** the stairs / **down** the hill. |
| **out of / off** | He came **out of** the room. / He fell **off** the horse. (✘ off of) |
| **from** | He came **from** Sylhet. |
| **to** | He went **to** Dhaka. (movement) |
| **within / without** | **within** the city / **without** money |
| **beyond** | **beyond** the river / **beyond** my understanding / **beyond** doubt |
| **amid / amidst** | **amid** the noise |

## 17.5 Prepositions of Agent, Instrument and Manner: *By* and *With*

- **By** = the **doer** (agent) in the passive; means of transport; method: *The letter was written* **by** *Rahim.* / *travel* **by** *bus, by train, by air* / *pay* **by** *cheque* / *learn* **by** *heart* / *by mistake, by chance, by post*.
- **With** = the **instrument** used: *He killed the snake* **with** *a stick.* / *Write* **with** *a pen.* / *cut* **with** *a knife*.

::: ex
- ✘ The snake was killed **by** a stick. ✔ The snake was killed **with** a stick (by Rahim).
- ✘ He wrote it **by** pencil. ✔ He wrote it **in** pencil / **with** a pencil.
- ✔ He came **on** foot (✘ by foot — common but non-standard in exam keys).
- ✔ **By** bus — but ✔ **in** my car / **on** the 9 o'clock bus (with determiner).
:::

## 17.6 Prepositions of Cause, Reason and Purpose

| Preposition | Example |
|---|---|
| **of** (cause of death) | He died **of** cholera / cancer / hunger / grief. |
| **from** (indirect cause) | He died **from** a wound / overwork / an accident (injury). |
| **for** (purpose, reason) | He was punished **for** stealing. / He is famous **for** his honesty. / Thank you **for** coming. |
| **because of / owing to / due to / on account of** | The match was cancelled **because of** rain. |
| **through** (agency, cause) | He got the job **through** his uncle. / He failed **through** laziness. |
| **with** (cause of feeling) | He is trembling **with** fear / shivering **with** cold. |
| **out of** (motive) | He did it **out of** curiosity / pity / jealousy. |

::: note
**Due to vs owing to.** Traditionally, *due to* is an adjective (= caused by) and must follow a noun or *be*: *His absence was* **due to** *illness.* *Owing to / because of* is used adverbially at the start: **Owing to** *illness, he was absent.* (✘ *Due to illness, he was absent* — common but marked wrong by strict keys.)
:::

## 17.7 Some Frequently Confused Prepositions

| Pair | Distinction |
|---|---|
| **in / within** | *in* a week (at the end of); *within* a week (before the end of) |
| **of / off** | *of* = belonging/part; *off* = away from: *He fell* **off** *the tree.* |
| **with / by** | instrument / agent |
| **die of / die from** | disease / external cause |
| **suffer from** (✘ suffer with/by) | *He is suffering* **from** *fever.* |
| **angry with / angry at** | *with* a person; *at* a thing/action: *angry* **with** *him,* **at** *his behaviour* |
| **agree with / agree to / agree on** | *with* a person/opinion; *to* a proposal; *on* a point/decision |
| **compare with / compare to** | *with* (examine differences/similarities); *to* (show likeness, metaphor) |
| **differ from / differ with** | *differ* **from** (be unlike); *differ* **with** a person (disagree) |
| **part from / part with** | *from* a person; *with* a thing |
| **live on / live in / live at** | *on* food/income; *in* a city; *at* an address |
| **wait for / wait on** | *for* someone (await); *on* someone (serve) |
| **look at / look for / look after / look into / look up to** | see / search / take care / investigate / respect |
| **sorry for / sorry about** | *for* a person; *about* a thing |
| **disappointed with / in / at** | *with* a person or thing; *at* a result |
| **in the end / at the end** | finally / at the final point (*at the end of the road*) |
| **in the way / on the way** | obstructing / during a journey |

## 17.8 Position of Prepositions

The preposition usually comes **before** its object. But it may come at the **end** of a clause in these cases:

1. **Wh-questions** (informal): *Who are you looking* **for**? (formal: *For whom are you looking?*)
2. **Relative clauses** with *that* or omitted relative: *This is the house (that) I live* **in**. (✘ *the house in that I live*)
3. **Passive**: *He was laughed* **at**. *The child was looked* **after**.
4. **Infinitive phrases**: *He has no house to live* **in**. *Give me a pen to write* **with**.
5. **Exclamations / emphasis**: *What a mess you are* **in**!

::: note
The old rule "never end a sentence with a preposition" is not followed in modern English; *What are you talking about?* is perfectly natural. But in the passive and with *in which / to whom*, choose one position only: ✘ *the house in which he lives in* ✔ *the house in which he lives / the house which he lives in*.
:::

## 17.9 Words That Can Be Prepositions, Adverbs or Conjunctions

| Word | Preposition | Adverb | Conjunction |
|---|---|---|---|
| after | He came **after** me. | He came soon **after**. | He came **after** I had left. |
| before | Come **before** 10. | I have seen him **before**. | Think **before** you speak. |
| since | **since** Monday | I haven't seen him **since**. | **Since** you are ill, rest. |
| till/until | Wait **till** five. | — | Wait **till** I come. |
| for | This is **for** you. | — | He felt sad, **for** he had failed. |
| but | All **but** him came. | He is **but** a child. | He is poor **but** honest. |
| up / down / in / out / on / off / over | He went **up** the stairs. | He looked **up**. | — |

## 17.10 Unnecessary and Missing Prepositions

### 17.10.1 Verbs That Take No Preposition (Do Not Add One)

*discuss, describe, reach, resemble, order, attack, enter (a place), approach, accompany, marry, request, obey, answer, comprise, consider, emphasise, investigate, mention, lack, demand, recommend, inform (+ person), enjoy, ask (a person), await, return (= come back), stress, combat.*

::: ex
- ✘ We discussed **about** the problem. ✔ We discussed the problem.
- ✘ He reached **at** the station. ✔ He reached the station.
- ✘ He resembles **to** his father. ✔ He resembles his father.
- ✘ He ordered **for** a coffee. ✔ He ordered a coffee.
- ✘ The enemy attacked **on** the city. ✔ The enemy attacked the city.
- ✘ He entered **into** the room. ✔ He entered the room.
- ✘ She married **with** a doctor. ✔ She married a doctor. / She **got married to** a doctor.
- ✘ He requested **for** help. ✔ He requested help. / He made a request **for** help.
- ✘ He lacks **in** confidence. ✔ He lacks confidence. / He is lacking **in** confidence.
- ✘ The book comprises **of** ten chapters. ✔ The book comprises ten chapters. / The book **consists of** / **is composed of** ten chapters.
- ✘ He emphasised **on** honesty. ✔ He emphasised honesty. / He laid emphasis **on** honesty.
- ✘ They **awaited for** the result. ✔ They awaited the result. / They **waited for** the result.
:::

### 17.10.2 Prepositions Often Missed

::: ex
- ✘ He listened the radio. ✔ He listened **to** the radio.
- ✘ He explained me the problem. ✔ He explained the problem **to** me.
- ✘ Please reply my letter. ✔ Please reply **to** my letter.
- ✘ He is not capable this work. ✔ He is not capable **of** this work.
- ✘ I am waiting you. ✔ I am waiting **for** you.
- ✘ He complained the noise. ✔ He complained **about / of** the noise.
- ✘ Let us dispense this formality. ✔ Let us dispense **with** this formality.
- ✘ He is senior me. ✔ He is senior **to** me.
- ✘ Search the lost book. (meaning *look for*) ✔ Search **for** the lost book. (*search the room* = examine the room)
:::

## 17.11 Common Errors with Prepositions

::: errs
1. ✘ He was born **in** 26 March. ✔ He was born **on** 26 March.
2. ✘ I will meet you **on** the evening. ✔ I will meet you **in** the evening.
3. ✘ He has been ill **from** Monday. ✔ He has been ill **since** Monday.
4. ✘ He has been living here **since** five years. ✔ … **for** five years.
5. ✘ The train arrived **in** time (= punctually). ✔ … **on** time.
6. ✘ Divide the apple **among** the two boys. ✔ … **between** the two boys.
7. ✘ **Beside** Bangla, he knows English. ✔ **Besides** Bangla, he knows English.
8. ✘ He jumped **in** the river (movement). ✔ He jumped **into** the river.
9. ✘ He died **from** cholera. ✔ He died **of** cholera.
10. ✘ He is suffering **with** fever. ✔ He is suffering **from** fever.
11. ✘ He is angry **on** me. ✔ He is angry **with** me.
12. ✘ He killed the bird **by** a stone. ✔ He killed the bird **with** a stone.
13. ✘ I prefer tea **than** coffee. ✔ I prefer tea **to** coffee.
14. ✘ He is good **in** English. ✔ He is good **at** English.
15. ✘ She is married **with** an engineer. ✔ She is married **to** an engineer.
16. ✘ He arrived **to** Dhaka yesterday. ✔ He arrived **in** Dhaka yesterday.
17. ✘ He is afraid **from** the dog. ✔ He is afraid **of** the dog.
18. ✘ I congratulate you **for** your success. ✔ I congratulate you **on** your success.
19. ✘ He insisted **for** going. ✔ He insisted **on** going.
20. ✘ He is fond **for** music. ✔ He is fond **of** music.
21. ✘ This is different **than** that (BrE exam). ✔ This is different **from** that.
22. ✘ He fell **off of** the horse. ✔ He fell **off** the horse.
23. ✘ Translate this **in** English. ✔ Translate this **into** English.
24. ✘ He walked **on** the road (meaning *along*). ✔ He walked **along** the road.
25. ✘ **Due to** rain, the match was postponed (strict). ✔ **Owing to** rain, the match was postponed.
:::

::: gre
**SAT / GRE / GMAT Corner — Prepositional Idioms:** Both the SAT and GMAT test idiomatic prepositions: *responsible* **for**, *capable* **of**, *consistent* **with**, *independent* **of**, *distinguish X* **from** *Y*, *prohibit X* **from**, *a debate* **over/about**, *an increase* **in**, *a decline* **in**, *the ability* **to**, *credit X* **with**, *depend* **on**, *in contrast* **to/with**, *a preference* **for**. They also test **"between X and Y"** (✘ between X to Y) and **"from X to Y"** (✘ from X and Y). See Chapter 18 for a full list.
:::
